BMI categories (WHO adult)

  • Underweight: BMI under 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25 – 29.9
  • Obesity: 30 or higher

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Consult a healthcare professional for advice.
