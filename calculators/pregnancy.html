How this works

Naegele’s rule estimates due date as LMP + 280 days (40 weeks), with a small adjustment when cycle length differs from 28 days. Ultrasound dating from your clinician is more accurate.

Medical disclaimer: This tool is informational only and is not a substitute for professional prenatal care. Always confirm dates with your doctor or midwife.
