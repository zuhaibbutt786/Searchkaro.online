Popular calculator searches

What is an EMI loan calculator?

An EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) calculator uses loan amount, interest rate, and tenure to estimate your monthly payment and total interest.

How do I calculate exact age?

Enter your date of birth. The age calculator shows years, months, and days lived, and how many days remain until your next birthday.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Our BMI calculator also accepts pounds and inches and shows underweight, normal, overweight, or obese ranges.

How is pregnancy due date estimated?

Using Naegele’s rule: last menstrual period (LMP) + 280 days (40 weeks). This is an estimate — confirm with your doctor.
