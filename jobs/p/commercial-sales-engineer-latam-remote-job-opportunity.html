Commercial Sales Engineer – LATAM (Remote) – Job Opportunity

Chainguard is the trusted source for hardened, production‑ready open‑source builds that power modern software and AI agents. As a Commercial Sales Engineer for the LATAM market, you will partner with global customers to showcase Chainguard's security platform and drive adoption across diverse industries. This remote role offers exposure to cutting‑edge cloud‑native technologies and a collaborative, multicultural team. Candidates from Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions are encouraged to apply.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (MS or PhD a plus).
  • 3+ years of technical sales or solutions engineering experience with SaaS or cloud‑native products.
  • Strong communication skills in English; ability to engage with stakeholders across LATAM, South Asia, and Europe.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary, performance‑based bonuses, and comprehensive health coverage.
  • Flexible remote work policy, professional development budget, and access to Chainguard’s open‑source security tools.

How to apply

  1. Visit the Chainguard careers page and locate the “Commercial Sales Engineer – LATAM” listing.
  2. Upload your updated resume and a brief cover letter highlighting relevant sales engineering experience.
  3. Complete the short online questionnaire and submit; a recruiter will contact you within a few business days.
