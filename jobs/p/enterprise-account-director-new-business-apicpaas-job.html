FAQs

Is this role available for candidates in Pakistan or India?

Yes, the position is marked as Worldwide, making it suitable for remote candidates in Pakistan, India, and other global regions.

What is CPaaS in the context of this job?

CPaaS stands for Communications Platform as a Service. It involves providing APIs for SMS, voice, and video communication, which is the core product Vonage offers.
