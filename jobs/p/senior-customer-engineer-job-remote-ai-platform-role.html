Senior Customer Engineer Job: Remote AI Platform Role

Builder.io is hiring a Senior Customer Engineer to support global teams using their collaborative AI development platform. Eligibility

  • 5+ years of experience in customer engineering, solutions engineering, or software development.
  • Strong proficiency in JavaScript, TypeScript, and modern web frameworks.
  • Proven ability to communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

Benefits

  • Fully remote work culture with flexible hours across time zones.
  • Opportunity to work with cutting-edge AI agents and collaborative tools.

How to apply

  1. Prepare a resume highlighting customer-facing technical roles.
  2. Submit your application through the official Builder.io careers portal.
  3. Complete the initial technical screening and behavioral interview.
