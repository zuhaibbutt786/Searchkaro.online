Staff Technical Product Manager – Ads ML Platform (MS/PhD Preferred)

Reddit is seeking a Staff Technical Product Manager to drive the Ads Machine Learning Platform. You will shape product strategy, guide engineering execution, and deliver AI-powered advertising experiences for a global audience. The role offers the chance to work with cutting‑edge ML technologies and influence the future of online community monetization. Candidates from Pakistan, India, Europe and beyond are encouraged to apply.

Eligibility

  • MS or PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or a related discipline
  • 10+ years of product management experience in ad tech, ML platforms, or large‑scale systems
  • Proven track record of leading cross‑functional engineering and data science teams
  • Strong analytical, strategic thinking, and communication skills

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with equity participation
  • Flexible remote‑work policy and global office access
  • Comprehensive health insurance covering Pakistan, India, and Europe
  • Generous vacation and paid parental leave
  • Learning & development budget for courses, conferences, and certifications

How to apply

  1. Visit the Reddit Careers portal and locate the "Staff Technical Product Manager, Ads ML Platform" posting
  2. Upload your updated resume and a tailored cover letter highlighting relevant experience
  3. Complete the online assessment and schedule interview rounds with product and engineering leaders

Yes. Reddit supports fully remote work for this role, with occasional in‑person meetings at regional hubs.

A PhD is not mandatory, but an advanced degree (MS or PhD) in a technical field is highly preferred.
