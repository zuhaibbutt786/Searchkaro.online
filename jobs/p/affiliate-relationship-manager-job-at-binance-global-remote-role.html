Affiliate Relationship Manager Job at Binance: Global Remote Role

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, is hiring an Affiliate Relationship Manager to drive growth across its global ecosystem. You will leverage your expertise in relationship management to expand Binance's reach in diverse markets. Join a team trusted by over 300 million users worldwide.

Eligibility

  • Proven experience in affiliate marketing, partnership management, or business development.
  • Strong understanding of the cryptocurrency industry and blockchain technology.
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to work effectively in a remote, global environment.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and performance-based incentives.
  • Fully remote work flexibility with access to global professional development resources.

How to apply

  1. Visit the official Binance careers page and search for 'Affiliate Relationship Manager'.
  2. Submit your updated resume and a cover letter highlighting relevant crypto experience.
  3. Complete the online application form and prepare for technical and behavioral interviews.
