Lead Data Platform Engineer Job: Remote & Global

Beyond Finance seeks a Lead Data Platform Engineer to build robust data infrastructure supporting its mission to help Americans escape debt. This role involves designing scalable systems and leading technical initiatives. The position is open to candidates worldwide, including those in Pakistan and India. You will drive innovation in data engineering to enable compassionate, individualized financial care.

Eligibility

  • Proven experience as a Data Platform or Data Engineer
  • Strong expertise in cloud data warehouses and ETL pipelines
  • Experience leading technical teams or defining platform architecture

Benefits

  • Fully remote work with flexible hours
  • Opportunity to work on impactful financial technology projects

How to apply

  1. Prepare a portfolio showcasing data platform architecture projects
  2. Submit your resume and cover letter via the company career portal
  3. Complete technical assessments and interview with the engineering team

FAQs

Is the Lead Data Platform Engineer role remote?

Yes, the role is open to candidates worldwide, including Pakistan, India, and Europe, with remote work arrangements.

What is Beyond Finance's primary mission?

Beyond Finance aims to help everyday Americans escape the cycle of crippling debt through compassionate, individualized financial care.
