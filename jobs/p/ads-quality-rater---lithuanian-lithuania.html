Ads Quality Rater - Lithuanian (Lithuania)

Join Welo Data, a globally recognized localization and data-driven company, as an Ads Quality Rater with Lithuanian language skills. Our award-winning Ads Rating Program requires an MS or PhD or relevant experience to join our team and ensure ad quality worldwide, including for Pakistan and India.

  • Experience in localization or data analysis preferred
  • Ability to work independently and meet deadlines
  • Passion for quality assurance and ad optimization

Benefits

  • Remote work option
  • Global team collaboration
  • Continuous training and development
  • Flexible working hours

  1. Step 1: Review job requirements and ensure you meet the eligibility criteria.

FAQs

What is the job role of an Ads Quality Rater?

As an Ads Quality Rater, you will ensure the quality of ads across various platforms by assessing their compliance with guidelines, providing feedback, and suggesting improvements. Your role is crucial in ensuring ad quality for Pakistan and India, as well as other global markets.

What qualifications should I have to apply for this role?

To apply for the Ads Quality Rater role, you should meet the following eligibility criteria: MS or PhD or relevant experience, Fluent in Lithuanian, Experience in localization or data analysis preferred, Ability to work independently and meet deadlines, Passion for quality assurance and ad optimization.

What is the working arrangement for this role?

As a Welo Data employee, the working arrangement for the Ads Quality Rater role can be flexible, allowing you to work remotely. However, please note that we may require you to work on-site at our Lithuanian office at times for training or team collaboration.
