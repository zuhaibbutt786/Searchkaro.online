Dental Lab Technician – Customer Experience (UK)

Dandy is revolutionising the $400B dental industry with cutting‑edge technology and a global vision. As a Dental Lab Technician, you’ll deliver exceptional customer experience, support our lab operations, and help drive innovation. The role is based in the UK but offers collaboration with teams across Europe, Pakistan, and India. If you thrive in a fast‑paced, tech‑driven environment, this is your chance to make a real impact.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s degree in Dental Technology, Prosthodontics, or a related field.
  • Minimum 2 years of lab technician experience, preferably in a dental lab setting.
  • Strong communication skills and a customer‑centric mindset.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and performance‑based bonuses.
  • Health and wellness benefits, including medical and dental coverage.

How to apply

  1. Submit your CV and a cover letter through our online portal.
  2. Complete a short skills assessment to showcase your technical expertise.
  3. Attend a virtual interview with the hiring manager and team leads.

FAQs

Can candidates from Pakistan or India apply for this UK role?

Yes, we welcome international applicants. Successful candidates will receive support for relocation and work visa sponsorship.

What is the work environment like at Dandy?

Dandy offers a collaborative, inclusive culture with a focus on continuous learning and innovation across all regions.
