Technical Support Engineer Job in Canada | Customer.io Remote

Customer.io is hiring a Technical Support Engineer to assist over 9,000 global companies with their messaging platform. This remote position supports startups and enterprise brands sending billions of notifications daily. The role is open to qualified candidates worldwide, including those based in Pakistan, India, and Europe. You will help users optimize email, push, and SMS campaigns through technical troubleshooting.

Eligibility

  • Proven experience in technical support or customer success roles within SaaS or B2B environments.
  • Strong understanding of web technologies, APIs, and debugging tools to resolve complex user issues.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills for remote collaboration across time zones.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and performance-based bonuses aligned with global standards.
  • Comprehensive health insurance, wellness stipends, and flexible remote work arrangements.

How to apply

  1. Visit the official Customer.io careers page to locate the Technical Support Engineer listing.
  2. Submit your resume and a tailored cover letter highlighting your technical support experience.
  3. Complete the online application form and prepare for a technical assessment and interview.

FAQs

Is this Technical Support Engineer job open to candidates in Pakistan and India?

Yes, Customer.io hires globally. Candidates from Pakistan, India, and Europe are encouraged to apply if they meet the technical requirements.

Does Customer.io offer remote work for this position?

Yes, this is a remote-first role. You can work from your location in Canada, Pakistan, India, or elsewhere, provided you have a stable internet connection.
