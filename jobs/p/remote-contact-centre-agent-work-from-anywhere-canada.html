Remote Contact Centre Agent – Work from Anywhere (Canada)

Procom is hiring independent contractor Contact Centre Agents to provide customer support for Canadian businesses. This fully remote role lets you work from Pakistan, India, or any location with a stable internet connection. You’ll handle inbound and outbound calls, resolve queries, and maintain high service standards. Competitive rates and ongoing training are offered to help you succeed.

Eligibility

  • Fluent English communication skills (both spoken and written)
  • Reliable high‑speed internet connection and a quiet workspace
  • Prior experience in customer service or call centre environments

Benefits

  • Flexible remote schedule that fits your time zone
  • Competitive contractor rates with performance incentives

How to apply

  1. Visit the Procom careers portal and locate the posting with reference code 132741.
  2. Complete the online application form and upload your updated resume.
  3. Submit the form; a recruiter will review your profile and contact you for the next steps.

FAQs

Do I need to be based in Canada to apply?

No. This role is fully remote, and candidates from Pakistan, India, Europe, or any country with reliable internet can apply.

What equipment is required for the job?

A computer with a headset, a stable broadband connection (minimum 5 Mbps), and a quiet environment for calls.
