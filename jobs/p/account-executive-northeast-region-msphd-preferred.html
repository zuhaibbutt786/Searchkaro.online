Account Executive – Northeast Region (MS/PhD preferred)

Join our mission to revolutionize the U.S. healthcare system as an Account Executive in the Northeast Region. Preferred qualifications include a Master's (MS) or Doctoral (PhD) degree and a passion for employer partnerships. Our proven track record and global impact make Garner a unique and rewarding career choice for professionals in Pakistan, India, and Europe.

Eligibility

  • MS/PhD preferred
  • Experience in healthcare or related field
  • Excellent communication and relationship-building skills
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment
  • Fluency in English (both written and spoken)
  • Preferably knowledge of U.S. healthcare landscape

Benefits

  • Competitive salary
  • Healthcare coverage
  • Pension plan
  • Annual leave and public holidays
  • Training and development opportunities

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Review job requirements and ensure you meet the eligibility criteria.
  2. Step 2: Apply through our website and include your resume and cover letter.
  3. Step 3: Prepare for a series of interviews with our team to showcase your skills and expertise.
