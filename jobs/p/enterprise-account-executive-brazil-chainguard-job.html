Enterprise Account Executive – Brazil | Chainguard Job

Chainguard is hiring an Enterprise Account Executive to drive growth in Brazil. You will sell hardened, production-ready open source builds to enterprise clients. Eligibility

  • Proven track record in enterprise software sales, preferably in security or DevOps.
  • Strong network within the Brazilian tech and enterprise landscape.
  • Experience selling to C-level executives and technical decision-makers.

Benefits

  • Competitive compensation package with performance-based incentives.
  • Opportunity to work with a leading provider of secure open source infrastructure.

How to apply

  1. Visit the official Chainguard careers page to locate the specific job listing.
  2. Prepare a tailored resume highlighting enterprise sales achievements in Brazil.
  3. Submit your application directly through the company's recruitment portal.
