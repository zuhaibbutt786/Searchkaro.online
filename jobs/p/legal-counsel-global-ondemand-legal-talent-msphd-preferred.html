Legal Counsel – Global On‑Demand Legal Talent (MS/PhD Preferred)

Benefits

  1. Submit your updated resume and cover letter through the Axiom career portal

Assignments range from corporate governance, contract drafting, compliance, to dispute resolution for multinational clients.
