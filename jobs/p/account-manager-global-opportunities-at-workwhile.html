Account Manager – Global Opportunities at WorkWhile

WorkWhile is on a mission to empower workers worldwide with better earning opportunities. As an Account Manager, you will nurture client relationships, oversee project delivery, and help grow our global portfolio. This fully remote role welcomes candidates from Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions. You’ll enjoy a supportive culture focused on impact and professional growth.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s degree or higher (MS/PhD a plus but not required)
  • 2+ years of experience in account management, sales, or client services
  • Excellent communication skills in English and strong analytical abilities

Benefits

  • Fully remote work with flexible hours to fit any timezone
  • Performance‑based bonuses, professional development resources, and health benefits

How to apply

  1. Submit your updated resume and cover letter through the WorkWhile careers portal
  2. Complete the short online assessment to showcase your skills
  3. Participate in a virtual interview with the hiring team

FAQs

Can candidates from Pakistan and India work remotely for this role?

Yes, the position is fully remote and open to qualified applicants in Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions.

What time zones are expected for collaboration?

We operate across multiple time zones; you’ll coordinate with teams during overlapping hours that suit both your location and our global clients.
