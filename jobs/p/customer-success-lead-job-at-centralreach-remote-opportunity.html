Customer Success Lead Job at CentralReach: Remote Opportunity

CentralReach is seeking a Customer Success Lead to support over 200,000 users of its autism and IDD care software. This remote role focuses on optimizing the experience for Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) and special education professionals. You will drive adoption and satisfaction for a leading healthcare technology provider.

Eligibility

  • Proven experience in customer success or account management within SaaS or healthcare tech.
  • Strong understanding of ABA, multidisciplinary therapy, or special education workflows.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills for a global, remote-first team.

Benefits

  • Fully remote work arrangement suitable for candidates in Pakistan, India, and Europe.
  • Competitive compensation package and professional development opportunities.

How to apply

  1. Prepare a resume highlighting relevant SaaS and healthcare customer success experience.
  2. Submit your application through the official CentralReach careers portal.
  3. Complete the initial screening and interview process with the hiring team.

FAQs

Is this Customer Success Lead position open to candidates in Pakistan and India?

Yes, the role is remote and open to qualified candidates worldwide, including those in Pakistan, India, and Europe.

CentralReach provides software for Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA), multidisciplinary therapy, and special education, trusted by over 200,000 users.
