Principal DevOps Engineer – Global Role (MS/PhD Preferred)

NBCUniversal, a leader in media and entertainment, is seeking a Principal DevOps Engineer to drive cloud-native solutions across its global streaming platforms. This senior role offers the chance to shape infrastructure strategy, mentor engineering teams, and work with cutting‑edge technologies. Candidates from Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions are encouraged to apply. Enjoy a collaborative, innovative environment that values diversity and continuous learning.

Eligibility

  • MS or PhD in Computer Science, Engineering or related field (preferred)
  • 8+ years of hands‑on DevOps experience with large‑scale cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
  • Proven expertise in Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, IaC (Terraform, CloudFormation) and automation scripting

Benefits

  • Competitive global compensation package with performance bonuses
  • Comprehensive health, dental and vision coverage, plus wellness programs
  • Remote‑work flexibility and generous paid time‑off
  • Professional development budget and access to NBCUniversal learning resources

How to apply

  1. Visit the NBCUniversal Careers portal and locate the Principal DevOps Engineer posting
  2. Upload your updated resume and a brief cover letter highlighting relevant experience
  3. Complete the online assessment and schedule the virtual interview with the hiring team

Yes, NBCUniversal welcomes applicants from Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions; the position supports remote work and relocation where applicable.

NBCUniversal assists qualified candidates with visa sponsorship and work‑permit processes for eligible locations.
