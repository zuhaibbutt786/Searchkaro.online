Senior Sales Engineer, Enterprise (German) | Fivetran Remote Job

Fivetran seeks a Senior Sales Engineer to support enterprise customers with data integration solutions. This remote role requires strong German language skills and technical sales experience. You will help simplify data access for clients globally, ensuring reliable warehouse connectivity. Ideal for professionals in Pakistan, India, or Europe seeking impactful tech careers.

Eligibility

  • Fluent German (native or C1 level) and professional English proficiency
  • 3+ years of experience in technical sales, pre-sales, or solutions engineering
  • Strong understanding of data warehouses, ETL/ELT, and cloud infrastructure

Benefits

  • Fully remote work with flexible hours across time zones
  • Competitive salary and equity package for high-performing engineers

How to apply

  1. Prepare your CV highlighting German language skills and sales engineering experience
  2. Submit your application via the official Fivetran careers page
  3. Complete the technical and language assessment if shortlisted

FAQs

Is this job open to candidates in Pakistan and India?

Yes, Fivetran hires remotely worldwide. Candidates from Pakistan and India are eligible if they meet the German language and technical requirements.

What is the primary responsibility of this role?

You will act as a technical bridge between sales and customers, demonstrating Fivetran’s data integration capabilities to enterprise clients in German-speaking regions.
