Platform Engineer (MS/PhD preferred)

Join the extraordinary team at Catawiki, where you'll build and maintain our platforms as a Platform Engineer. MS or PhD preferred, candidates from Pakistan, India, and Europe are encouraged to apply.

Eligibility

  • MS or PhD in Computer Science or a related field
  • Experience with software development methodologies and best practices
  • Experience with cloud computing platforms (AWS, Azure, etc.)
  • Experience with containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
  • Experience with test-driven development and continuous integration/deployment
  • Fluency in English (verbal and written)

Benefits

  • Competitive salary
  • Health insurance
  • Annual bonus
  • Vacation days
  • Career development opportunities

How to apply

  1. 1. Review the job description and ensure you meet the eligibility criteria.
  2. 2. Apply via our website with your CV and cover letter.
  3. 3. If shortlisted, we'll invite you for an interview and possibly a second interview.
