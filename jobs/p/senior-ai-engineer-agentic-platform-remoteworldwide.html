Senior AI Engineer: Agentic Platform (Remote/Worldwide)

We are seeking a product-minded Senior AI Engineer to transform customer decision-making into reliable autonomous agents. You will enhance AskWaive, our live in-product assistant, by designing robust agentic workflows. This remote role is open to candidates worldwide, including Pakistan and India, focusing on high-impact AI solutions.

Eligibility

  • 5+ years of experience in software engineering with strong Python or TypeScript skills.
  • Proven expertise in building LLM applications, RAG systems, or agentic workflows.
  • Strong product sense to translate user needs into technical agent architectures.

Benefits

  • Fully remote position with flexible hours for global candidates.
  • Opportunity to work on cutting-edge agentic AI technology in production.

How to apply

  1. Prepare a portfolio or GitHub link showcasing previous AI or LLM projects.
  2. Submit your resume and a brief cover letter highlighting your agentic experience.
  3. Complete the application form on the company's careers page.

FAQs

Is this job available for candidates in Pakistan and India?

Yes, the role is open worldwide, including candidates based in Pakistan and India.

What does an Agentic Platform engineer do?

They design and build AI systems that can plan, use tools, and execute multi-step tasks autonomously to solve complex user problems.
