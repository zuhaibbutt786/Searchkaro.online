Senior C++/iOS Engineer – User Platform

Spotify’s User Platform (UP) seeks a Senior C++/iOS Engineer to join the Baldur squad, responsible for Session Management and Delegated Authorization. You’ll design high‑performance, secure systems that power Spotify’s core user experience worldwide. The role offers remote flexibility, cross‑functional collaboration, and the chance to shape the future of music streaming.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science or related field, or equivalent experience
  • 5+ years of professional software development in C++ and iOS (Swift/Objective‑C)
  • Strong understanding of distributed systems, authentication, and session management

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and equity package
  • Flexible remote work with global team collaboration
  • Access to Spotify’s premium services and continuous learning resources

How to apply

  1. Submit your resume and a brief cover letter via the Spotify Careers portal
  2. Prepare a portfolio of relevant C++/iOS projects or contributions
  3. Attend a technical interview with senior engineers and a cultural fit assessment

FAQs

Is this a fully remote position?

Yes, the role is fully remote and open to candidates worldwide, including Pakistan, India, and Europe.

What is the expected travel requirement?

Minimal travel is required, primarily for occasional in‑person meetings or conferences.
