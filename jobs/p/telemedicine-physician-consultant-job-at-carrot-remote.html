Telemedicine Physician Consultant Job at Carrot | Remote

Carrot is hiring a Telemedicine Physician Consultant to provide expert clinical guidance within its intelligent care orchestration platform. This remote role focuses on delivering the right clinical support at the right moment for global fertility and family care users. The position is open to qualified medical professionals worldwide, including candidates from Pakistan and India. You will help ensure high-quality, context-aware medical advice for patients across different regions.

Eligibility

  • Valid medical license to practice in your home country
  • Specialization or significant experience in fertility, reproductive health, or family medicine
  • Experience with telemedicine platforms and remote patient consultation
  • Strong clinical judgment and ability to navigate complex care contexts

Benefits

  • Fully remote work opportunity with flexible scheduling
  • Competitive compensation and professional development opportunities
  • Access to cutting-edge intelligent care orchestration technology
  • Work with a global team impacting fertility and family care worldwide

How to apply

  1. Prepare your CV highlighting relevant clinical and telemedicine experience
  2. Submit your application through the official Carrot careers portal
  3. Complete the initial screening and technical assessment if shortlisted

FAQs

Is this Telemedicine Physician Consultant job fully remote?

Yes, the role is designed for remote work, allowing physicians to provide consultation from anywhere in the world, including Pakistan and India.

Do I need a specific medical degree like MS or PhD to apply?

A valid medical license (MBBS/MD) is required. Specialized degrees in fertility or reproductive health are highly preferred but not strictly mandatory if you have relevant experience.

Can doctors from Pakistan and India apply for this global role?

Yes, Carrot hires globally. Candidates from Pakistan, India, and other regions are encouraged to apply if they hold valid local medical licenses.
