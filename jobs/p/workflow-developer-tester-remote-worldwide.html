Workflow Developer & Tester – Remote (Worldwide)

We are seeking a skilled Workflow Developer & Tester to design and implement Temporal workflow activities using Go. You will build and maintain an inventory management database, write comprehensive unit tests, and ensure high‑quality code from day one. This remote role offers the chance to collaborate with a worldwide team on cutting‑edge cloud technologies. Candidates from Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions are encouraged to apply.

Eligibility

  • Proficiency in Go programming language with hands‑on experience.
  • Familiarity with Temporal workflow engine and activity implementation.
  • Strong understanding of relational database design, indexing and partitioning.
  • Experience writing unit and integration tests using Go testing frameworks.
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field (MS/PhD a plus).

Benefits

  • Competitive salary aligned with global market rates.
  • Fully remote work with flexible hours.
  • Access to modern cloud infrastructure and development tools.
  • Continuous learning budget and mentorship opportunities.
  • Health, wellness, and employee assistance programs.

How to apply

  1. Submit your resume and a brief cover letter through our online career portal.
  2. Complete the short technical questionnaire that assesses Go and Temporal knowledge.
  3. Participate in a virtual interview with the hiring manager and senior engineers.

FAQs

Do I need to be located in a specific country to apply?

No. This is a fully remote position open to candidates worldwide, with a focus on talent from Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions.

What is the expected experience level for this role?

We look for candidates with at least 2–3 years of professional Go development experience and familiarity with workflow orchestration tools such as Temporal.
