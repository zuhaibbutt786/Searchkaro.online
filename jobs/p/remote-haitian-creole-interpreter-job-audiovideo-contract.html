Remote Haitian Creole Interpreter Job: Audio/Video Contract

Language Services of America (LSA) is seeking independent contractors to serve as audio and video interpreters for Haitian Creole. This remote position allows you to facilitate critical language communication by handling on-demand calls from anywhere in the world. It is an ideal opportunity for bilingual professionals in Pakistan, India, and Europe seeking flexible freelance work.

Eligibility

  • Native or near-native proficiency in Haitian Creole and English
  • Reliable high-speed internet connection and quiet workspace
  • Ability to work independently as a contract interpreter

Benefits

  • Fully remote work with flexible scheduling
  • Opportunity to work with a global network of clients

How to apply

  1. Visit the LSA careers page to locate the Haitian Creole interpreter listing
  2. Submit your resume and a brief cover letter highlighting your language skills
  3. Complete any required proficiency assessments or interviews

FAQs

Is this job available in Pakistan and India?

Yes, this is a remote role open to candidates worldwide, including Pakistan, India, and Europe, provided you meet the technical and language requirements.

Do I need a specific degree to apply?

While formal education is not always mandatory, you must demonstrate native-level proficiency in Haitian Creole and strong English communication skills.
