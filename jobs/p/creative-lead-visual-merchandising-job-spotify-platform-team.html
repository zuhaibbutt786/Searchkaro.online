Creative Lead – Visual Merchandising Job | Spotify Platform Team

Spotify's Platform team seeks a Creative Lead in Visual Merchandising to drive visual strategy for rapid global growth. This role spans multiple disciplines to enable the technology that powers Spotify's user experience. Ideal for creative professionals seeking impact in a remote, international environment.

Eligibility

  • Proven experience in visual merchandising, brand design, or creative direction.
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating large-scale visual strategy and execution.
  • Ability to work remotely and collaborate across global time zones.

Benefits

  • Remote-first work culture with flexibility for candidates in Pakistan, India, and Europe.
  • Opportunity to influence global brand aesthetics for a leading tech platform.

How to apply

  1. Prepare a portfolio showcasing relevant visual merchandising projects.
  2. Submit your application through the official Spotify careers portal.
  3. Complete the initial screening and interview process with the Platform team.

FAQs

Is this position available to candidates in Pakistan and India?

Yes, the role is open to qualified candidates worldwide, including those based in Pakistan, India, and Europe, provided they meet the remote work requirements.

What does the Platform team do at Spotify?

The Platform team creates the technology that enables Spotify to learn quickly and scale easily, supporting rapid growth in users and business globally.
