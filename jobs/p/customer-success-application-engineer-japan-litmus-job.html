Customer Success Application Engineer (Japan) | Litmus Job

Litmus is building the data foundation that powers industrial AI by making real-world, contextualized data usable. As a Customer Success Application Engineer, you will bridge the gap between complex AI technology and client needs in the Japan market. This role focuses on ensuring successful implementation and adoption of Litmus’s data solutions. It is an excellent opportunity for technical professionals to work at the intersection of AI and industrial applications.

Eligibility

  • Strong background in software engineering, data science, or a related technical field.
  • Experience in customer success, technical support, or application engineering roles.
  • Ability to communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders effectively.

Benefits

  • Work with cutting-edge industrial AI and data foundation technologies.
  • Opportunity to contribute to a global mission accelerating AI adoption.

How to apply

  1. Prepare your resume highlighting technical and customer-facing experience.
  2. Submit your application via the official Litmus careers portal.
  3. Prepare for technical and behavioral interviews focusing on problem-solving.
