Clinical Implementation Manager – MS/PhD Preferred

CentralReach, a trusted provider of autism and IDD care software, is seeking a Clinical Implementation Manager to drive worldwide implementations of our ABA and multidisciplinary therapy platforms. You will partner with clinics, schools, and therapy centers to ensure seamless integration and optimal use of our technology. This role offers the chance to impact thousands of individuals with autism and IDD while advancing your career in a fast‑growing health‑tech company.

Eligibility

  • Master's or PhD in psychology, special education, health administration, or a related field
  • 3+ years of experience implementing clinical or therapy software, preferably in ABA or multidisciplinary settings
  • Strong understanding of autism, IDD care practices, and ability to train diverse stakeholder groups

Benefits

  • Make a meaningful impact on autism and IDD care across Pakistan, India, Europe and beyond
  • Competitive salary, flexible remote‑work options, and professional development support

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Review the full job description and confirm you meet the eligibility criteria
  2. Step 2: Submit your updated CV and a cover letter highlighting relevant implementation experience
  3. Step 3: Complete the online assessment and attend virtual interviews with the hiring team

FAQs

Do I need to be based in Pakistan or India to apply?

No. The role is worldwide and can be performed remotely, but candidates from Pakistan, India, and Europe are especially encouraged to apply.

What type of training will I receive?

CentralReach provides comprehensive onboarding, product certification, and ongoing mentorship to ensure you succeed in client implementations.
