Psychiatrist Collaborative Physician (MD) - Join the Revolution in Mental Healthcare

UpLift and BetterHelp are redefining access to mental healthcare. Join our team as a Psychiatrist Collaborative Physician (MD) and help provide quality mental health support to individuals in Pakistan, India, and globally.   • Experience in collaborative care models is a plus.

  • Continuing education and professional development support.
