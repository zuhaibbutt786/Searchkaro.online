Sales Director – Japan & Asia | Litmus

Litmus is building the data foundation that powers industrial AI and is looking for a Sales Director to lead its Japan and Asia market expansion. You will design and execute sales strategies, build high‑performing teams, and partner with global customers to accelerate AI adoption. The role offers exposure to cutting‑edge technology and the chance to shape a fast‑growing business. Candidates from Pakistan, India, Europe and worldwide are encouraged to apply.

Eligibility

  • Proven sales leadership experience in AI, technology or industrial sectors
  • Deep understanding of industrial AI applications and market dynamics in Asia
  • Track record of building and managing high‑performing sales teams

Benefits

  • Competitive salary, performance bonuses and comprehensive benefits
  • Opportunity to work with cutting‑edge AI technology and a global leadership team

How to apply

  1. Submit your updated resume and a tailored cover letter through the Litmus careers portal
  2. Complete a virtual interview with the sales and leadership teams
  3. Receive an offer and discuss onboarding details

FAQs

What experience is required for this Sales Director role?

We require at least 8‑10 years of sales leadership in AI, SaaS, or industrial technology, with a proven record of revenue growth in Asian markets.

Can candidates from Pakistan or India apply?

Yes, the position is open to qualified candidates worldwide, including Pakistan, India, and Europe. Remote work arrangements are considered.
