Senior Marketing Manager – Global Sports Tech Role

TeamSnap is a leading sports and communication platform that connects teams, coaches, and fans worldwide. As a Senior Marketing Manager, you will shape global growth strategies, drive user acquisition, and amplify brand presence across digital channels. This fully remote position offers the flexibility to work from Pakistan, India, Europe, or any location with reliable internet. Join a passionate team dedicated to making sports more accessible for everyone.

Eligibility

  • 5+ years of B2C marketing experience, preferably in sports, SaaS, or tech industries
  • Proven track record of leading multi‑channel campaigns that deliver measurable ROI
  • Strong analytical skills with expertise in data‑driven decision making and marketing automation tools

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with performance‑based bonuses
  • Fully remote work setup, flexible hours, and generous paid time off
  • Health insurance options, professional development budget, and access to global sports events

How to apply

  1. Visit the TeamSnap careers page and click “Apply Now” for the Senior Marketing Manager role
  2. Upload your updated resume and a cover letter highlighting relevant achievements
  3. Complete the short online questionnaire and submit your application

FAQs

Can I work from Pakistan or India for this remote position?

Yes, the role is fully remote and open to candidates in Pakistan, India, Europe, and other regions with reliable internet connectivity.

Do I need a specific degree to apply?

A bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, or a related field is required; an MS, MBA, or PhD is a plus but not mandatory.
