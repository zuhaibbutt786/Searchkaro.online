Manager, Global Talent Acquisition – Cloudbeds (Remote/Worldwide)

Cloudbeds is reshaping hospitality with a cloud‑based platform used in over 150 countries. As the Manager of Global Talent Acquisition, you will design and execute hiring strategies that attract top tech and hospitality talent worldwide. This role offers the chance to work remotely while influencing growth across diverse markets, including Pakistan and India. You’ll partner with senior leaders to build high‑performing teams that drive our mission forward.

Eligibility

  • 5+ years of experience in talent acquisition, preferably in tech or hospitality sectors
  • Proven track record of managing end‑to‑end recruitment for senior and specialized roles
  • Strong understanding of global hiring practices and ability to work across time zones

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with performance‑based bonuses
  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision coverage
  • Flexible remote work policy and generous paid time off
  • Professional development stipend and access to global learning resources

How to apply

  1. Visit the Cloudbeds careers portal and locate the “Manager, Global Talent Acquisition” posting
  2. Upload your updated resume and a brief cover letter highlighting relevant global hiring experience
  3. Complete the short online questionnaire and submit your application

FAQs

Is this role fully remote?

Yes, the position is remote‑first. You can work from Pakistan, India, Europe, or any location with reliable internet.

Do I need a specific degree to apply?

A bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business, or a related field is preferred, but extensive recruiting experience can substitute for formal education.

What time zones will I be collaborating with?

You’ll work closely with teams in North America, Europe, and APAC, so flexibility to attend meetings across multiple time zones is essential.
