Dental Lab Technician – Removables (UK)

Dandy is revolutionizing the $400B dental industry with cutting‑edge technology. As a Dental Lab Technician – Removables, you’ll craft high‑quality prosthetics while driving customer experience excellence. Work with a global team, enjoy flexible scheduling, and contribute to a mission that simplifies dental care worldwide.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s degree in Dental Technology, Prosthodontics, or related field
  • Proven experience in removable prosthesis fabrication
  • Strong communication skills in English

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with performance bonuses
  • Flexible work hours and remote options
  • Access to continuous professional development and global networking

How to apply

  1. Submit your resume and cover letter via our online portal
  2. Prepare a portfolio of previous removable prosthesis work
  3. Attend a virtual interview with the hiring team

FAQs

Is relocation support available for candidates outside the UK?

Yes, Dandy offers relocation assistance for qualified candidates who are willing to move to the UK.

What languages are required for this role?

Proficiency in English is essential; knowledge of Urdu, Hindi, or other South Asian languages is a plus but not mandatory.
