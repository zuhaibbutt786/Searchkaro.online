Strategic Customer Success Manager, EMEA | Ashby

Ashby is hiring a Strategic Customer Success Manager to lead deep, consultative partnerships within the Enterprise space across the EMEA region. This role focuses on driving customer value, retention, and expansion through strategic account management. As part of Loren's team, you will act as a trusted advisor to enterprise clients, ensuring long-term success and alignment with business goals. This position is open to candidates worldwide, including those based in Pakistan, India, and Europe.

Eligibility

  • Proven experience in Customer Success, Account Management, or Client Services within the SaaS or Enterprise technology sector.
  • Strong consultative selling and stakeholder management skills, with a history of driving enterprise-level outcomes.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to operate effectively in a remote, distributed team environment across time zones.

Benefits

  • Competitive compensation package with performance-based incentives.
  • Comprehensive health, wellness, and professional development benefits.
  • Flexible remote work culture supporting work-life balance.

How to apply

  1. Prepare a tailored resume and cover letter highlighting your enterprise customer success experience.
  2. Submit your application through the official Ashby careers portal.
  3. Complete the initial screening and interview process with the hiring team.

FAQs

Yes, the role is listed as Worldwide, meaning candidates from Pakistan, India, and other regions are eligible to apply, provided they can effectively collaborate with the EMEA team.
