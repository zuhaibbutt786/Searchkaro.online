Join Welo Data, part of Welocalize, to contribute to global AI development. Our team of experts is seeking Korean-speaking AI Trainers with MS/PhD or relevant job roles to help build smarter AI systems. This opportunity is ideal for candidates in Pakistan, India, and Europe.

  1. Step 1: Apply with your resume and cover letter

FAQs

What is the role of an AI Trainer?

AI Trainers at Welo Data help develop and improve AI systems by training them using human-generated data. You will work on cutting-edge AI projects and contribute to global AI advancements.

What are the required qualifications for this role?

To qualify for the AI Trainers role, you must have a relevant MS/PhD or job role in AI, Linguistics, or related fields. Fluency in Korean and experience in AI training or language modeling are also essential requirements.

Are the projects remote or on-site?

As a remote AI Trainer, you will work on AI projects from the comfort of your home. Our team collaborates globally, so you will have opportunities to work with colleagues from different locations.
