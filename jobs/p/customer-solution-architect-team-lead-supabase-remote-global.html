Customer Solution Architect Team Lead – Supabase (Remote, Global)

Supabase, the open‑source Postgres development platform, is seeking a Customer Solution Architect Team Lead to own solution delivery for AMER customers. In this role you will guide technical teams, design end‑to‑end architectures, and ensure client satisfaction across the product suite. The position is fully remote, welcoming candidates from Pakistan, India, Europe and worldwide. You will work closely with sales, product, and engineering to drive adoption of Supabase's backend services.

Eligibility

  • 5+ years of experience designing and delivering cloud‑native solutions, preferably with PostgreSQL or similar databases
  • Strong expertise in backend technologies such as Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search
  • Proven leadership skills managing technical teams and mentoring junior architects
  • Excellent communication skills in English; ability to engage with global stakeholders
  • Experience working with SaaS customers in the AMER region is a plus

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with performance bonuses and equity options
  • Fully remote work setup with flexible hours and a global team culture
  • Health insurance, wellness stipend, and professional development budget
  • Opportunity to shape the future of an open‑source backend platform used by developers worldwide

How to apply

  1. Visit the Supabase careers page and locate the "Customer Solution Architect Team Lead" posting
  2. Submit your updated resume, a cover letter highlighting relevant experience, and any portfolio or case studies
  3. Complete the short online questionnaire and schedule a video interview with the hiring team

FAQs

Do I need to be based in the AMER time zone?

No. The role is fully remote and flexible, but you should be comfortable working occasional hours that overlap with AMER business hours.

Can candidates from Pakistan or India apply?

Yes. Supabase welcomes applicants from Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions, and provides support for remote work arrangements.
