Audio Transcription QA – Turkish (Andromeda Delta) – Remote

Andromeda Delta is hiring experienced Audio Transcription QA specialists to review Turkish speech recordings. You will resolve word‑level transcription and timestamp discrepancies, ensuring high‑quality data for AI training. This remote position welcomes talent from Pakistan, India, Europe and beyond. Work flexible hours, collaborate with a global team, and advance your career in language technology.

Eligibility

  • Minimum 2 years of experience in audio transcription, QA, or adjudication for Turkish language projects
  • Strong command of Turkish (native or near‑native) and excellent English reading skills
  • Familiarity with transcription tools, timestamp annotation, and quality‑control guidelines

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate with performance bonuses
  • Fully remote work – set your own schedule within agreed time zones

How to apply

  1. Visit the Andromeda Delta careers page and locate the "Audio Transcription QA – Turkish" posting
  2. Upload your updated resume and a brief cover letter highlighting relevant QA experience
  3. Complete the short online assessment to demonstrate transcription accuracy

FAQs

Do I need to be based in Turkey to apply?

No. The role is fully remote and open to qualified candidates residing in Pakistan, India, Europe or any other country with reliable internet access.

What equipment is required?

A computer with a stable internet connection, headphones, and access to the transcription platform provided by Andromeda Delta.
