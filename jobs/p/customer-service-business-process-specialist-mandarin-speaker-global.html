Customer Service Business Process Specialist (Mandarin Speaker) – Global

We are seeking a Mandarin‑speaking Customer Service Business Process Specialist to join our worldwide team. You will create and maintain SOPs, manage CS tickets, and drive service improvements. The role is fully remote, allowing candidates from Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions to contribute. Competitive compensation, flexible schedules, and professional development are part of the package.

Eligibility

  • Fluent Mandarin (native or near‑native) with strong written English
  • 2+ years experience in customer service, process documentation or SOP management
  • Proficiency with ticketing systems, macros, and knowledge‑base tools
  • Ability to work across time zones and collaborate with global teams

Benefits

  • Fully remote work with flexible hours
  • Competitive salary plus performance bonuses
  • Professional development budget and mentorship
  • Comprehensive health coverage for eligible regions

How to apply

  1. Prepare an updated CV and a cover letter highlighting Mandarin and CS process experience
  2. Submit your application through our online careers portal
  3. Complete the online assessment and attend virtual interviews with the hiring team

FAQs

Do I need to live in a specific country to be considered?

No. The position is remote and open to qualified candidates in Pakistan, India, Europe and other locations worldwide.

What level of Mandarin proficiency is required?

Native or near‑native fluency is required, along with the ability to communicate clearly in English.

What equipment do I need to work from home?

A reliable internet connection, a computer that can run our ticketing and documentation tools, and a headset for calls are essential.
