Lead Recruiter (Corporate Functions) Job at BJAK

BJAK is seeking a Lead Recruiter to build high-performing teams across corporate functions. This remote-first role supports our mission to provide smarter financial planning tools. You will drive hiring strategies for a global fintech company. Ideal for experienced recruiters based in Pakistan, India, or Europe.

Eligibility

  • 5+ years of experience in talent acquisition with a focus on corporate functions
  • Proven track record of leading end-to-end recruitment cycles in fast-paced environments
  • Strong understanding of remote hiring practices and global labor markets

Benefits

  • Fully remote work flexibility for candidates in Pakistan, India, and worldwide
  • Opportunity to shape the hiring culture of a mission-driven fintech company

How to apply

  1. Prepare a resume highlighting your experience in corporate function recruitment
  2. Submit your application through the official BJAK careers portal
  3. Complete the initial screening and interview process with the talent team

FAQs

Is this Lead Recruiter position available in Pakistan and India?

Yes, BJAK operates remotely and welcomes applications from qualified candidates in Pakistan, India, and other global regions.

What specific corporate functions will I be recruiting for?

You will focus on non-engineering roles such as finance, operations, marketing, and administrative support to support the company's growth.
