Remote Georgian Audio/Video Interpreter Job (Worldwide)

LSA is hiring Independently Contracted Interpreters to handle on-demand audio and video calls for Georgian speakers. This remote role allows you to work from anywhere, including Pakistan, India, and Europe. You will facilitate clear communication for clients requiring professional language support. It is an ideal opportunity for fluent speakers seeking flexible freelance work.

Eligibility

  • Native or near-native proficiency in Georgian and English
  • Reliable high-speed internet and quiet workspace
  • Experience with remote interpreting platforms preferred

Benefits

  • Work from home with flexible scheduling
  • Join a global network of professional interpreters

How to apply

  1. Visit the LSA careers page to find the Georgian Interpreter listing
  2. Submit your resume and a brief cover letter highlighting your language skills
  3. Complete the required proficiency assessment if requested

FAQs

No specific degree is mentioned. Proficiency in Georgian and English is the primary requirement.

Yes, this is a remote role open to candidates worldwide, including those in Pakistan, India, and Europe.
