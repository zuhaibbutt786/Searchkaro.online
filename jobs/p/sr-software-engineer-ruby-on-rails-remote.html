Sr. Software Engineer – Ruby on Rails (Remote)

CentralReach, a leader in autism and IDD care software, is looking for a Sr. Software Engineer specialized in Ruby on Rails. You will design and enhance platforms that support Applied Behavior Analysis, multidisciplinary therapy, and special education. Our solutions serve over 200,000 users globally, making a real impact on lives. This remote role offers the chance to work with a passionate team dedicated to improving therapy outcomes.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field
  • 5+ years of professional experience developing web applications with Ruby on Rails
  • Strong proficiency in RESTful APIs, PostgreSQL, Git, and Agile development practices
  • Excellent problem‑solving abilities and effective communication skills

Benefits

  • Competitive salary, health benefits, and flexible remote‑work policy
  • Opportunity to contribute to mission‑driven software that improves lives of individuals with autism and IDD

How to apply

  1. Visit the CentralReach careers page and locate the Sr. Software Engineer – Ruby on Rails posting
  2. Submit your updated resume and a cover letter highlighting relevant Ruby on Rails experience
  3. Complete the online technical assessment and schedule a virtual interview with the hiring team

FAQs

Is this position open to candidates based in Pakistan, India, or Europe?

Yes, the role is fully remote and welcomes qualified applicants from Pakistan, India, Europe, and other regions.

What technologies will I work with on a daily basis?

You will primarily work with Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL, JavaScript (React or Vue), Docker, and cloud services such as AWS.
