Frontend Engineer – Music | Remote Job for Pakistan & India

Join the Rights Systems team to build and operate Spotify Rights Center, the platform for managing music licensing rights. This Frontend Engineer role focuses on creating robust user interfaces for rights identification and enforcement. The position is open to candidates worldwide, including those in Pakistan and India. You will collaborate with partners to enhance the digital music rights ecosystem.

Eligibility

  • Proven experience in frontend development with modern JavaScript frameworks like React or TypeScript.
  • Strong understanding of UI/UX principles and ability to build accessible, responsive web applications.
  • Experience working in distributed teams and familiarity with music industry rights management is a plus.

Benefits

  • Fully remote work environment with flexible hours suitable for Pakistan and India time zones.
  • Competitive global compensation package and comprehensive health benefits.

How to apply

  1. Visit the official Spotify careers page to locate the Frontend Engineer – Music listing.
  2. Submit your application with an updated resume and a portfolio showcasing relevant frontend projects.
  3. Complete the initial technical assessment and prepare for interviews with the Rights Systems team.

FAQs

Is this Frontend Engineer role open to candidates in Pakistan and India?

Yes, this position is listed as Worldwide, making it accessible to qualified applicants in Pakistan, India, and other global regions.

Candidates should have strong proficiency in JavaScript, TypeScript, and React, along with experience in building scalable frontend architectures.
