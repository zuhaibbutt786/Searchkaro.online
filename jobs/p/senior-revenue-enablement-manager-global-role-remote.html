Senior Revenue Enablement Manager – Global Role (Remote)

Cribl is building the telemetry infrastructure that powers the AI era, partnering with IT and Security teams at the world’s largest enterprises. As a Senior Revenue Enablement Manager you will shape go‑to‑market strategies, enable sales teams, and accelerate revenue growth worldwide. The role is fully remote, allowing talent from Pakistan, India, Europe and beyond to contribute to a fast‑growing tech leader. You’ll work alongside product, marketing, and engineering experts to deliver measurable impact.

Eligibility

  • Minimum 7 years of experience in revenue enablement, sales operations, or related fields, preferably in SaaS or security‑analytics markets
  • Proven track record of designing and executing enablement programs that boost quota attainment and pipeline velocity
  • Strong analytical skills with expertise in data‑driven decision making, CRM (Salesforce) and GTM tools

Benefits

  • Competitive base salary + performance‑based bonuses, plus equity participation
  • Flexible remote work policy, health insurance, generous paid time off, and professional development budget

How to apply

  1. Visit the Cribl careers page and locate the Senior Revenue Enablement Manager listing
  2. Click “Apply Now”, upload your updated resume and a cover letter highlighting relevant achievements
  3. Complete the short online questionnaire and submit; a recruiter will contact you within 5‑7 business days

FAQs

Is this position open to candidates living in Pakistan and India?

Yes. The role is fully remote and welcomes qualified applicants from Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions.

What time zone overlap is expected for collaboration?

Cribl operates on a flexible schedule; we ask for at least 2‑3 hours of overlap with the US‑based leadership team, which works well from most Asian time zones.

Do I need a specific degree to apply?

A bachelor’s degree is required; an MS, MBA, or PhD in business, engineering, or a related field is a strong advantage but not mandatory.

What visa or work‑authorization requirements exist?

Since the role is remote, no visa sponsorship is needed. You must have legal right to work in your country of residence.
