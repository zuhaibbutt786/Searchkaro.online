Manager, Strategy & Analytics – Global (Remote) – Beyond Finance

Beyond Finance is seeking a Manager, Strategy & Analytics to lead data‑driven strategy across our worldwide operations. You will translate complex data into actionable insights that help millions break free from debt. The role offers the chance to work remotely while collaborating with teams in the US, Europe, Pakistan and India. Join us to make a measurable impact on financial wellbeing globally.

Eligibility

  • Master's (MS) or equivalent degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Data Science or related field
  • 5+ years of experience in strategy, analytics, or financial data analysis
  • Proven ability to turn data into strategic recommendations and lead cross‑functional projects

Benefits

  • Competitive salary, health benefits and performance bonuses
  • Fully remote work with flexible hours, enabling you to work from Pakistan, India, Europe or anywhere

How to apply

  1. Visit the Beyond Finance careers page and locate the Manager, Strategy & Analytics posting
  2. Upload your resume and a tailored cover letter highlighting relevant experience
  3. Complete the online application and await a follow‑up from the recruitment team

FAQs

Do I need to be based in the United States to apply?

No. The role is fully remote and open to qualified candidates in Pakistan, India, Europe and other regions.

What level of education is required?

A Master's (MS) degree or equivalent in a relevant discipline is required; a PhD is a plus but not mandatory.
