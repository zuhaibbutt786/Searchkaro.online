Account Director, Public Sector (DACH) – AI Intelligence

TRM Labs is looking for an Account Director to lead public‑sector engagements across the DACH region while supporting global clients. You will partner with government and private agencies to deploy AI‑driven intelligence platforms that trace illicit activity and disrupt crime. The role blends strategic account management with deep technical insight, enabling you to shape safer societies worldwide. Ideal candidates have a strong public‑sector background and a passion for cutting‑edge technology.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a relevant field (e.g., public policy, data science, cybersecurity)
  • 5+ years of experience managing public‑sector accounts or consulting for government agencies
  • Proven ability to translate AI‑powered solutions into tangible outcomes for investigators

Benefits

  • Competitive salary, health benefits, and performance bonuses
  • Access to advanced AI tools, continuous learning programs, and a global collaborative team

How to apply

  1. Submit your updated resume and a tailored cover letter via the TRM Labs careers portal
  2. Highlight your public‑sector experience and any work with AI or intelligence platforms
  3. A recruiter will review your application and contact you for the next interview steps

FAQs

Do I need to be based in the DACH region to apply?

While the role focuses on DACH clients, remote work from Pakistan, India, or any location with reliable internet is accepted.

What kind of travel is expected?

Occasional travel to client sites in Europe (including DACH) may be required, but most engagements are virtual.
