Remote Optometrist Job: Virtual Eye Care Specialist (Global)

Join a fully virtual optometry practice to deliver high-quality, human-centered care to patients nationwide. This remote role allows you to support in-office patients virtually from anywhere. It is an ideal opportunity for licensed professionals seeking flexibility and work-life balance.

  • Experience in clinical eye examinations and patient consultation
  • Reliable internet connection and quiet workspace for virtual visits

Benefits

  • 100% remote work flexibility with no office commute
  • Competitive compensation and professional development opportunities

How to apply

  1. Prepare your updated CV and license verification documents
  2. Submit your application through the official career portal
  3. Complete the initial virtual interview with the clinical team
