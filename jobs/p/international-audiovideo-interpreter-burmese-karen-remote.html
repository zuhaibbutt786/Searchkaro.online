International Audio/Video Interpreter – Burmese & Karen (Remote)

We are seeking skilled Burmese and Karen interpreters to provide on-demand audio and video interpretation services worldwide. As part of LSA’s network of independently contracted interpreters, you will handle calls, ensure accurate translation, and support clients across multiple time zones. This remote role offers flexibility, professional growth, and the chance to make a real difference in global communications.

Eligibility

  • Fluent in Burmese and Karen with strong oral and written communication skills.
  • Minimum of 2 years of professional interpretation or related experience.
  • Reliable high-speed internet, a quiet workspace, and a headset with microphone.

Benefits

  • Competitive pay per minute with transparent invoicing.
  • Flexible schedule – choose your own hours and work from anywhere.

How to apply

  1. Prepare a brief resume highlighting your interpretation experience.
  2. Submit your application through the LSA portal with a cover letter and a short audio sample.
  3. If shortlisted, complete a short interview and a live interpretation test.

FAQs

Do I need a university degree to apply?

No degree is required, but a proven track record in interpretation or related fields is essential.

What equipment do I need?

A stable internet connection, a headset with a microphone, and a quiet workspace are mandatory.
