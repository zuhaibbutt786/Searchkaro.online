Solutions Engineer – UK (Italian Speaking)

Benefits

  • Inclusive, people‑first culture with coaching and professional development
  • Competitive salary, flexible working arrangements, and global collaboration

How to apply

  1. Visit the Quantum Metric careers portal and locate the Solutions Engineer – UK (Italian Speaking) posting

  3. Submit the application and await a confirmation email from the recruitment team
