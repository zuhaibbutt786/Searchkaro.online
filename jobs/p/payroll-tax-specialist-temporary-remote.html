Payroll Tax Specialist (Temporary – Remote)

Fanatics, a global digital sports platform, is hiring a Payroll Tax Specialist on a temporary remote basis. You will ensure payroll tax compliance across multiple regions and work closely with the finance team to maintain accurate tax processes. This role offers the chance to contribute to a fast‑growing sports brand while working from anywhere.

Eligibility

  • Minimum 2+ years of experience in payroll tax or a related field
  • Strong knowledge of payroll tax regulations in multiple regions
  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related discipline

Benefits

  • Competitive temporary contract with performance incentives
  • Fully remote work – flexibility to work from Pakistan, India, Europe or anywhere

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Visit the Fanatics careers portal and locate the Payroll Tax Specialist posting
  2. Step 2: Submit your resume and a cover letter highlighting relevant payroll tax experience
  3. Step 3: Complete the short online questionnaire and await a confirmation email

No relocation is required. You can work flexible hours that align with the core finance team’s schedule.
