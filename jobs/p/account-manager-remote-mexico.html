Account Manager (Remote – Mexico)

Housecall Pro is looking for a proactive Account Manager to join our remote team from Mexico. In this role you’ll manage client relationships, drive adoption of our field‑service platform, and collaborate with cross‑functional teams worldwide. You’ll help shape products that make everyday life easier for homeowners and service professionals alike. This position offers a flexible, remote work environment and the chance to impact millions of users globally.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, or related field (MS or PhD preferred)
  • 3+ years of account management or sales experience in SaaS or tech
  • Strong communication skills in English; Spanish fluency is a plus

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with performance bonuses
  • Comprehensive health and wellness benefits
  • Unlimited PTO and flexible working hours

How to apply

  1. Submit your resume and cover letter via our online portal
  2. Complete a brief skills assessment and case study
  3. Attend a virtual interview with the hiring team

FAQs

Is this role truly remote?

Yes, you can work from anywhere in Mexico or globally as long as you have a reliable internet connection.

Will I need to travel?

Occasional travel to our headquarters or client sites may be required, but most work is done remotely.
