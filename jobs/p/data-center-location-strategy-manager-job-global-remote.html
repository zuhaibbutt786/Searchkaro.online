Data Center Location Strategy Manager Job: Global Remote

Meta is hiring a Data Center Location Strategy Manager to oversee long-range site selection for its global infrastructure.   • Deep understanding of global geographic, regulatory, and infrastructure landscapes.
  • Strong analytical skills to evaluate long-term portfolio risks and opportunities.

Benefits

  • Competitive global compensation package with equity options.
  • Opportunity to shape the physical infrastructure of the world's largest tech platforms.

How to apply

  1. Visit the official Meta careers website and search for 'Data Center Location Strategy Manager'.
  2. Ensure your resume highlights international site selection and strategic planning experience.
  3. Submit your application and prepare for technical and strategic case study interviews.
