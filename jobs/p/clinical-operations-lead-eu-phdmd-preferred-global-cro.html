Clinical Operations Lead (EU) – PhD/MD Preferred – Global CRO

Alimentiv is a worldwide contract research organization dedicated to gastrointestinal disease therapies. The Clinical Operations Lead (EU) will steer multi‑national studies, ensuring scientific rigor and regulatory compliance. This role offers the chance to collaborate with top investigators across Europe while leveraging talent from Pakistan and India. You will drive project timelines, mentor junior staff, and contribute to breakthrough treatments.

Eligibility

  • PhD, MD, or equivalent advanced degree in life sciences, clinical research, or related field
  • 5+ years of experience managing clinical trials, preferably in gastro‑intestinal therapeutic areas
  • Strong knowledge of EU regulatory requirements (EMA, ICH) and experience with global study sites

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with performance‑based bonuses and comprehensive health coverage
  • Flexible remote‑working options, professional development budget, and opportunity to travel across Europe

How to apply

  1. Visit the Alimentiv careers portal and locate the “Clinical Operations Lead (EU)” posting
  2. Upload your updated CV, a cover letter highlighting GI trial experience, and copies of relevant certifications
  3. Submit the application and await a confirmation email with next‑step interview details

FAQs

Can candidates from Pakistan or India apply for this EU‑based role?

Yes. Alimentiv welcomes qualified applicants from Pakistan, India, and other regions; remote work and relocation assistance are available based on project needs.

What language proficiency is required?

Fluent English is mandatory; additional proficiency in a European language (e.g., German, French) is advantageous but not required.

Is travel within Europe mandatory?

Travel is expected for site visits and sponsor meetings, typically 20‑30% of the time, with all expenses covered by the company.

What are the key performance metrics for this position?

Metrics include on‑time study start-up, adherence to budget, regulatory submission success rate, and team mentorship outcomes.
