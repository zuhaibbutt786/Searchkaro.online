Customer Success Application Engineer (EMEA) Job at Litmus

Litmus is seeking a Customer Success Application Engineer to support its mission of building the data foundation for industrial AI. This role involves making real-world data usable for AI adoption in the EMEA region. Candidates will work with contextualized data to enhance industrial applications. This is a remote-first opportunity suitable for global talent.

Eligibility

  • Strong background in software engineering or application development.
  • Experience with data pipelines, APIs, or industrial IoT systems.
  • Excellent communication skills for customer-facing technical support.
  • Ability to work effectively in a remote, distributed team environment.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary commensurate with experience and location.
  • Opportunity to work on cutting-edge industrial AI technologies.
  • Flexible remote work arrangement within the EMEA time zone.

How to apply

  1. Prepare a resume highlighting technical and customer success experience.
  2. Submit your application through the official Litmus careers portal.
  3. Complete the initial screening and technical assessment process.
