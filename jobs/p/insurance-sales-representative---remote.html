Insurance Sales Representative - Remote

The Allen Agency is looking for motivated individuals interested in building a flexible career within the insurance industry. This is not a traditional 9-to-5 office job; it's a remote opportunity with a flexible schedule. As an Insurance Sales Representative, you will be responsible for selling insurance policies to clients. If you are self‑motivated, results‑driven, and enjoy working independently, this could be the perfect opportunity for you.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor's degree in any field; experience in sales or customer service is a plus.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage your time effectively.

Benefits

  • Flexible working hours and remote work arrangement.
  • Opportunity to build a career in the insurance industry.
