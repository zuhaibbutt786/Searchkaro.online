Inside Sales Representative

At Beyond Finance, we're on a mission to help everyday Americans escape the endless cycle of crippling debt and step into a brighter financial future. As an Inside Sales Representative, you will reach out to customers, understand their financial challenges, and offer personalized solutions. You will collaborate with a compassionate team to create a meaningful financial impact. If you are driven to help others and excel in sales, we want to hear from you.

Eligibility

  • 1+ year of sales experience
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to thrive in a fast‑paced, target‑driven environment

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package
  • Opportunity to make a meaningful impact on people’s financial well‑being

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Review the job description and confirm you meet the eligibility criteria
  2. Step 2: Submit your resume and a cover letter highlighting your relevant experience
  3. Step 3: Complete the interview process and discuss your potential role

FAQs

What level of sales experience is required?

A minimum of one year of proven sales experience is required, preferably in a financial or SaaS environment.

Is remote work possible for this role?

Yes, Beyond Finance offers flexible work arrangements, including remote options, depending on business needs and candidate location.
