Promo Features Marketing & Commercialisation Lead

Playson is seeking a dynamic Promo Features Marketing & Commercialisation Lead to champion its suite of promotional tools, such as Power Blasts, Flexible Free Spins, Grand Race and Turbo Races. You will design and execute marketing strategies that boost player engagement and revenue across global operator networks. The role works closely with product, analytics and sales teams to optimise commercial performance. Candidates based in Pakistan, India or Europe are encouraged to apply.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business or a related field
  • Minimum 3 years experience in iGaming marketing or promotional product commercialisation
  • Strong analytical, communication and project‑management skills

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and performance bonuses
  • Access to cutting‑edge iGaming technology and a global talent network

How to apply

  1. Visit the Playson careers portal and locate the Promo Features Marketing & Commercialisation Lead posting
  2. Upload your CV and a tailored cover letter highlighting relevant experience
  3. Complete the short online questionnaire and await interview invitation

FAQs

What are the main responsibilities of this role?

You will lead the marketing and commercialisation of Playson’s promotional tools, create campaigns that increase player engagement, analyse performance data and collaborate with product, sales and analytics teams to drive revenue.

Can I work remotely from Pakistan, India or Europe?

Playson offers flexible working arrangements; candidates located in Pakistan, India or any European country can apply and discuss remote or hybrid options during the interview process.
