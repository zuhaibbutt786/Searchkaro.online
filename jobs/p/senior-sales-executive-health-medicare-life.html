Senior Sales Executive, Health, Medicare & Life

QuinStreet is a pioneer in powering decentralized online marketplaces that match searchers and “research and compare” consumers with brands. We are looking for a Senior Sales Executive to join our team in the Health, Medicare & Life sector. As a Senior Sales Executive, you will play a crucial role in driving sales growth and expanding our market presence across Pakistan, India, Europe and the globe. If you have a passion for sales and a background in health, Medicare, or life insurance, we encourage you to apply.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or a related field; MS or PhD preferred
  • Proven sales experience in the health, Medicare, or life insurance industry
  • Strong understanding of digital media and performance marketing

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and commission structure
  • Opportunity to work in a dynamic and growing industry

How to apply

  1. Submit your resume and a cover letter explaining why you're a great fit for this role
  2. Complete the online application form on our careers portal
  3. Participate in a series of interviews with the hiring team
