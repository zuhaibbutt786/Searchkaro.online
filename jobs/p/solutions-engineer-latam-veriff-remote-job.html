Solutions Engineer LATAM: Veriff Remote Job

Veriff seeks a commercially minded Solutions Engineer to join the LATAM team. You will partner with Sales to drive new business and accelerate revenue growth. This role requires strong technical skills and fluency in Spanish. It is an excellent opportunity for remote professionals in Pakistan, India, and globally.

Eligibility

  • Proven experience as a Solutions Engineer, Pre-Sales, or similar technical sales role.
  • Fluency in Spanish is mandatory; English proficiency is required.
  • Strong ability to explain complex identity verification technology to non-technical stakeholders.

Benefits

  • Fully remote position with flexible working hours.
  • Competitive salary and equity package.
  • Opportunity to work with a global team in a high-growth fintech sector.

How to apply

  1. Prepare your resume highlighting technical sales and LATAM market experience.
  2. Submit your application via the official Veriff careers page.
  3. Complete the initial screening and technical assessment if shortlisted.

FAQs

Is this job open to candidates in Pakistan and India?

Yes, Veriff operates globally and this remote role is open to qualified candidates in Pakistan, India, and other regions, provided you meet the language and time zone requirements.

What is the primary responsibility of this Solutions Engineer?

The primary responsibility is to support the Sales team by demonstrating Veriff’s technology, addressing technical queries, and helping close new business deals in the LATAM region.
