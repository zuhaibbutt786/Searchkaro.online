Salman Khan Sidesteps 'Maatrubhumi' Clash, Apologises to Shraddha Kapoor

Salman Khan said he would not engage in a public feud over the upcoming film Maatrubhumi and later posted an apology to Shraddha Kapoor. The statements have sparked discussion across South Asian media, especially in Pakistan.

Latest developments

In a recent interview, Bollywood veteran Salman Khan clarified his stance on the controversy surrounding the upcoming film Maatrubhumi. He said he chose not to clash with Shah Rukh Khan's project King or with Khan's daughter Suhana, emphasizing that he prefers to keep his focus on his own productions rather than engage in public disputes. Khan added that the timing of the two releases could lead to unnecessary competition, and he does not wish to fuel a media battle that could distract from the films' artistic merits.

Khan also addressed rumours that Maatrubhumi might be involved in a clash with another film titled Eetha. He reiterated that he will not take part in any exchange of statements or social media posts that could be interpreted as a feud. Instead, he urged the parties involved to let the audience decide based on the quality of the work. The actor highlighted his long‑standing policy of staying away from headline‑grabbing arguments, preferring to let his films speak for themselves.

Following his comments, actress Shraddha Kapoor, who was linked to the Maatrubhumi dispute, received an apology from Khan on his official social media account. Khan wrote that he was sorry for any inconvenience caused to Kapoor and expressed respect for her professionalism. The apology was brief but was widely reported by Indian entertainment portals, including The Times of India and Filmfare, and quickly circulated among fans on various platforms.

Pakistani media outlets, which regularly cover Bollywood news, picked up the story and highlighted Khan's apology as a sign of goodwill. Social media users in Pakistan shared the news, noting that both Khan and Kapoor enjoy large fan bases in the country. The coverage sparked conversations about the influence of Indian cinema in Pakistan and the expectations fans have regarding the conduct of their favourite stars.
