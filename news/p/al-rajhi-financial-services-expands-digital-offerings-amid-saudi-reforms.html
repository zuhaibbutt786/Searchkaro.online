Al Rajhi Financial Services Expands Digital Offerings Amid Saudi Reforms

Al Rajhi Financial Services is rolling out new digital tools to meet growing demand for online banking in Saudi Arabia. The move aligns with the kingdom’s Vision 2030 reforms.

Latest developments

Al Rajhi Financial Services (Arabic: الراجحي المالية, meaning 'Al Rajhi Financial') is one of the largest banks in Saudi Arabia. The group has been working on expanding its digital platform to keep pace with the country’s Vision 2030 agenda, which seeks to broaden financial services and improve customer experience.

The bank has introduced a new mobile application that offers real‑time account monitoring, instant loan approvals, and a range of payment services. These features are part of a broader strategy to reduce reliance on physical branches and to provide customers with 24/7 access to banking.

Saudi Arabia’s central bank has issued new guidelines that encourage digital banking and fintech collaboration. Al Rajhi Financial Services is expected to comply with these rules while exploring partnerships with technology firms to enhance its product suite.

In addition to consumer banking, the group is focusing on supporting small and medium enterprises through tailored financing solutions. This approach is designed to stimulate economic activity and align with national development goals.

Why it matters

For everyday consumers, the expansion of digital services means faster transactions and lower fees. Customers can open accounts, apply for credit, and manage investments from their smartphones, reducing the need for in‑branch visits.

The move also supports the government’s objective of increasing financial inclusion. By making banking services more accessible, the bank helps to bring more residents into the formal financial system, which can improve savings rates and credit availability.

From a sector perspective, Al Rajhi’s digital push raises the bar for competitors. Other banks are likely to accelerate their own technology initiatives, fostering a more competitive and innovative banking environment across the kingdom.

Background

Al Rajhi Bank was founded in 1957 by the Al Rajhi family and has grown into the world’s largest Islamic bank by assets. Its headquarters are in Riyadh, and it operates a network of branches throughout Saudi Arabia.

Over the past decade, the group has diversified into a range of financial services, including wealth management, insurance, and digital banking. The company’s emphasis on Islamic finance principles has helped it maintain a strong reputation among local and international clients.

The bank’s contribution to the Saudi economy is significant, providing financing for infrastructure projects, housing, and commercial ventures. Its commitment to sustainable growth aligns with the kingdom’s long‑term development plans.

What happens next

Regulators are expected to issue further guidance on digital banking standards, which could open new avenues for product development. Al Rajhi Financial Services is likely to respond by enhancing its cybersecurity measures and expanding its fintech collaborations.

The bank may also explore cross‑border partnerships to offer international remittance services, reflecting the growing demand from expatriate workers and global investors.
