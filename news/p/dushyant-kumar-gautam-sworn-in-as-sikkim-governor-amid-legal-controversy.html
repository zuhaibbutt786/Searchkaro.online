Dushyant Kumar Gautam Sworn in as Sikkim Governor Amid Legal Controversy

Latest developments

On 15 September 2024, Dushyant Kumar Gautam was formally sworn in as the 13th Governor of Sikkim at the Raj Bhavan in Gangtok. The ceremony was attended by senior BJP leaders, the Chief Minister, and a small contingent of officials. The event was marked by a subdued mood, as the opposition and civil society groups had been vocal about the timing of the appointment.

Shortly after the oath, the Sikkim Bar Association issued a statement condemning the decision. The association cited the pending legal case against Gautam, linked to the Ankita Bhandari controversy, and argued that his appointment violated the principle of judicial independence. The statement called for a review of the appointment process and urged the central government to reconsider.

The Congress party, which holds a minority in the state assembly, reacted strongly. Party leaders accused the Bharatiya Janata Party of using the governor's post to shield Gautam from potential legal scrutiny. They claimed that the central government had rushed the appointment to avoid a court hearing that could have implicated the former chief minister, Ankita Bhandari, in corruption allegations.

National media outlets have covered the dispute extensively, with commentators debating the propriety of appointing a political figure with unresolved legal matters to a constitutional post. Public opinion on social media is divided, with some residents supporting the BJP’s decision and others expressing concern over the perceived erosion of checks and balances in the state’s governance.

Why it matters

The controversy raises questions about the integrity of constitutional appointments in India. Governors are expected to act impartially, and any perception of bias can undermine public trust in democratic institutions. The Sikkim Bar Association’s protest signals a broader concern among legal professionals about the potential politicisation of key state offices.

Politically, the appointment has intensified the rivalry between the BJP and Congress in the northeastern region. The BJP’s decision to place Gautam in a high‑profile role may be interpreted as a strategic move to consolidate influence in Sikkim, while Congress’s opposition highlights the party’s ongoing challenge to counter the BJP’s expanding footprint.

For the citizens of Sikkim, the debate touches on accountability and transparency. If the governor’s legal background remains unresolved, it could set a precedent for future appointments, potentially weakening the mechanisms that hold public officials accountable for past conduct.

Background

Dushyant Kumar Gautam has been a prominent figure in the Bharatiya Janata Party for over two decades, serving in various capacities including as a Member of Parliament and a minister in the central government. His political career has been marked by a focus on development projects and infrastructure initiatives across the country.

The Ankita Bhandari case involves allegations of corruption and financial mismanagement during Bhandari’s tenure as Chief Minister of Sikkim from 2014 to 2019. Investigations by the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) have led to a pending court case that could potentially implicate Gautam, who is believed to have had close ties with Bhandari’s administration. The case remains unresolved, and legal experts have warned that appointing a figure linked to the controversy could compromise the impartiality expected of a governor.

What happens next

The central government has indicated that it will review the legal status of Gautam before any further action is taken. Meanwhile, the Sikkim Bar Association is preparing to file a formal petition with the Supreme Court, seeking a judicial review of the appointment. The Congress party plans to raise the issue in the Lok Sabha, demanding a transparent inquiry into Gautam’s legal background.
