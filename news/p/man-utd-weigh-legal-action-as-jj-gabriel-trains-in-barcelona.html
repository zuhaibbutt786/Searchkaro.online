Man Utd weigh legal action as JJ Gabriel trains in Barcelona

Manchester United is preparing to take legal action if academy star JJ Gabriel leaves the club. The teenager has been spotted training in Barcelona as interest from European giants intensifies.

Manchester United is reportedly considering legal action if winger JJ Gabriel departs the club without a formal transfer agreement. According to Sky Sports, the Red Devils are monitoring the situation closely as several top European clubs, including Bayern Munich, Barcelona, and Real Madrid, pursue the young talent. The club's stance suggests a firm position against any unauthorized exit, highlighting the value they place on their academy prospects.

Recent reports from the Manchester Evening News indicate that Gabriel has been seen training with Barcelona's setup. This development has raised concerns within Manchester United regarding the player's commitment and the potential for a free transfer or a breach of contract. The sighting in Barcelona adds fuel to the fire, suggesting that the player may already be integrating into the Catalan club's environment.

While specific details of his comments are not fully detailed in the headlines, his public acknowledgment confirms that Barcelona is actively involved in the negotiations. This high-profile interest from multiple elite clubs underscores the significant market value of Gabriel at such a young age.
