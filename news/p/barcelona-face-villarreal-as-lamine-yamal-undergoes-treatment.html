Barcelona face Villarreal as Lamine Yamal undergoes treatment

Barcelona's rising star Lamine Yamal received medical attention ahead of the La Liga match against Villarreal. The incident has raised questions about his availability for both club and country.

Latest developments

Barcelona confirmed on Saturday that academy forward Lamine Yamal was taken to the club medical centre after showing signs of a possible injury during the pre‑match warm‑up. Sources close to the team said the 16‑year‑old appeared to have suffered a knock to his lower leg, prompting immediate assessment by the club’s physiotherapy staff. The medical report indicated that Yamal received treatment and was later cleared to travel with the squad for the upcoming La Liga encounter with Villarreal. While the exact nature of the injury was not disclosed, the swift response was aimed at preventing any escalation before the match kickoff. The injury scare arrived at a critical moment for Barcelona, which is seeking to regain momentum after a mixed start to the season. Coach Xavi Hernández is expected to field a side that includes Yamal, who has been praised for his technical ability and composure on the ball despite his age. However, the uncertainty surrounding his fitness has forced the coaching staff to consider alternative options on the left wing, including veteran players who can provide defensive stability. The club’s medical team will continue to monitor Yamal throughout the week, with a final decision likely to be made after the team’s training session on Tuesday. Germany’s head coach Julian Flick was quoted as saying he had spoken with Spain manager Luis de la Fuente about the young forward’s condition. Both coaches are expected to stay in close contact as the player’s health is reassessed.
