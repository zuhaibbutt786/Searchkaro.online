Sara Ali Khan’s Emotional Visit to Amritsar’s Golden Temple Sparks Buzz

Latest developments

On the day of Dussehra, Bollywood actress Sara Ali Khan travelled to Amritsar’s Golden Temple, the holiest shrine of Sikhism, to offer prayers and wish the festival’s devotees. Video footage of her standing in front of the temple’s golden dome, tears streaming down her face, quickly circulated across social media platforms. The clip captured her pausing for a moment of silence, then speaking softly about the importance of peace and unity.

The actress was accompanied by fellow actor Ayushmann Khurrana, who joined her in a brief ceremony at the temple. Together they observed the traditional rituals, including the lighting of lamps and the distribution of sweets to visitors. Khurrana’s presence added a layer of star power to the event, drawing additional media attention.

The emotional moment resonated with audiences worldwide, especially in Pakistan where the phrase "udta teer"—meaning "flying arrow" in Urdu—has surged in search queries. Social media users shared the video with captions expressing admiration for the actress’s humility and the symbolic significance of the temple during the festival.

The Golden Temple, located in the city of Amritsar, is a symbol of spiritual inclusivity. Its golden dome reflects the light of the divine, and it has historically welcomed people of all faiths. The Dussehra celebrations at the temple are a rare sight, as the festival is predominantly associated with Hindu traditions. The convergence of a Bollywood star, a Sikh shrine, and a Hindu festival created a unique cultural moment that captured the imagination of viewers across borders.

Background

The Golden Temple, also known as Harmandir Sahib, is the central place of worship for Sikhs. Built in the 1580s, it has become a symbol of equality and devotion. Its architecture, featuring a gold-plated dome and a surrounding pool, draws millions of pilgrims annually.

Dussehra, celebrated on the 15th day of the Hindu month of Ashwin, marks the victory of good over evil. In India, it is observed with fireworks, processions, and the burning of effigies. The festival’s celebration at a Sikh shrine is uncommon, making the event notable.

Sara Ali Khan, daughter of Bollywood actors Saif Ali Khan and Kareena Kapoor, has a history of engaging with social causes.
