This is particularly relevant for items that may sell out quickly, such as popular sizes and colors of core apparel lines.

Background

Lululemon has become one of the most recognized names in the athletic wear sector, known for its high-quality materials and focus on comfort and performance. The brand has consistently ranked among the top retailers in the US, with a strong presence both online and in physical stores. Its product lines, including the Align and Wunder Under collections, have become cultural staples in fitness and casual wear.

Black Friday has traditionally been the peak day for retail discounts in the United States, following Thanksgiving. However, in recent years, many major retailers, including Lululemon, have begun releasing sales earlier in the week or even in the preceding days. This change is driven by the desire to manage website traffic and inventory more effectively, as well as to compete with other brands that have already launched their promotions.

The current sale is part of Lululemon’s annual holiday promotion cycle, which typically includes a mix of new arrivals and seasonal favorites. The brand often uses this period to introduce new colorways or styles, while also clearing out existing inventory to make room for new stock in the following season.
