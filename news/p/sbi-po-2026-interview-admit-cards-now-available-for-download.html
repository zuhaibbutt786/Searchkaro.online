SBI PO 2026 Interview Admit Cards Now Available for Download

The State Bank of India has posted the 2026 interview admit cards online, prompting a surge of downloads in Pakistan. Candidates are urged to retrieve their PDFs promptly as final results loom.

Latest developments

The State Bank of India (SBI) has released the interview admit cards for the 2026 Probationary Officer (PO) recruitment on its official portal. Candidates can now download the call letters in PDF format by visiting the link provided on the SBI website. The announcement was first reported by Jagran Josh, which shared a direct download link for the admit card. The admit cards contain essential details such as the candidate’s roll number, test centre, and schedule for the group exercise and interview stages. According to the Times of India, the final result for the 2026 SBI PO selection process is expected to be published shortly on the sbi.bank.in portal. The result will confirm the candidates who have cleared Phase III, which includes the group exercise and interview. The bank has urged all aspirants to keep their admit cards handy, as they will be required for verification at the test centres. PW Live also confirmed that the interview call letters are now live and that the group exercise admit cards can be downloaded alongside the interview admit card. The portal lists the download as a single PDF file, simplifying the process for candidates who need to print multiple documents. The release comes after a brief delay caused by technical issues on the SBI website, which were resolved earlier this week. The download surge has been noticeable in Pakistan, where a significant number of Indian exam aspirants reside or have familial ties. Local internet service providers reported a spike in traffic to the SBI download page, and several Pakistani educational forums are sharing the links and offering guidance on how to complete the download on slower connections.

For many Pakistani residents, the SBI PO exam represents a lucrative career opportunity in one of South Asia’s largest banking institutions. The ability to download the admit card quickly ensures that candidates can meet the strict deadlines set by the bank and avoid disqualification due to missing documentation. The digital nature of the admit card also highlights the growing reliance on online platforms for government and corporate recruitment in the region. Furthermore, the upcoming result announcement could influence the job market in Pakistan’s private banking sector, as candidates who succeed may seek similar roles locally or consider relocation. Employers are watching the outcome to gauge the pool of talent familiar with SBI’s rigorous selection process.

Background

The SBI Probationary Officer exam is conducted in multiple phases, starting with a preliminary test, followed by a mains examination, and culminating in a group exercise and personal interview. The 2026 cycle began with the preliminary exam in early 2025 and has progressed through each stage according to the bank’s published schedule. Historically, the interview admit card is issued a few weeks before the final assessment, allowing candidates to plan travel to designated test centres across India. The admit card serves as both an identity proof and a gate‑keeping document, and failure to present it can result in immediate exclusion from the interview. In recent years, the SBI PO recruitment has attracted over a million applicants annually, making it one of the most competitive banking exams in the subcontinent. The 2026 batch is expected to be slightly larger, reflecting the bank’s expansion plans and the continued interest from neighboring countries.

What happens next

The bank has indicated that the final merit list will be uploaded on the SBI website within the next two weeks. Candidates who clear the interview will receive an appointment letter and will be required to complete medical and background verification before joining. Applicants who do not make the cut are advised to monitor the SBI portal for future recruitment drives, as the bank typically announces new cycles every year. Training institutes in Pakistan are already preparing supplementary courses to help aspirants improve their chances in the next round.
