Background

Pakistan and Sri Lanka have a long‑standing cricket rivalry dating back to Sri Lanka’s Test debut in 1982. Over the decades, the two sides have produced memorable contests, including Pakistan’s 1992 World Cup win over Sri Lanka and the 2017 Champions Trophy final that went to a Super Over. Their encounters are often characterized by aggressive batting and spin‑friendly conditions, making matches between them popular with fans across South Asia.

Sri Lanka’s economy has been under strain since the 2022 debt crisis, which led to severe shortages of essential goods and a sharp devaluation of the rupee. Inflation has remained above 10 percent for most of 2023 and 2024, prompting the government to implement price controls on staples. The recent wheat flour price hike reflects lingering supply chain bottlenecks and the island’s reliance on imported wheat, underscoring the challenges of achieving price stability.
