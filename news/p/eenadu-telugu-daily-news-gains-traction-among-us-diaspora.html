Eenadu: Telugu Daily News Gains Traction Among US Diaspora

Background

Eenadu was founded in 1931 and has since become a cornerstone of Telugu journalism. It is known for its broad coverage of politics, sports, entertainment, and social issues. The newspaper has a strong legacy in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, where it is often considered a primary source of daily information.

The Telugu diaspora in the US is significant, with large populations in states like Texas, California, New Jersey, and New York. The rise of the internet has made it easier for these communities to access news in real-time, regardless of their physical location.

Historically, diaspora communities relied on printed copies shipped from India or limited local news segments. The transition to digital platforms has democratized access, allowing even younger generations who may not read print to stay connected through mobile devices.
