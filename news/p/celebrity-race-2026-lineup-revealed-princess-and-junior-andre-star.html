Celebrity Race 2026 line‑up revealed: Princess and Junior Andre star

The Celebrity Race Across the World 2026 has announced its star‑filled line‑up, featuring Princess and Junior Andre. The series will take contestants on a globe‑spanning adventure with high‑stakes challenges.

Latest developments

BBC Television announced the full line‑up for the fourth series of Celebrity Race Across the World, set to air in 2026. The announcement came during a press briefing in London, where the network confirmed that the programme will return to its original format of contestants racing across multiple countries to reach a final destination.

The star‑filled roster includes Princess, a former Olympic sprinter, and Junior Andre, a popular singer, both of whom were highlighted in the Belfast Telegraph’s coverage. Other confirmed participants are former footballer Tom Hiddleston, comedian Liza Koshy, and reality‑TV star Sophie Ellis‑Bextor, bringing a mix of sports, music and comedy talent to the show.

The series will feature 12 episodes, each covering a different continent and a series of physical and mental challenges. Filming is scheduled to begin in late spring, with locations ranging from the deserts of Namibia to the fjords of Norway.

According to BBC’s entertainment director, the programme aims to combine adventure with celebrity appeal, hoping to attract a broad audience. Viewers can expect a blend of travelogue, competition and personal stories as the contestants navigate unfamiliar terrain.

Why it matters

For viewers across Great Britain, the Celebrity Race offers a familiar format with a fresh twist. The inclusion of high‑profile personalities like Princess and Junior Andre is expected to boost viewership, especially among younger demographics who follow the celebrities on social media.

The programme also provides a platform for cultural exchange, as contestants visit remote villages and major cities, showcasing local traditions and landscapes. This aligns with BBC’s commitment to diverse representation and global storytelling.

Economically, the show is projected to generate significant advertising revenue and boost tourism interest in featured destinations. Local businesses in filming locations have already expressed enthusiasm for the influx of production crews and media attention.

Background

Celebrity Race Across the World first aired in 2023, quickly becoming one of the network’s most watched reality series. The premise—celebrity contestants racing across the world—has proven popular, drawing comparisons to long‑running adventure shows.

The show’s production team has built a reputation for logistical precision, coordinating complex travel arrangements and safety protocols. Previous seasons have highlighted the importance of teamwork, resilience and adaptability among participants.

The 2026 series builds on this legacy, adding new challenges such as urban obstacle courses and wilderness survival tasks. The format remains true to the original concept while introducing innovative twists to keep audiences engaged.

What happens next

Filming is slated to begin in May 2026, with the first episode scheduled for broadcast in late July. The production will travel through six continents, culminating in a final race across the Sahara Desert.

Viewers can anticipate weekly updates on the BBC iPlayer, where behind‑the‑scenes footage and contestant interviews will be available. The network has also announced a companion podcast to provide deeper insights into each episode.
