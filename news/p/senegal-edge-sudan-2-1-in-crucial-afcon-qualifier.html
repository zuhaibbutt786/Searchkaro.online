Senegal Edge Sudan 2-1 in Crucial AFCON Qualifier

Senegal defeated Sudan 2-1 in a tightly contested Africa Cup of Nations qualifier on Saturday. The result puts the Senegalese side in a stronger position as the qualification race intensifies.

Latest developments

The match between Sudan and Senegal took place at the Al-Merrikh Stadium in Omdurman on Saturday, with the Senegalese side emerging victorious by a narrow 2-1 margin. Early pressure from Sudan saw them create several chances, but a disciplined Senegal defence held firm. In the 27th minute, Senegal broke the deadlock through a well‑timed header from their forward, giving them a 1-0 lead. Sudan responded quickly, equalising before halftime after a swift counter‑attack that left the Senegal goalkeeper exposed. The second half was marked by tactical adjustments from both coaches, but it was Senegal who found the decisive goal in the 78th minute, restoring their lead and ultimately sealing the win.

The victory is a significant boost for Senegal's qualification campaign. According to CAFOnline, Senegal, alongside Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon and Benin, have been actively strengthening their bids for the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations. The win against Sudan adds three points to Senegal's tally, placing them firmly in the top tier of Group B. The team’s recent form, highlighted by a disciplined defensive record and an efficient attack, aligns with the broader strategy outlined by the Senegalese Football Federation to secure early qualification and avoid a last‑minute scramble.

Sudan, on the other hand, now faces a more challenging path. The loss leaves them trailing the group leaders and intensifies pressure on their coaching staff to deliver results in the remaining fixtures. The Sudanese squad, while showing moments of promise, struggled to convert possession into clear-cut chances, a shortfall that the VAVEL live‑score report emphasised. Their next match against a higher‑ranked opponent will be crucial in determining whether they can remain in contention for a qualification spot.

The broader context of the qualifiers sees several African nations jockeying for position as the tournament approaches. The CAF has confirmed that the top two teams from each group automatically qualify, while the best third‑placed teams will also have a chance through a playoff system. Senegal's win therefore not only adds points but also improves their goal difference, a factor that could become decisive if teams finish level on points later in the campaign.

The Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualification process for the 2025 edition began in early 2024, featuring 54 member associations divided into ten groups. Sudan and Senegal were placed in Group B, alongside other regional rivals. Historically, Senegal has been one of the continent's powerhouses, reaching the AFCON final in 2019 and consistently producing talent for top European clubs. Sudan, while not as decorated, has a proud footballing heritage and has qualified for the tournament on several occasions, most recently in 2022.

In their previous encounter during the 2023 qualifiers, Senegal secured a comfortable win, setting a precedent for their dominance in the head‑to‑head record.
