Amitabh Bachchan praises Shah Rukh Khan's grit, picks up Gen Z slang

Amitabh Bachchan lauded Shah Rukh Khan's perseverance during a candid conversation on television. He later learned contemporary Gen Z expressions from contestant Aarya Sahani, sparking online buzz in Pakistan.

Latest developments

During a recent episode of the popular quiz show "Kaun Banega Crorepati", veteran actor Amitabh Bachchan engaged in a candid discussion with fellow actor Rajkummar Rao. In the conversation, Bachchan highlighted Shah Rukh Khan's refusal to accept defeat, describing the star as someone who "never gives up" and whose confidence grows when faced with challenges. The remarks were reported by Gulf News and echoed in a Moneycontrol article, where Bachchan reiterated his admiration for Khan's resilience, noting that the Bollywood icon consistently pushes forward despite setbacks.

Later in the week, Bachchan appeared as a guest on a televised talent competition where he interacted with contestant Aarya Sahani. Sahani offered the legendary actor a quick lesson in contemporary Gen Z vocabulary, explaining terms such as "suss" and "gwop" that are popular among younger internet users. Telangana Today covered the exchange, noting that Bachchan responded with good humour and thanked Sahani for the crash‑course, acknowledging the rapid evolution of language among youth.

The two separate appearances generated considerable discussion on social media platforms in Pakistan. Searches for Amitabh Bachchan spiked, with the search interest label exceeding 200, indicating a high level of curiosity among Pakistani netizens. Viewers praised the actor's willingness to engage with both senior colleagues and younger talent, and many shared clips of his Gen Z lesson, commenting on the blend of nostalgia and modernity that the veteran brings to contemporary media.

By praising Khan's mindset, Bachchan reinforces a narrative of perseverance that aligns with the aspirations of many young Pakistanis.
