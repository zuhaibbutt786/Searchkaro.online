Background

The British Superbike Championship (BSB) is the leading motorcycle racing series in the country, featuring production‑based machines modified for competition. Since its inception in 1988, the series has served as a proving ground for riders aspiring to compete on the world stage. Brands Hatch, a circuit that first opened in 1926, has hosted numerous BSB rounds and is renowned for its fast, flowing layout and challenging elevation changes.

The 2026 season comprises twelve rounds, each offering riders the chance to accumulate points toward the championship. Pre‑qualifying sessions determine the starting grid, while practice sessions allow teams to adjust bike settings for optimal performance. The combination of rider skill, team engineering, and circuit characteristics makes each round a distinct test.

Kyle Ryde, a veteran of the series, has previously secured multiple podiums and is regarded as a consistent front‑runner. Redding, a younger talent, has risen quickly through the ranks, earning a reputation for aggressive riding and rapid lap times. Their rivalry adds a compelling storyline to the current championship narrative.
