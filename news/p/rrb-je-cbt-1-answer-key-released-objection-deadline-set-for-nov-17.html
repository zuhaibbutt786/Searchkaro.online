RRB JE CBT 1 Answer Key Released; Objection Deadline Set for Nov 17

Candidates appearing for the Railway Junior Engineer Computer Based Test can now access the provisional answer key. The board has opened a window for objections until mid-November.

Latest developments

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has officially published the provisional answer key for the Junior Engineer (JE) Computer Based Test (CBT 1). The document is now available for download on the official RRB digital portal. This release allows aspirants to cross-check their responses against the official solutions provided by the examination body.

According to recent reports, the objection window for the answer key is open until November 17. Candidates who believe there are discrepancies in the provided answers can submit their grievances through the designated online interface. The process requires users to register their objections question by question, often accompanied by supporting evidence or references to standard textbooks.

The release of the answer key follows the completion of the CBT 1 phase for the JE recruitment drive. It is a standard procedure in RRB examinations to provide a provisional key before declaring final results. This step ensures transparency and gives applicants a fair opportunity to challenge any perceived errors in the answer sheet.

News outlets have highlighted the importance of acting within the specified timeline. Once the objection period closes, the board will review the submitted queries. No further changes will be made to the answer key after the deadline has passed, making the next two weeks critical for all test-takers.

Why it matters

For millions of applicants in India, the RRB JE exam is a primary gateway to stable government employment in the railway sector. The accuracy of the answer key directly impacts the final merit list. A single incorrect answer in the key can alter a candidate's score significantly, potentially moving them in or out of the selection zone.

The ability to raise objections is a safeguard for candidates who may have encountered ambiguous questions or identified factual errors in the provided solutions. This mechanism helps maintain the integrity of the recruitment process. It ensures that the final results reflect a fair assessment of the candidates' knowledge and skills.

As the railway sector continues to expand, competition for these positions remains intense. The current cycle involves a large number of applicants, making every mark count. The timely release of the answer key and the clear communication of the objection deadline help reduce uncertainty and allow candidates to plan their next steps accordingly.

Background

The Railway Recruitment Board conducts various recruitment drives for different posts within the Indian Railways. The Junior Engineer position is one of the most sought-after roles, requiring specific technical qualifications and passing a multi-stage selection process. The Computer Based Test is the first stage of this process.

In recent years, RRB has moved towards digital platforms for exam administration and result processing. The use of portals like rrb.digialm.com streamlines the distribution of exam materials and answer keys. This digital approach aims to reduce delays and improve the accuracy of the recruitment pipeline.

Previous recruitment cycles have seen similar patterns where provisional keys are released shortly after the exam. Candidates are advised to review the key carefully. The board typically evaluates objections based on the validity of the arguments presented. If an objection is accepted, the answer key may be revised, and the scores of all candidates may be adjusted accordingly.

What happens next

After the objection window closes on November 17, the RRB will review all submitted queries. This process may take several weeks, depending on the volume of objections. Once the review is complete, the board will publish the final answer key. Any changes made during this phase will be reflected in the final scorecards.

Following the finalization of the answer key, the RRB will proceed to declare the CBT 1 results. Candidates who clear the cutoff will be shortlisted for the next stage of the selection process, which typically includes a skill test or interview. Applicants should monitor the official website for updates regarding the result release date.
