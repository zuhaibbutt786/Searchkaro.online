Saleem Sheikh’s daughter Seleena marries Ibrahim Sheikh in lavish ceremony

Latest developments

Karachi witnessed a high‑profile wedding this week as Seleena Sheikh, the only daughter of veteran television presenter Saleem Sheikh, married businessman Ibrahim Sheikh. The union was marked by three distinct events – the Mayoun ceremony, a Sangeet night and the Nikkah – held at a luxury hotel in the city's Clifton area. Family members, close friends and a number of celebrities from the Pakistani media circuit were present, adhering to the customary practice of keeping the guest list intimate yet notable.

The Mayoun ceremony, traditionally held a day before the main wedding, featured a gathering of women from both families who applied a mixture of turmeric, sandalwood and rose water to the bride and groom. Seleena was seen wearing a pastel‑hued, embroidered lehenga that highlighted her graceful silhouette, while Ibrahim opted for a classic sherwani with intricate zari work. Photographs released by the families showed the couple surrounded by close relatives, with Saleem Sheikh offering a heartfelt blessing.

The following evening, the Sangeet celebration turned the venue into a vibrant stage for music and dance. A live band performed a blend of contemporary pop and classic qawwali, and several guests participated in choreographed performances. Media reports noted that the bride’s mother, Shazia Sheikh, led a dance number that received applause from attendees. The event concluded with a lavish dinner featuring a menu of traditional Pakistani dishes and international cuisine, underscoring the family's penchant for blending cultural heritage with modern tastes.

The Nikkah, the formal Islamic marriage contract, took place the next day in a decorated hall within the same hotel. An Imam officiated the ceremony, and the couple exchanged vows in the presence of witnesses. Saleem Sheikh delivered a short speech, expressing pride in his daughter’s choice and wishing the couple a prosperous future. The ceremony concluded with a traditional reception where guests exchanged gifts and congratulatory messages. The entire series of events was covered extensively by local entertainment outlets, with video clips trending on social media platforms.

Over two decades, he built a reputation for his engaging interview style and became a familiar face on Pakistani entertainment channels. His personal life has remained relatively private, with occasional mentions of his family in interviews. Seleena, his only child, pursued a degree in business administration and has been involved in philanthropic activities alongside her father.

Ibrahim Sheikh, the groom, is a Karachi‑based entrepreneur involved in the textile export sector. He has maintained a low public profile, with most references to him appearing in business directories and social media posts. The families reportedly met through mutual acquaintances in the entertainment and business circles, and the engagement was announced quietly a few months before the wedding.

In recent years, Pakistani celebrity weddings have attracted significant media attention, often becoming cultural touchstones that set fashion and event trends.
