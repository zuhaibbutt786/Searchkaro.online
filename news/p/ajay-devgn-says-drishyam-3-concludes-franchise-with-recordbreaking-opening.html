Ajay Devgn says Drishyam 3 concludes franchise with record‑breaking opening

The third instalment of the Drishyam series has opened to critical praise and strong ticket sales. Ajay Devgn announced it will be the franchise's final chapter.

Latest developments

The Indian mystery thriller "Drishyam: The Conclusion" opened across Pakistan on Thursday, drawing more than 1.1 million tickets within its first weekend, according to Gulf News. Box‑office analysts project an opening collection of around Rs500 million, a figure that places the film among the highest‑grossing releases of the year in the region. Early reports from multiplexes in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad indicate near‑full occupancy, especially for evening shows.

Critical response has been equally positive. A live‑blog from The Times of India recorded the first wave of reviews, with several critics describing the film as "brilliant" and praising the performances of Ajay Devgn, Tabu and Shriya Saran. Reviewers highlighted the tight screenplay and the way the narrative resolves lingering questions from the previous two films while delivering fresh twists.

In an exclusive interview with Variety, Ajay Devgn said the film surpasses its predecessors in terms of narrative complexity and emotional weight. He added that "Drishyam: The Conclusion" will serve as the definitive end to the series, confirming that no further sequels are planned. The actor emphasized that the decision was driven by a desire to close the story on a high note rather than risk diluting the brand.

The film’s marketing team reported that social media engagement in Pakistan has surged, with the hashtag #Drishyam3 trending on Twitter and Instagram for several days before release. Promotional events in major cities, including a press conference in Lahore attended by the cast, contributed to heightened public interest. Distributors have announced plans to extend the theatrical run into the second week, contingent on sustained audience turnout.

Why it matters

The success of "Drishyam: The Conclusion" reflects the growing appetite for Indian thriller cinema among Pakistani audiences. The film’s strong opening demonstrates that cross‑border cultural products can thrive despite political sensitivities, offering a shared entertainment experience that transcends national boundaries.

For local exhibitors, the film provides a much‑needed boost to box‑office revenues after a period of reduced footfall caused by competing streaming releases. The high occupancy rates have encouraged cinema chains to schedule additional screenings, creating employment opportunities for staff and ancillary service providers. Moreover, the film’s performance may influence future acquisition decisions, prompting distributors to prioritize high‑profile Indian titles for Pakistani release windows.

Background

The Drishyam franchise began in 2015 with a Hindi remake of a Malayalam hit, starring Ajay Devgn as a protective father who engineers a cover‑up to shield his family. The first film was praised for its clever plotting and strong performances, leading to a sequel, "Drishyam 2", in 2022, which expanded the story and deepened character arcs.

Both earlier films enjoyed commercial success across South Asia, establishing a loyal fan base. The narrative premise—a seemingly ordinary family caught in extraordinary circumstances—has resonated with audiences seeking suspenseful yet emotionally grounded storytelling. The third instalment, directed by the original team, was billed as the final chapter, promising to resolve lingering plot threads while introducing new challenges for the protagonists.

What happens next

Industry observers expect the film to maintain its momentum through the first two weeks, after which box‑office performance will be measured against other regional releases. If ticket sales remain strong, distributors may negotiate extended runs in secondary cities and consider limited releases in neighboring markets such as Bangladesh and Afghanistan. The conclusion of the franchise also opens space for the production house to explore new intellectual properties, potentially shifting focus to original thriller concepts.
