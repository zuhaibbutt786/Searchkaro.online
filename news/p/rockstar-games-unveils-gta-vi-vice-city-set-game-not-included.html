Rockstar Games unveils GTA VI Vice City set, game not included

Rockstar Games has announced a GTA VI Vice City collector set priced at $399.99, but the package does not include the game itself. The announcement has generated strong search interest, especially in Pakistan.

What is happening

Rockstar Games released details of a new GTA VI Vice City collector set on its official channels early this week. The company confirmed that the set will be sold for $399.99 and will be available for pre‑order while supplies last. The announcement was accompanied by high‑resolution images of the box design, which prominently features the iconic Vice City skyline in neon colours.

The collector set includes a range of physical items designed for fans of the franchise. Among the items are a 200‑page art book showcasing concept art and environment designs, a detailed map of the fictional Vice City, a set of enamel pins, and a 12‑inch statue of a classic character from the series. The packaging also contains a limited‑edition steel case that can be used to store the items securely.

Rockstar has stated that the set will be shipped from its distribution centers worldwide, with a limited quantity allocated to each region. The price of $399.99 places the set in the premium collector market, and the company has warned that the stock will be exhausted quickly. Pre‑order links have been posted on the Rockstar website and on major retail partners.

The community response has been mixed. Some fans appreciate the opportunity to own high‑quality memorabilia, while others express disappointment that the actual GTA VI game is not part of the bundle. Social media platforms in Pakistan show a surge of discussion, with many users sharing screenshots of the box art and debating whether to purchase the set without the game.

Why people are searching this now

The announcement coincides with a period of heightened anticipation for the next Grand Theft Auto title. Rumors about GTA VI have circulated for years, and any official communication from Rockstar tends to generate spikes in online searches. In Pakistan, the gaming community is particularly active on platforms such as YouTube and Discord, where fans discuss upcoming releases and collector items.

Additionally, the pricing and the exclusion of the game itself have raised questions about the value proposition of the set. Potential buyers are looking for clarification on whether the collector edition will be bundled with the game at a later date, or if it will remain a standalone merchandise product. These uncertainties drive traffic to search engines as users seek official statements, retailer policies, and community opinions.

Background and context

Rockstar Games has a history of releasing premium collector editions for its major franchises. Previous titles such as Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2 featured deluxe packages that included physical collectibles, soundtracks, and exclusive in‑game content. Those editions were typically sold alongside the standard game, offering fans a bundled experience.

The current GTA VI collector set breaks from that pattern by offering only physical memorabilia without the game. This approach mirrors a recent trend in the industry where publishers sell standalone merchandise to capitalize on brand loyalty while the core product remains under development. The decision may also reflect production timelines, as the game itself is still in a confidential stage and has not been assigned a release date.

What to know next

Rockstar has indicated that further details about the GTA VI launch will be shared in the coming months. Fans should monitor the official Rockstar website and verified social media accounts for announcements regarding the game's release window, pre‑order options, and any potential bundles that could include the collector set.

Retailers that have listed the collector set are expected to update their inventory status as orders are fulfilled. Consumers interested in the set should act quickly if they wish to secure a copy, but they should also be aware that the package does not contain the game. Keeping an eye on official communications will help avoid misunderstandings about what is included in the purchase.
