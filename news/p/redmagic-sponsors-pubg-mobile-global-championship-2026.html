RedMagic Sponsors PUBG Mobile Global Championship 2026

The 2026 PUBG Mobile Global Championship will bring together 32 teams from around the world to compete between December 1 and 13. RedMagic has secured the title of official gaming phone sponsor, highlighting the growing esports scene in Pak

Latest developments

The 2026 PUBG Mobile Global Championship (PMGC) is set to commence on December 1, with 32 teams from 16 countries advancing through regional qualifiers. The event will run until December 13, featuring a group stage followed by knockout rounds.

According to the official schedule released by PUBG Mobile, the group stage will be divided into eight groups of four teams each. Matches will be played on a rotating roster of six maps, including Verdansk, Erangel, and Miramar, to test teams' adaptability across different terrains.

Teams will compete in a best‑of‑three format during the group stage, with the top two from each group progressing to the playoffs. The tournament will be streamed live on the PUBG Mobile Esports channel and major platforms such as YouTube and Twitch.

RedMagic, a leading smartphone manufacturer, has announced it will serve as the official gaming phone sponsor for the championship. The partnership includes exclusive in‑game branding, a dedicated RedMagic arena, and a prize‑pool boost for the event.

Why it matters

For Pakistani players, the PMGC offers a chance to showcase talent on a global stage, as the country has produced several top‑ranked teams in recent years. The event also provides a platform for local sponsors to invest in esports infrastructure.

RedMagic's involvement signals a growing confidence in the esports market in Pakistan, where mobile gaming is a major pastime. The partnership could spur increased access to high‑performance gaming devices for aspiring players.

Moreover, the championship’s broadcast on local networks will bring international esports content to a wider Pakistani audience, potentially boosting viewership and encouraging grassroots participation.

Background

PUBG Mobile has been a dominant force in mobile esports since its launch in 2018, hosting annual global championships that attract millions of viewers worldwide. The 2025 edition concluded with a record prize pool of $1.5 million.

RedMagic, founded in 2014, has positioned itself as a premium gaming phone brand, sponsoring events such as the PUBG Mobile Invitational and collaborating with professional teams across Asia.

The 2026 PMGC builds on the momentum of previous tournaments, aiming to increase prize money, expand regional representation, and enhance viewer experience through improved streaming technology.

What happens next

The next phase involves finalizing the list of participating teams, which will be announced by the end of November. Organizers will also release detailed match schedules and map rotations ahead of the opening ceremony.

Players and coaches are expected to begin intensive training camps in early December, with a focus on mastering the rotating maps and refining team strategies for the high‑stakes knockout rounds.
