Latest developments

Aston Martin Aramco Formula 1 Team has officially announced that Fernando Alonso and Lance Stroll will continue to form the team's driver lineup for the 2027 season. The confirmation ends speculation regarding the Spaniard's long-term future in the sport, as he commits to another year with the Silver Arrows. This move signals a stable period for the team as they look to consolidate their position in the midfield and challenge for higher finishes.

In a statement accompanying the announcement, Alonso expressed his confidence in his current form, stating that he still feels as fast as anyone in the grid. The two-time world champion emphasized his desire to remain competitive at the highest level, noting that his physical and mental condition allow him to push the limits of the car just as effectively as his younger counterparts. His comments reflect a determination to prove that experience and skill can still yield top-tier results in modern Formula 1.

The contract extension also cements the partnership between Alonso and Lance Stroll, who will continue to represent the team. For Aston Martin, retaining both drivers provides continuity in development and strategy, allowing the engineering team to focus on long-term car evolution rather than adapting to new driver preferences. The announcement was made through official channels, including the team's website and major sports broadcasters, ensuring the news reached a wide audience of motorsport fans.

This decision comes at a crucial time for the team, as they aim to build on their recent progress. By locking in their driver lineup for 2027, Aston Martin can plan their technical regulations and resource allocation with greater certainty. The stability is expected to bolster the team's morale and attract further sponsorship interest, as brands often prefer long-term associations with established figures in the sport.
