Minister L. Murugan Resigns, Vacates Rajya Sabha Seat

Union Minister L. Murugan has stepped down and relinquished his Rajya Sabha membership. He sent a detailed letter to Prime Minister Narendra Modi explaining the decision.

Latest developments

On October 13, 2026, Union Minister L. Murugan announced his resignation from the cabinet and simultaneously surrendered his Rajya Sabha seat representing Tamil Nadu. The announcement was made through a press conference in New Delhi, where Murugan said he had submitted a five‑page letter to Prime Minister Narendra Modi outlining his reasons. The letter, described by officials as a "personal communication," referenced his desire to focus on regional issues and cited internal party dynamics.

In the letter, Murugan reportedly referenced his recent appearance on Prime Minister Modi's "Mann Ki Baat" broadcast, where he highlighted development projects in Tamil Nadu. He indicated that stepping down would allow a fresh face to take on the responsibilities of the ministry and the parliamentary seat. The resignation has been formally accepted by the Prime Minister's Office, and the Rajya Sabha Secretariat has begun the procedural steps to declare the seat vacant.

Party leaders across the country reacted swiftly. Senior BJP officials praised Murugan's decision as a "selfless act" aimed at strengthening the party's grassroots outreach in the southern state. The Prime Minister, in a brief statement, thanked Murugan for his service and assured that the party would nominate a capable successor. Tamil Nadu opposition parties, including the DMK, welcomed the development, suggesting it could open space for a more competitive electoral environment.

Analysts note that the timing of the resignation, just months before the 2029 general elections, may be strategic. The BJP has been seeking to improve its standing in Tamil Nadu, a state traditionally dominated by regional parties. By creating a vacancy, the party can introduce a candidate with broader appeal or negotiate alliances that could influence the upcoming electoral calculus.

Why it matters

The departure of a central minister from both the Union Cabinet and the Rajya Sabha alters the balance of power within the BJP's parliamentary team. Murugan's portfolio included key responsibilities for rural development and Panchayati Raj institutions, areas that are crucial for the party's outreach in agrarian regions of Tamil Nadu. His exit may lead to a reshuffle that could affect policy continuity and the implementation of ongoing projects.

For voters in Tamil Nadu, the resignation signals a possible shift in the party's candidate selection process. The BJP has struggled to secure a significant vote share in the state, and a new face could either rejuvenate its image or further fragment its support base. The move also invites scrutiny of internal party dynamics, as senior leaders assess whether the decision reflects broader disagreements over strategy or simply a personal choice by Murugan.

Background

L. Murugan, whose full name is Lakshmanan Murugan, entered national politics as a member of the Bharatiya Janata Party in the early 2010s. He was elected to the Rajya Sabha in 2018 and later appointed as a Union Minister of State for Rural Development and Panchayati Raj in 2022. Over the past four years, he has been credited with launching several infrastructure schemes in Tamil Nadu, including road upgrades and digital village initiatives.

Murugan's political rise coincided with the BJP's broader effort to expand its footprint in southern India. While the party has made inroads in neighboring states, Tamil Nadu remains a stronghold of regional parties such as the DMK and AIADMK. Murugan's role was often highlighted as a bridge between the central leadership and local constituencies, aiming to translate national policies into state-level benefits.

What happens next

The Rajya Sabha seat vacated by Murugan will be filled through a by‑election, as mandated by parliamentary rules. The BJP is expected to nominate a candidate with strong regional connections, possibly a senior state legislator or a technocrat with a clean public image. Simultaneously, the party will need to appoint a new minister to oversee the rural development portfolio, a decision likely to be announced within the next two weeks. These moves will be closely watched as indicators of the BJP's preparation for the 2029 general elections.
