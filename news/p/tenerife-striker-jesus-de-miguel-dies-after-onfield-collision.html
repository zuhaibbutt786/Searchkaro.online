Tenerife striker Jesús de Miguel dies after on‑field collision

Jesús de Miguel, a 30‑year‑old striker for Tenerife, died after a serious on‑field collision during a league match. The incident has sparked mourning among fans and raised questions about player safety.

Latest developments

Jesús de Miguel, a 30‑year‑old forward for CD Tenerife, died on Thursday after sustaining a severe head injury during a Segunda División match against a rival side. The collision occurred in the second half when de Miguel contested a high ball and collided head‑first with an opposing defender. Medical staff rushed him to the stadium's medical facility, and he was later transferred to a nearby hospital where he was pronounced dead. The match was suspended shortly after the incident, and the league announced a period of mourning. Club officials released a brief statement confirming the tragedy and expressing condolences to the player's family, teammates, and supporters. The Spanish Football Federation (RFEF) has opened an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the injury, and the match referee's report will be reviewed as part of the process.

Background

Jesús de Miguel began his senior career in the lower tiers of Spanish football before joining Tenerife in 2022. Known for his pace and finishing ability, he quickly became a regular starter and contributed to the club's push for promotion. The Segunda División, Spain's second tier, is known for its competitive intensity, with clubs often playing on tightly packed schedules. In recent years, governing bodies have introduced concussion protocols, but implementation varies across leagues. Previous incidents involving head injuries have prompted debates about the adequacy of protective gear and the speed of medical intervention during matches.

What happens next

The RFEF investigation will examine video footage, medical reports, and referee statements to determine whether existing safety measures were followed. Depending on the findings, the federation may recommend changes to concussion assessment procedures or introduce mandatory protective headgear for certain positions. Tenerife will hold a memorial service for de Miguel, and the club has pledged financial support to his family.
