Background and context

Nikki Bella, born Stephanie Nicole Garcia, debuted in WWE in 2006 as part of the Bella Twins with her sister Brie. Over the years, she captured the WWE Divas Championship and later the SmackDown Women's Championship, establishing herself as a key figure in the women's division. Her career has been marked by both in‑ring success and television appearances that broadened her appeal.

Paige, whose real name is Saraya‑Jane Bevis, entered WWE in 2012 and quickly rose through the ranks. She became the first NXT Women's Champion and later held the Raw and SmackDown Women's titles. The two have intersected on multiple occasions, with their most notable interaction occurring during a backstage segment that hinted at a future conflict.

The New Day, formed in 2014, has been one of WWE's most popular tag teams. Their camaraderie with Nikki Bella stems from backstage friendships and occasional joint appearances on shows like "Total Divas." Their involvement in the current storyline reflects WWE's tendency to weave established alliances into new narratives.

The storyline also taps into the nostalgia of fans who followed the original Bella Twins era.
