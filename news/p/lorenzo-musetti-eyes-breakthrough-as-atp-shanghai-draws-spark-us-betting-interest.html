Lorenzo Musetti eyes breakthrough as ATP Shanghai draws spark US betting interest

Lorenzo Musetti steps onto the Shanghai stage amid growing US interest. Analysts and bettors weigh his chances as the tournament kicks off.

Latest developments

The ATP Shanghai Open opened on Monday with a slate of first‑round matches that have drawn considerable attention from analysts and bettors in the United States. Among the highlighted pairings were Holger Rune versus Daniel Altmaier and a showdown between Fery and Marin Cilic, as noted by Last Word on Sports and The Grandstand. Italian prodigy Lorenzo Musetti, ranked inside the top 30, was also listed in the day‑one draw, though his opponent was not named in the preview articles. The same day, a Robinhood prediction market opened for a separate round‑of‑64 match between Dalibor Svrcina and Tomas Martin Etcheverry, illustrating the broader appetite for tennis wagering. Sports commentators have pointed to Musetti’s recent form – a series of deep runs on clay and hard courts – as a factor that could make his Shanghai debut a focal point for US bettors. While the official tournament schedule has not released detailed odds for Musetti, betting platforms have already posted early lines, reflecting a cautious optimism about his ability to navigate the opening round against a lower‑ranked opponent.

The surge in US interest around Musetti’s Shanghai appearance underscores a growing appetite for international tennis beyond the traditional Grand Slam events. American fans, who have historically gravitated toward home‑grown stars, are now tracking a broader pool of talent, driven in part by the accessibility of online prediction markets. The Robinhood market for the Svrcina‑Etcheverry match, for example, shows how casual observers are turning to data‑driven platforms to gauge outcomes. Musetti’s stylish one‑handed backhand and his reputation for inventive shot‑making appeal to a US audience that values flair and competitiveness. Moreover, his potential success could influence future broadcast rights negotiations, as networks seek to showcase emerging European players to diversify their tennis programming.

Background

Lorenzo Musetti burst onto the professional scene in 2020, quickly earning a reputation for his elegant one‑handed backhand and aggressive baseline play. By the end of 2025, he had broken into the top 30 of the ATP rankings, highlighted by a semifinal run at the Monte Carlo Masters and a quarterfinal appearance at the Paris Masters. Musetti’s 2024 season saw him claim his first ATP title on clay in Barcelona, reinforcing his status as a versatile surface player. His rise has been supported by a strong junior record, including a 2019 French Open junior title. The Italian’s recent performances on hard courts have been solid, with multiple top‑10 wins that have boosted his confidence heading into the Asian swing of the tour.

What happens next

Musetti’s next challenge in Shanghai will likely come against a qualifier or a lower‑ranked main‑draw entrant, a match that analysts expect him to win comfortably. A victory would set up a potential second‑round clash with a higher seed, possibly Holger Rune if the draw aligns, creating a marquee encounter that could attract further US betting activity. The tournament’s progression will also feed into the upcoming Asian hard‑court series, where Musetti could solidify his ranking points ahead of the year‑end championships. Observers will watch the betting lines closely, as early odds may shift dramatically based on his performance in the opening round.
