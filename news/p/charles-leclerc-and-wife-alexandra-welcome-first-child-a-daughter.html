Charles Leclerc and wife Alexandra welcome first child, a daughter

Monegasque Formula One driver Charles Leclerc and his wife Alexandra have welcomed their first child, a baby girl. The couple shared the news with fans worldwide, highlighting a personal milestone amid the racing season.

Latest developments

Charles Leclerc, the 2026 Formula One race winner for Scuderia Ferrari, confirmed on 4 November that his wife Alexandra gave birth to a daughter earlier this week. The announcement was made through a brief statement posted on the team's official website and shared on Leclerc's social media accounts, where a photo of the newborn’s tiny hand was shown alongside a thank‑you note to supporters.

Ferrari team principal Fred Vasseur praised the new addition to the Leclerc family, noting that the driver’s focus and determination have always been driven by personal ambition and strong family values. The team’s social media channels quickly amplified the news, generating thousands of congratulatory messages from fans across Europe, the Middle East and India, where interest in the driver has surged in recent months.

Leclerc, who finished third in the recent Singapore Grand Prix, is now preparing for the season‑ending race in Japan. While he has not indicated any intention to miss practice sessions, the team has confirmed that he will receive full support to balance his new parental responsibilities with his racing commitments.

Alexandra Leclerc, a former model and entrepreneur, has kept a low public profile since the couple married in 2023. The birth of their daughter marks the first child for the pair, and sources close to the family say the couple is eager to keep their private life shielded from the intense media scrutiny that often accompanies high‑profile athletes.

His marriage to Alexandra in 2023 was a low‑key ceremony attended by close friends and a few team members, reflecting the couple’s desire for privacy.
