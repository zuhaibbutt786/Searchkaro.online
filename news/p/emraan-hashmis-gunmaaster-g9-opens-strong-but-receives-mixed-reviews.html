Emraan Hashmi's 'Gunmaaster G9' Opens Strong but Receives Mixed Reviews

Emraan Hashmi and Genelia Deshmukh's new thriller Gunmaaster G9 debuted with a Rs 4.30 crore opening. Critics are split, with some applauding the action turn and others calling the film daft.

Latest developments

Emraan Hashmi and Genelia Deshmukh's new Hindi thriller Gunmaaster G9 opened on Friday across more than 2,500 screens in India. The film recorded a day‑one net collection of roughly Rs 4.30 crore, according to box‑office data released by the Times of India, and achieved an occupancy rate of 12.85 per cent. Trade analysts described the opening as modest for a mid‑budget action title, noting that the film’s release coincided with a crowded weekend slate. Critical response has been divided. The Hindu’s review highlighted Hashmi’s shift to a more pulp‑style action hero, stating that the performance “hits more than it loses” and that the film delivers the kind of high‑octane spectacle expected from its genre. In contrast, Scroll.in labelled the movie “daft” and argued that it “misses its target by miles”, criticizing the script and visual effects for lacking coherence. The mixed reception is reflected in early audience feedback on social media, where viewers praised the chemistry between Hashmi and Deshmukh but questioned the narrative logic.

Why it matters

The performance of Gunmaaster G9 matters for several reasons. First, Emraan Hashmi has been rebuilding his career after a series of lukewarm releases, and a solid box‑office start could reaffirm his viability as a bankable star in action‑driven projects. Second, the film’s earnings provide a barometer for the health of mid‑budget productions in a market increasingly dominated by big‑budget franchises and streaming releases. A Rs 4.30 crore opening suggests that there is still appetite for theatrical experiences that blend familiar star power with genre conventions. Third, the divergent critical opinions underline a broader debate within the Indian film industry about the balance between style and substance. While some viewers enjoy the unapologetic pulp aesthetic, others demand tighter storytelling, a tension that could shape future green‑lighting decisions for similar projects.

Background

Gunmaaster G9 is directed by Himesh Reshammiya, who also composed the film’s soundtrack. The project pairs Hashmi with Genelia Deshmukh, marking their first collaboration in a full‑length feature. The script, written by Reshammiya and co‑writer Aparshakti Khurana, positions Hashmi as a hardened mercenary tasked with retrieving a stolen artifact, a premise that allows for elaborate set pieces and stunt work. The film’s title, a stylised spelling of “Gunmaster”, signals its emphasis on gun‑fueled action, a departure from Hashmi’s earlier reputation as a romantic thriller lead. Production began in early 2023, with principal photography taking place in Mumbai and parts of Gujarat. The movie’s marketing campaign leaned heavily on action trailers and social media teasers that showcased high‑energy fight sequences.

What happens next

Industry watchers will monitor the film’s performance over the next weekend to see if word‑of‑mouth can boost attendance. If the box‑office trajectory remains steady, Gunmaaster G9 could cross the Rs 20 crore mark within two weeks, a benchmark that often determines a film’s profitability. The producers have also hinted at a possible digital release on a major streaming platform after the theatrical window, which could extend the film’s revenue stream.
