Halys Faces Sonego in Stockholm Open First Round

French qualifier Quentin Halys will face Italian Lorenzo Sonego in the first round of the Stockholm Open. Analysts predict a tight contest as Halys seeks to advance on the ATP tour.

The Stockholm Open, part of the ATP 250 series, has drawn attention this year as French qualifier Quentin Halys prepares to face Italian veteran Lorenzo Sonego in the first round. According to the latest betting outlooks, Sonego holds a slight advantage, with odds reflecting his experience on hard courts and a higher ranking. Halys, meanwhile, has shown a steady rise in recent tournaments, earning a spot in the main draw after a strong performance in the qualifiers.

Background

Quentin Halys, born in 1998, has steadily climbed the ATP rankings, reaching a career-high of 85 earlier this year. His breakthrough came after a series of solid performances in Challenger events, where he secured multiple titles.

Lorenzo Sonego, a 1994-born Italian, has been a mainstay on the ATP circuit for several years, with a career-high ranking of 27. He is known for his consistent play and has reached the quarterfinals of several ATP 250 tournaments.

Halys’s journey from the Challenger circuit to the ATP main draw illustrates the opportunities available to players who combine skill with determination. Sonego’s experience, on the other hand, brings a seasoned perspective that often proves decisive in tight matches.
