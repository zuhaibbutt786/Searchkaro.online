Ghana eyes AFCON redemption as they face Somalia in qualifiers

Ghana's national team is gearing up for a decisive AFCON qualifier against Somalia. The outcome could determine their chances of advancing to the final tournament.

Latest developments

The Ghana Football Association has released a statement ahead of the upcoming qualifier, emphasising the need for a clean sheet and a win against Somalia. Head coach Graham Arnold has called for a disciplined approach, noting that the team must secure six points from the remaining matches to qualify for the African Cup of Nations.

In a bid to strengthen the squad, Ghana has recalled striker Oumar Kudus, who has been performing well in the Premier League. Kudus' return is expected to add a new dimension to the attack, providing the Black Stars with a fresh goal threat.

Somalia, a lower‑ranked side in the CAF standings, will look to use the home advantage and the element of surprise to upset the favourites. The team has been working on a compact defensive structure, hoping to frustrate Ghana's midfield creativity.

The match is scheduled for 20 October 2024 at the Accra Sports Stadium, with Ghana hosting the game. The venue has hosted several high‑profile fixtures in recent years, and the local crowd is expected to create a lively atmosphere.

Ghana's recent form has been mixed, with a draw against Nigeria and a narrow loss to Ivory Coast in friendly matches. The national side is under pressure to turn its fortunes around before the qualifiers conclude.

Why it matters

For football fans across India, the Ghana vs Somalia qualifier offers a glimpse into the competitive nature of African football. The match showcases the tactical evolution of the game and highlights the importance of squad depth in international tournaments.

The outcome of this fixture could influence the dynamics of the CAF qualification group, affecting teams such as Egypt, Senegal, and Morocco. Indian viewers following the CAF calendar may find the result relevant for predictions about the final tournament lineup.

Background

Ghana has a storied history in the African Cup of Nations, having won the tournament four times and reaching the final on several occasions. The national team, known as the Black Stars, has consistently been a strong contender in CAF competitions.

Somalia, on the other hand, has yet to make a significant impact on the continental stage. Their last appearance in a major African tournament was in 2008, and they have struggled to secure a spot in recent qualifiers.

What happens next

In the coming days, the Ghanaian coaching staff will conduct intensive training sessions focused on defensive solidity and counter‑attack efficiency. The team will also participate in a friendly against a regional side to fine‑tune their tactics.

Should Ghana secure a win, they will move closer to qualification, while a draw or loss would leave their fate uncertain. The match will be broadcast live across Africa and streamed internationally, allowing fans worldwide to follow the action.
