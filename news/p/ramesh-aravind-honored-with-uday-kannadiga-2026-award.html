Ramesh Aravind Honored with Uday Kannadiga 2026 Award

Kannada film veteran Ramesh Aravind has been selected for the prestigious Uday Kannadiga 2026 award. The ceremony, honoring achievers from the region, is scheduled to air on Sun Uday TV in late October and early November.

Latest developments

The award ceremony, organized by Sun Uday TV, is scheduled to be telecast on October 31 and November 1, 2026.
