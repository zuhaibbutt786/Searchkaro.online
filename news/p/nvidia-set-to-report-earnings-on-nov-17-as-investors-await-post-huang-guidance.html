Nvidia Set to Report Earnings on Nov. 17 as Investors Await Post-Huang Guidance

Nvidia’s earnings call on Nov. 17 will be the first under new leadership. Analysts and investors are watching closely for guidance on AI demand and revenue growth.

Latest developments

Nvidia has confirmed that its earnings will be released on November 17, marking the first report since CEO Jensen Huang stepped down. The company will be led by Colette Kress, who has been promoted to chief executive following Huang’s resignation earlier this year. The earnings call is expected to provide a detailed view of the company’s performance across its gaming, data‑center and automotive segments.

Market watchers anticipate that Nvidia will report revenue that continues to outpace analyst expectations, driven by strong demand for its graphics processing units (GPUs) in artificial‑intelligence workloads. The company’s guidance for the next quarter is likely to reflect the current slowdown in the broader AI market, which has seen a pullback after a period of rapid growth.

The stock market has reacted to the announcement with a mixed response. While Nvidia’s shares have remained resilient, the broader AI‑related sector has experienced a sell‑off, prompting investors to seek clarity on the company’s future earnings trajectory. Analysts note that the earnings call will be a key indicator of how Nvidia plans to navigate this shift.

Financial analysts are also watching the company’s cash flow and capital allocation strategy. With a significant cash reserve, Nvidia has the flexibility to invest in research and development or pursue strategic acquisitions. The upcoming earnings report will shed light on whether the company will continue to focus on GPU innovation or diversify further into other AI‑related products.

Why it matters

For investors, the earnings call represents a critical moment to assess Nvidia’s ability to sustain its growth in a market that is becoming increasingly competitive. The company’s performance will influence the valuation of AI‑related stocks and could impact the broader technology sector.

The AI industry relies heavily on Nvidia’s GPUs for training and inference tasks. A slowdown in demand could ripple through the supply chain, affecting suppliers, developers and end‑users of AI applications. As a result, the earnings report will be closely monitored by stakeholders across the technology ecosystem.

Additionally, the outcome of the earnings call may affect the company’s dividend policy and share repurchase plans. Investors who hold Nvidia shares or consider adding them to their portfolios will be looking for signals that the company can maintain profitability and return value to shareholders.

Background

Nvidia, founded in 1993, has grown from a niche graphics card manufacturer to a dominant player in AI computing. Under the leadership of Jensen Huang, the company expanded its product line to include GPUs for data centers, autonomous vehicles and gaming consoles.

Huang’s resignation in early 2026 came after a series of high‑profile product launches and a record‑breaking revenue year. His departure has prompted speculation about the company’s strategic direction and leadership style.

Colette Kress, who has served as chief operating officer, was chosen to guide Nvidia through the transition. She has overseen operations for several years and is expected to maintain the company’s focus on innovation while addressing shareholder concerns.

What happens next

Following the earnings announcement, Nvidia is expected to release a detailed earnings presentation, including financial statements, guidance for the upcoming fiscal year and a Q&A session with analysts. The company may also discuss upcoming product releases and strategic initiatives.

The market will likely react to the earnings data within the trading day, with potential adjustments to Nvidia’s share price and the valuation of AI‑related indices. Analysts will update their forecasts based on the company’s performance and any new guidance provided.
