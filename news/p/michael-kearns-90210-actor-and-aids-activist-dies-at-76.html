Michael Kearns, 90210 actor and AIDS activist, dies at 76

Michael Kearns, a pioneering gay actor and AIDS activist, has died at the age of 76. His passing comes six days before he was scheduled to return to the Celebration Theatre.

Kearns was widely recognized for his role in the popular television series 90210, which brought him significant public recognition in the early 1990s.
