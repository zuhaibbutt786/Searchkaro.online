Background

Dragon Ball is a manga series created by Akira Toriyama, first published in the 1980s. The story follows the adventures of Son Goku and his friends as they train and battle various opponents. The franchise has expanded to include multiple anime series, movies, and video games. Dragon Ball Super is the direct sequel to the original Dragon Ball Z series.

The character Beerus, known as the God of Destruction, was introduced in Dragon Ball Z: Battle of Gods. He played a central role in the subsequent anime series, Dragon Ball Super. The new anime adaptation focuses on stories involving Beerus, providing a deeper look into his character and his interactions with other key figures in the series. This focus allows for a more detailed exploration of the lore surrounding the gods of destruction.
