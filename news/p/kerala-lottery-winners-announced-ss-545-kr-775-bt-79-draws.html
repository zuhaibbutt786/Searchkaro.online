Kerala Lottery Winners Announced: SS-545, KR-775, BT-79 Draws

Kerala’s lottery draws have produced a ₹1 crore first prize in the SS-545 draw, while the KR-775 and BT-79 draws also announced winners. The results are now available online.

Latest developments

On 10 November 2026, the SS-545 draw of the Sthree Sakthi lottery in Kerala announced a first‑prize winner who received ₹1 crore. The winning ticket number was SV 182817. The draw was held at the Kerala State Lottery Office and the results were posted on the official website and local news outlets. Earlier in the month, the weekly Karunya KR-775 draw on Saturday, 7 November, also released its list of winners. The first prize of ₹1 crore was awarded to a ticket holder whose number matched the winning combination. The second and third prizes were ₹25 lakh and ₹10 lakh respectively, as per the standard prize structure. The most recent draw, Bhagyathara BT‑79, took place on 9 November 2026. The winning numbers were published by News18 and other media. While the first prize for this draw was not as large as the SS‑545 prize, the draw still attracted a large number of participants across Kerala. All three draws followed the same verification process, with winners required to present their ticket and identification at the lottery office to claim their prizes. The Kerala State Lottery Department has confirmed that the results are final and that any disputes will be handled through the established grievance mechanism.

Background

Kerala’s state lottery has operated since the 1990s, offering a range of draws such as the Sthree Sakthi, Karunya and Bhagyathara series. Each series targets different segments of the population, with Sthree Sakthi focusing on women’s participation. The SS‑545 draw is part of the Sthree Sakthi series, which is known for its high prize amounts and strict regulatory oversight. The numbering system for tickets follows a standardized format that ensures fairness. The Kerala State Lottery Department publishes results online and through local media to ensure accessibility. The department also provides guidelines on how winners can claim their prizes, including required documentation and timelines.

What happens next

Winners of the SS‑545, KR‑775 and BT‑79 draws will need to visit the Kerala State Lottery Office within the stipulated period to collect their prizes. The department has set a 30‑day window for claim submissions, after which unclaimed prizes will be returned to the state fund.
