Granblue Fantasy Relink Leaves PS Plus Catalog in December

Latest developments

PlayStation has confirmed that Granblue Fantasy Relink will be removed from the PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium catalogs in December 2026. This announcement aligns with a broader list of titles scheduled to exit the service during the same month. The removal means that new subscribers will no longer be able to claim the game as part of their monthly benefits once the date passes.

According to reports from PlayStation Lifestyle and other gaming outlets, this move is part of Sony’s standard rotation strategy for its subscription library. The game, which offers a substantial role-playing experience, is being replaced by new titles entering the catalog. Players who already own the game through the PS Plus service are advised to download it before the removal date to retain access. If the game is not downloaded prior to the cutoff, it will be removed from the user's library.

This update follows a trend of significant titles leaving the service in recent months. For instance, Rockstar Games’ major titles, including GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, have also faced removal from the platform. The consistent rotation ensures that the library remains fresh, though it often causes concern among subscribers who wish to keep specific games in their collections.

Why it matters

For US-based PlayStation Plus subscribers, this news is critical for managing their digital game libraries. Granblue Fantasy Relink is a high-profile RPG that requires a significant time investment, often exceeding 100 hours for a full playthrough. If players have not yet started or completed the game, the impending removal creates a sense of urgency. Missing the download window means losing access to the title unless they purchase it separately from the PlayStation Store.

The financial impact for consumers can be notable. While the game is free for active subscribers during its availability, purchasing it later could cost tens of dollars. Therefore, the decision to download the game before December 2026 is a cost-saving measure for many players. This pattern of removals highlights the transient nature of subscription-based gaming, where ownership is conditional on the service provider’s catalog decisions.

Background

Granblue Fantasy Relink is a modernized remake of the popular mobile game Granblue Fantasy. Developed by Cygames and published by Square Enix, the title was well-received for its action-oriented combat and vibrant visuals. Its inclusion in the PlayStation Plus catalog was a significant draw for fans of Japanese RPGs who wanted to experience the story on a home console without an upfront purchase.

PlayStation Plus operates on a tiered subscription model, with the Extra and Premium tiers offering access to a large library of games. Sony regularly rotates this library, removing older or less-played titles to make room for new releases. This practice has been a point of contention among gamers, who often feel that the constant changes make it difficult to build a permanent collection through the subscription service alone.

What happens next

Subscribers should check their PlayStation Plus library immediately to see if Granblue Fantasy Relink is available for download. If it is, they should initiate the download process well before the end of December 2026 to avoid any last-minute technical issues or server congestion. Once downloaded, the game will remain in the user's library even after it is removed from the service, provided they maintain an active PlayStation Plus subscription.

Sony is expected to announce the new titles entering the catalog in December 2026 in the coming weeks. These additions will likely include recent releases and highly anticipated games, aiming to offset the loss of popular titles like Granblue Fantasy Relink. Gamers are encouraged to stay updated with official PlayStation communications to track these changes.
