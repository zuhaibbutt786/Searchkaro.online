Pakistan A edge Nepal in Asia Cup Rising Stars clash at Doha

Pakistan A clinched a 96‑run victory over Nepal in the Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament in Doha, thanks to Rohail Nazir’s unbeaten half‑century. The win kept the Shaheens on track in Group B.

Latest developments

In the 11th match of Group B of the Asia Cup Rising Stars 50‑over tournament, Pakistan A, playing under the Shaheens banner, overcame Nepal by a margin of 96 runs at the Doha International Cricket Stadium on 24 November 2026. The encounter, classified as a day‑night fixture, saw Pakistan post a total of 168 runs, a score that at one stage appeared vulnerable as Nepal mounted a late surge. Rohail Nazir anchored the Pakistani innings with an unbeaten half‑century, scoring 57 runs off 68 balls. His measured approach, combined with disciplined stroke play, allowed the side to recover from early wickets and set a defendable target. The half‑century remained unbeaten as the innings concluded, providing a steady platform for the lower order to add crucial runs. Nepal’s chase began steadily, reaching 120 for 3 with 30 runs required from the final ten overs. However, disciplined bowling from Pakistan’s seamers, particularly the spell of Mohammad Aslam who claimed two wickets for 22 runs, stemmed the flow of runs. Nepal fell short, finishing on 72 all out, leaving Pakistan with a comfortable 96‑run victory. The win marked Pakistan A’s third consecutive success in the group stage, reinforcing their position as one of the favourites to progress to the knockout phase. The performance also highlighted the depth of emerging talent in Pakistan’s cricket pipeline, with several young players contributing both with bat and ball. Key contributions for Nepal came from batsman Sagar Thapa, who top‑scored with 42 runs, and all‑rounder Prakash Gurung, who added 28 runs before being dismissed in the penultimate over. On the Pakistani side, spinner Ahmed Raza claimed three wickets for 31 runs, proving effective on the Doha pitch that offered turn in the evening session.

Why it matters

Cricket enjoys massive popularity across Pakistan, and the Rising Stars tournament offers a platform for upcoming players to showcase their abilities on an international stage. A victory against Nepal not only boosts the confidence of the squad but also signals to selectors that the current pool of talent is ready to step up for senior national duties. For Nepal, the match underscored the challenges of competing against more experienced associate sides. The narrow escape in the middle overs demonstrated progress, yet the inability to chase a modest total highlighted areas needing improvement, such as death‑over batting and handling pressure in day‑night conditions.
