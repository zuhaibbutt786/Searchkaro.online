Tata Aeris sedan launches in India with price under 6 lakh

Tata Motors has introduced the Aeris, a sub‑4‑metre sedan priced at Rs 5.29 lakh. The model replaces the Tigor and brings updated design and equipment to the entry‑level segment.

What is happening

Tata Motors officially launched the Aeris compact sedan in India on 15 September 2026. The new model is positioned below the Nexon and replaces the outgoing Tigor in the company's lineup. It is offered with a single‑engine configuration, a 1.2‑litre Revotron petrol unit paired with a manual gearbox, and is priced from Rs 5.29 lakh, making it one of the most affordable four‑door cars in the market.

The Aeris features a refreshed exterior that includes a revised front grille, slimmer LED headlamps, and a more aerodynamic roofline. Inside, the cabin receives a redesigned dashboard with a 7‑inch touchscreen infotainment system, dual‑zone climate control, and a set of safety features such as dual front airbags, ABS with EBD, and rear parking sensors. Tata Motors has highlighted the vehicle's compliance with the Indian government's sub‑4‑metre tax incentives, which keep the on‑road price competitive.

Production of the Aeris is taking place at Tata's Pune plant, where the company has allocated a dedicated assembly line to meet expected demand. Early reports suggest that the launch will be supported by a series of promotional offers, including zero‑down financing and extended warranty packages for the first batch of buyers.

The introduction of the Aeris aligns with Tata Motors' broader strategy to expand its portfolio of affordable passenger vehicles. By targeting first‑time car owners and small families, the automaker aims to capture a larger share of the price‑sensitive segment that continues to grow in urban and semi‑urban areas across the country.

Why people are searching this now

The announcement of the Aeris coincides with a period of heightened interest in low‑cost personal mobility solutions. Rising fuel prices and tightening household budgets have prompted many consumers to explore alternatives to two‑wheelers and older, less efficient cars.

Background and context

Tata Motors entered the compact sedan market with the Tigor in 2017, positioning it as a budget-friendly alternative to larger family cars. Over the past nine years, the Tigor underwent several facelifts but faced stiff competition from newer models that offered more modern technology and design.

The Indian automotive sector has been shaped by government policies that favor vehicles under four metres in length, granting tax benefits that lower the overall cost to consumers. Manufacturers have responded by developing a range of sub‑4‑metre cars that balance affordability with acceptable levels of comfort and safety.

In recent years, Tata Motors has emphasized a shift toward electrification and premium segments, launching models such as the Nexon EV and the Harrier. The Aeris represents a renewed focus on the entry‑level market, ensuring the brand maintains a presence across all price points.

Industry analysts note that the compact sedan segment remains resilient despite a global trend toward SUVs and crossovers. The combination of lower ownership costs, easier maneuverability in congested city streets, and the continued appeal of a traditional trunk space keep demand steady.

What to know next

Potential buyers should monitor Tata Motors' official website and authorized dealerships for the final specification sheet, which will confirm the exact list of standard and optional equipment. Availability is expected to roll out in major metropolitan areas first, followed by tier‑2 and tier‑3 cities.

In the weeks following the launch, Tata Motors plans to release detailed fuel‑efficiency figures and real‑world mileage data. Consumers are advised to compare these numbers with those of competing models before making a purchase decision, especially if long‑distance travel is a regular requirement.
