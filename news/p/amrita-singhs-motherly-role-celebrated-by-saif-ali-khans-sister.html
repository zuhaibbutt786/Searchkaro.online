Amrita Singh’s Motherly Role Celebrated by Saif Ali Khan’s Sister

Saba Ali Khan, sister of actor Saif Ali Khan, spoke about Amrita Singh’s caring influence after their father's passing. The actress is being remembered for her supportive, mother‑like presence within the family.

Latest developments

In an IWMBuzz feature dated 7 November 2026, Saba described the final moments of her father’s life and how Amrita, then a close family friend, stepped in to provide emotional steadiness. She recounted that Amrita stayed by the bedside, offered comforting words, and helped coordinate family arrangements, actions that left a lasting impression on Saba and reinforced a bond that extended beyond the usual celebrity acquaintances.
