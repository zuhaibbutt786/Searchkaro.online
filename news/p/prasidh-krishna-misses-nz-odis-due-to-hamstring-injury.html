Latest developments

India’s cricket management has confirmed that Prasidh Krishna will not feature in the One Day International series against New Zealand. The decision comes as the pacer continues his recovery from a hamstring injury sustained recently. This absence leaves India without a key left-arm seamer for the limited-overs encounters scheduled in the coming days.

According to reports from Cricinfo and the ICC, Krishna is currently undergoing rehabilitation and is not yet fit enough to handle the physical demands of international ODI cricket. The medical team has advised against rushing his return to prevent further complications. His omission from the ODI squad has been officially communicated to the selectors and the team staff.

Despite missing the ODI series, Krishna is not entirely out of the picture for the ongoing tour. Cricbuzz reports that he is expected to join the India A squad for a third unofficial Test match. However, this participation is strictly conditional, dependent on him receiving full fitness clearance from the medical staff. The unofficial Test serves as a valuable platform for players to gain match sharpness and experience.

The injury setback is a significant blow to India’s bowling attack, which was looking to add depth and variety to its pace options. Krishna’s speed and swing make him a crucial asset in limited-overs formats, particularly in the powerplay and middle overs. His absence forces the coaching staff to rely on other pacers or adjust the bowling strategy accordingly.

The team management has emphasized that player welfare remains the top priority. They have stated that no player will be fielded unless they are fully fit and confident in their abilities. This approach aims to ensure long-term sustainability in the players' careers, especially given the packed international calendar.
