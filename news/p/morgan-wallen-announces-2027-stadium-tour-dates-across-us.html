Morgan Wallen Announces 2027 Stadium Tour Dates Across US

Country music star Morgan Wallen has revealed the schedule for his upcoming Last Time I Checked Tour, marking a significant expansion into large stadium venues. Fans are currently rushing to secure presale tickets for the 2027 dates.

Latest developments

Morgan Wallen has officially announced the dates for his Last Time I Checked Tour, which will take place in 2027. The tour schedule includes high-profile stops at major American stadiums, signaling a continued rise in the artist's commercial appeal. Among the confirmed venues is Highmark Stadium in Buffalo, New York, where Wallen is set to perform on July 23 and 24, 2027. This double-header announcement was highlighted by the Buffalo Bills organization, indicating the scale of the event and its integration into the local sports and entertainment calendar.

In addition to the Buffalo dates, the tour will visit Nissan Stadium in Nashville, Tennessee, with a performance scheduled for Friday, August 27, 2027. Nashville remains a central hub for country music, and a stadium show there represents a significant milestone for any artist in the genre. The tour will also make a stop at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, on August 13, 2027. These selections suggest a strategic route that targets major metropolitan areas with large fan bases and substantial stadium infrastructure.
