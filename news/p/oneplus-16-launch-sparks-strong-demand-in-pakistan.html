OnePlus 16 Launch Sparks Strong Demand in Pakistan

OnePlus has unveiled its latest flagship, the OnePlus 16, in Pakistan, sparking a wave of enthusiasm among tech enthusiasts. The device boasts a new processor, upgraded camera system, and 5G connectivity, with pricing set to compete in the

Latest developments

On 12 October 2026, OnePlus announced the launch of its 16th flagship model in Pakistan, making the device available through official retail partners and online stores. The OnePlus 16 is priced at PKR 119,999, positioning it competitively against other premium offerings in the local market.

Under the hood, the phone is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, coupled with 12GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256GB of UFS 4.0 storage. The 6.7‑inch Fluid AMOLED display runs at 120Hz with HDR10+ support, while the rear camera array features a 48‑megapixel main sensor, a 50‑megapixel ultra‑wide lens, and a 32‑megapixel telephoto module capable of 3‑x optical zoom.

Battery capacity is 5,000mAh, with 65W Warp Charge support that can bring the battery from 0 to 70% in just 20 minutes. Software comes with OxygenOS 13 based on Android 14, and OnePlus promises a 5‑year software update cycle for the device.

Demand for the new model has already outstripped supply, with pre‑orders exceeding 10,000 units within the first 48 hours. Analysts note that the high interest is driven more by the brand’s reputation for value and performance than by the hardware itself, echoing earlier observations that hardware was never the issue for OnePlus in Pakistan.

The launch follows a steady rise in smartphone penetration in the country, where 70% of the population now owns a mobile device. OnePlus’s entry into the premium segment is seen as a response to growing consumer appetite for high‑end features at affordable prices.

Why it matters

For Pakistani consumers, the OnePlus 16 offers a compelling combination of performance, camera quality, and 5G connectivity at a price that is lower than many international competitors. The device’s 65W charging capability also addresses a common pain point for users who need quick top‑ups during busy commutes.

Retailers anticipate that the phone will capture a significant share of the mid‑to‑high end market, potentially reshaping the competitive dynamics with brands such as Samsung, Xiaomi, and Oppo. The availability of a local warranty and after‑sales support further enhances its appeal.

Moreover, the launch comes at a time when the Pakistani telecom sector is expanding 5G coverage, making the device’s network compatibility timely for consumers looking to future‑proof their purchases.

Background

OnePlus entered the Pakistani market in 2017, quickly building a loyal following through aggressive online marketing and a focus on flagship‑grade specifications at a mid‑tier price. The brand’s reputation for clean software and fast charging has been a key differentiator.

Previous models, such as the OnePlus 9 and 10 series, were well received, with sales figures indicating a steady growth in market share. However, the company has faced challenges in maintaining a consistent supply chain, which has occasionally led to delays in product availability.

Despite these hurdles, OnePlus has managed to establish a robust presence, with a network of authorized dealers and a growing community of users who share tips and reviews on social media platforms.

What happens next

OnePlus is expected to ramp up production in the coming months to meet the high demand, with reports suggesting that additional manufacturing units will be activated in its South Korean and Indian facilities. The company will also likely launch a localized marketing campaign to promote the device’s features.

Consumers can expect the first wave of software updates within the next two weeks, followed by a major Android 15 rollout in the third quarter. After‑sales support will be available through official service centers across major cities.
