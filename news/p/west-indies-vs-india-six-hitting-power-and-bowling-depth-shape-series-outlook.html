The West Indies and India have a storied rivalry dating back to the early days of limited‑overs cricket. In the past twelve months, India’s batting line‑up has been praised for its consistency, while the West Indies have undergone a rebuilding phase, integrating younger talent into the squad.

Bumrah’s injury earlier this year forced India to reassess its fast‑bowling strategy. Known for his lethal yorkers and ability to bowl at the death, his absence created a vacuum that the team attempted to fill with a mix of experience and youth. Simultaneously, the West Indies have been experimenting with their batting order, promoting aggressive players to the top to maximize six‑hitting opportunities. These strategic shifts set the stage for the current series, where both teams are testing new combinations under pressure.
