Background

The International League T20 was launched in 2022 as a franchise-based tournament hosted in the United Arab Emirates, featuring eight teams from the Gulf region. The Gulf Giants, based in Sharjah, and the Dubai Capitals, representing the host city, are two of the league's most competitive sides. Both teams have a mix of local talent and overseas stars, with the Giants often relying on aggressive batting and disciplined bowling.

The 2026 edition follows a double round‑robin format, where each team plays the others twice before the top four qualify for the semi‑finals. The 10th match, scheduled for November 28, pitted the Giants against the Capitals in a crucial mid‑tournament clash. Prior to this encounter, the Giants had recorded two wins and one loss, while the Capitals held a balanced record of one win, one loss, and one no‑result.

The ILT20 has attracted significant attention from broadcasters and sponsors, aiming to capture a share of the lucrative T20 market dominated by the Indian Premier League and the Big Bash. The presence of Pakistani players like Gurbaz and Omarzai adds a layer of cross‑border interest, especially given the historical cricketing ties between Pakistan and the Gulf nations.
