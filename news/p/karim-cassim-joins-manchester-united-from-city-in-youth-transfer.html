Karim Cassim joins Manchester United from City in youth transfer

Seventeen‑year‑old midfielder Karim Cassim has moved from Manchester City to Manchester United. The transfer has generated considerable attention among fans and pundits in the United Kingdom.

What is happening

Manchester United announced on Monday that they have secured the services of 17‑year‑old midfielder Karim Cassim, who had been a member of Manchester City’s academy since the age of nine. The deal is a free transfer, with United paying no compensation to City, as Cassim’s contract with the Red Devils expired earlier this summer. United’s academy director confirmed that Cassim will be registered with the club’s under‑18 squad and will train alongside the senior development team.

The contract signed by Cassim runs for three years, with an option for a fourth season based on performance milestones. While the exact financial terms have not been disclosed, United’s statement highlighted the club’s commitment to nurturing home‑grown talent and integrating promising players into the first‑team pathway. Cassim will be eligible to play in the Premier League 2 competition and may be called up for cup fixtures if his development progresses as expected.

Both clubs issued brief statements. Manchester United praised Cassim’s technical ability, vision, and work ethic, describing him as a “midfield prospect with a strong footballing IQ.” Manchester City, on the other hand, expressed gratitude for the player’s contributions at youth level and wished him success at his new club. Fans on social media reacted with a mix of surprise and optimism, noting the rarity of a direct academy switch between the city’s two biggest rivals.

The transfer also raises questions about the Homegrown Player rule, which allows clubs to register a limited number of players trained in England for a minimum of three years before the age of 21. By adding Cassim to their roster, United strengthens their pool of eligible homegrown players, a factor that can influence squad composition for European competitions. The Football Association confirmed that all paperwork was submitted on time and that Cassim meets the eligibility criteria to feature for United at both youth and senior levels.

Background and context

Karim Cassim entered Manchester City’s academy at the under‑9 level and progressed through each age group, earning a reputation as a technically gifted midfielder with a calm demeanor on the ball. Over the past two seasons, he featured regularly for City’s under‑16 and under‑18 sides, contributing to league titles and cup runs in youth competitions. His performances attracted attention from national scouts, and he received a call‑up to England’s under‑17 training camp, though he has yet to make an official appearance.

City’s academy has produced several senior internationals, and the club’s emphasis on possession‑based football shaped Cassim’s development. However, competition for midfield places at City is intense, with a deep pool of talent vying for limited promotion opportunities. United’s recent focus on rebuilding through youth signings offered Cassim a clearer pathway to senior football, prompting his decision to switch clubs.
