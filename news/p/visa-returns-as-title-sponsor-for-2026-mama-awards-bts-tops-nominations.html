Visa returns as title sponsor for 2026 MAMA Awards, BTS tops nominations

Visa has returned as the title sponsor for the 2026 MAMA Awards, with BTS topping the nomination list. The ceremony, featuring acts like Blackpink and TXT, will open presale tickets on 13 October.

Latest developments

Visa has re‑entered as the title sponsor for the 2026 MAMA Awards, a partnership first announced by the award organiser Mnet on 13 October. The company will feature its branding across all event materials and will manage a presale registration that opens on the same day, allowing fans to secure tickets before the general sale. The move restores Visa’s presence after a brief hiatus and signals confidence in the commercial appeal of the Korean music awards, which attract a global audience.

The nomination list released by Mnet shows BTS leading with the highest number of nods across major categories such as Album of the Year, Song of the Year and Best Male Group. Alongside the seven‑time Grammy‑winning group, other high‑profile acts like Blackpink, TXT, BIGBANG, aespa, IVE and ATEEZ have secured multiple nominations, confirming the depth of talent competing for the coveted trophies.

BTS’s dominance reflects its continued chart success in both domestic and overseas markets. Blackpink, the all‑female powerhouse, appears in categories ranging from Best Female Group to Best Dance Performance, while TXT is recognised for its songwriting and visual concepts. The spread of nominations across both established veterans and newer entrants highlights the broadening scope of K‑pop’s artistic output this year.

Mnet confirmed that the awards ceremony will be staged in Seoul later this year, with live streaming options for international viewers. The event is expected to feature performances from the nominated artists, though specific line‑up details remain under wraps. Visa’s branding will be visible throughout the broadcast, and the company has pledged to support fan engagement initiatives, including exclusive digital content for ticket holders.

Why it matters

K‑pop enjoys a massive following in Pakistan, where streaming platforms report millions of monthly listeners for groups like BTS and Blackpink. The return of a global brand such as Visa to the MAMA Awards underscores the commercial viability of the genre and may encourage more local sponsors to invest in related events and merchandise. For Pakistani fans, the presale registration provides an early opportunity to secure seats at a high‑profile international concert, a rare chance to experience live K‑pop performances outside Asia.

The awards also serve as a cultural bridge, showcasing South Korean music trends that influence fashion, dance and online content creation among Pakistani youth. Increased visibility of the ceremony can stimulate interest in Korean language courses, tourism and broader entertainment collaborations, reinforcing the soft power impact of Korean pop culture in the region.

Background

The Mnet Asian Music Awards, commonly known as MAMA, were launched in 1999 and have grown into one of the most prestigious music award shows in Asia. Over the years the ceremony has expanded its reach, incorporating artists from across the continent and broadcasting to a worldwide audience. Visa first partnered with MAMA in 2020, providing payment solutions for ticketing and fan‑engagement platforms, before stepping back in 2024.

The award categories cover a wide spectrum of musical achievement, from best album and song to performance and visual design. Nomination announcements are typically made in early October, generating intense media coverage and fan speculation. The 2026 edition follows a year in which K‑pop groups have dominated global charts, reinforcing the awards' role as a barometer of international music trends.

What happens next

Fans can register for the presale starting 13 October, after which a limited number of tickets will be allocated on a first‑come, first‑served basis. Full ticket sales are expected to open in early November, with pricing tiers reflecting seat location and access to exclusive backstage content. The ceremony itself is slated for December, and Mnet will stream it live on its official platforms, offering subtitles in multiple languages, including Urdu for Pakistani viewers.
