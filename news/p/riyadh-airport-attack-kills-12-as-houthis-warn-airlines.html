Riyadh airport attack kills 12 as Houthis warn airlines

At least 12 people were killed and hundreds wounded in a deadly strike on Riyadh airport. The Houthi movement has issued warnings to airlines and passengers regarding the safety of Saudi aviation hubs.

Latest developments

A significant security breach occurred at Riyadh airport, resulting in the deaths of at least 12 individuals and leaving hundreds injured. The attack, attributed to the Houthi movement, marks one of the most severe incidents targeting Saudi infrastructure in recent years. Emergency services responded rapidly to the scene, managing to contain the immediate aftermath while assessing the full extent of the damage to the terminal and surrounding areas.

In the wake of the incident, the Houthis issued a stark warning to international airlines and passengers. The group explicitly advised against the use of Saudi Arabian airports, citing ongoing threats and instability in the region. This statement aims to disrupt commercial aviation routes and exert pressure on the Saudi-led coalition. The warning has already caused confusion among travelers and prompted several carriers to review their flight paths and safety protocols.

The Saudi-led coalition has vowed to respond to the attack, though specific details of the countermeasures have not been fully disclosed. Officials have condemned the act as a violation of international norms and a direct threat to regional stability. The incident has intensified diplomatic tensions, with various nations calling for de-escalation while also expressing concern over the potential for further escalation in the Middle East.

International media outlets, including BBC, CNN, and Al Jazeera, have reported on the event, highlighting the scale of the casualties and the broader implications for regional security. The live coverage has drawn global attention, with updates continuing to emerge as investigations proceed. The focus remains on identifying the perpetrators and understanding the method used to breach the airport's security perimeter.

Background

The Houthi movement, based in Yemen, has been involved in a prolonged conflict with the Saudi-led coalition. The group has previously targeted Saudi infrastructure, including oil facilities and airports, as part of its strategy to pressure the coalition. These attacks have often been supported by Iran, which has provided the Houthis with advanced weaponry and strategic guidance.

Riyadh airport is a major hub for both domestic and international flights, serving as a critical node in the region's aviation network. The security of such facilities is paramount, given the high volume of passengers and the symbolic importance of the capital city. Previous incidents in the region have led to enhanced security measures, but this attack suggests that vulnerabilities still exist.

The broader context includes ongoing tensions between Iran and Saudi Arabia, with the conflict in Yemen serving as a proxy battleground. The involvement of external actors has complicated the situation, making it difficult to achieve a lasting peace. The recent attack on Riyadh airport is part of this larger pattern of instability and conflict in the Middle East.
