Buffalo Bills schedule: Week 1 roster moves and injury outlook

The Bills have announced their Week 1 schedule and key roster changes ahead of the season opener against Houston. Fans are looking at new active‑roster players and injury reports to gauge the team's early prospects.

What is happening

The Buffalo Bills released their official Week 1 schedule on Tuesday, confirming a home opener against the Houston Texans on September 10 at 1:00 p.m. ET. The game will be televised nationally on CBS and streamed on the NFL app, giving fans multiple ways to watch the start of the 2024 season.

In the final roster moves before the season, the Bills elevated wide receiver Greg Dortch and running back Frank Gore Jr. from the practice squad to the 53‑man active roster. Both players earned the promotion after impressive performances in preseason games and provide additional depth at skill positions.

The team also listed three players as questionable for the Texans game due to lingering injuries sustained during training camp. The injuries affect the defensive line and secondary, and the players are expected to undergo further evaluation before the kickoff. No official status changes have been announced yet.

These roster changes and injury notes are reflected in the schedule’s accompanying details on the Bills’ official website and major sports news outlets. Fans can view the updated depth chart and injury report alongside the schedule to understand who is likely to see playing time in the opener.

Background and context

The Buffalo Bills finished the previous season with a playoff berth but fell short of the conference championship. In the offseason, the front office focused on strengthening depth at wide receiver and running back, leading to the signings of several free agents and the retention of promising young talent like Frank Gore Jr.

Practice‑squad promotions have become a common strategy for NFL teams looking to develop players without committing a full roster spot. By moving Greg Dortch and Frank Gore Jr. to the active roster, the Bills align with league trends that reward strong preseason performances with immediate playing opportunities.

Injuries are an inevitable part of football, and the Bills entered training camp with several players nursing minor ailments. The three questionable designations reflect the coaching staff’s cautious approach, preferring to assess each player’s health on a day‑by‑day basis rather than risk aggravating existing conditions.
