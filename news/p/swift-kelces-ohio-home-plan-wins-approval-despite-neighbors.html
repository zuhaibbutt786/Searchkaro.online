Swift, Kelce's Ohio Home Plan Wins Approval Despite Neighbors

The couple's expansive Ohio property plan cleared the Bratenahl planning board after a contentious meeting, even as some locals voiced strong objections. Critics warned the development could strain community ties and hinted at personal fall

Latest developments

The Bratenahl Planning Commission met on Thursday to consider the proposed exterior plan for the property owned by pop star Taylor Swift and NFL tight end Travis Kelce. After a session that lasted more than two hours, the commission voted to approve the plan, allowing the couple to move forward with construction of a multi‑million‑dollar residence on the lakefront. Neighbors in the affluent suburb voiced sharp criticism, arguing the design would overwhelm the existing streetscape and set a precedent for oversized builds. Several residents attended the meeting in person, while others submitted written comments expressing concerns about traffic, privacy, and the visual impact of the new structure. Despite the pushback, the board concluded that the proposal complied with local zoning codes and granted the necessary permits. In the same meeting, a handful of attendees made a tongue‑in‑cheek prediction that the stress of the dispute could lead to a split between Swift and Kelce, a comment that quickly spread on social media. The couple has not responded publicly to the remarks. Local media outlets, including the Akron Beacon Journal and Cleveland.com, reported that the approval marks the final hurdle before construction can begin, with contractors expected to break ground early next year. The decision also sparked a broader conversation about how celebrity projects intersect with community planning processes in small municipalities.
