What Hi-Fi? Announces 2026 Awards Winners Across Audio and Video

What Hi-Fi? has published its 2026 Awards winners, naming leading products in both audio and video categories. The results highlight trends that could shape UK buying decisions this year.

Latest developments

The annual What Hi-Fi? Awards for 2026 have been published, confirming the magazine's selections for the best consumer audio and video equipment released over the past year. The winners were announced on the What Hi-Fi? website and covered by several online outlets, including SuaraGarut.ID, which reproduced the full list of awardees across categories such as headphones, speakers, soundbars, turntables and televisions. The ceremony, traditionally held in London, recognised products that delivered a combination of performance, design and value, reflecting the publication's long‑standing reputation as a trusted guide for UK shoppers.

Among the audio categories, the awards highlighted a range of over‑ear, on‑ear and in‑ear headphones, as well as premium wireless earbuds that have set new standards for noise cancellation and battery life. In the speaker segment, both bookshelf and floor‑standing models were honoured for their clarity and power handling, while a selection of compact Bluetooth speakers received commendations for portability without compromising sound quality. The soundbar and home‑theatre awards went to systems that integrate advanced processing technologies, offering immersive surround experiences that rival dedicated speaker setups.

The video awards recognised televisions that combine high dynamic range (HDR) performance with innovative panel technologies, such as OLED and Mini‑LED, delivering deeper blacks and brighter highlights. Winners in this segment also included smart TV platforms that have improved user interfaces and integrated voice assistants, making streaming services more accessible. Additionally, a few premium projectors were acknowledged for their ability to produce cinema‑grade images in home environments.

Overall, the 2026 winners reflect a market that continues to push the boundaries of audio fidelity and visual immersion, while also emphasizing sustainability and connectivity. What Hi-Fi? noted that many of the awarded products incorporate recycled materials and energy‑saving features, aligning with growing consumer expectations for environmentally responsible technology.
