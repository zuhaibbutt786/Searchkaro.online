Pakistan’s Single Window adds remote biometric verification via PakID

The government has linked the Single Window trade platform with NADRA’s PakID app to allow remote biometric checks. This move aims to speed up document processing for businesses and officials alike.

What is happening

The Pakistan Single Window (PSW) platform has announced an integration with NADRA’s PakID mobile application that enables remote biometric verification. The change allows users to confirm their identity using fingerprint or facial data captured on a smartphone, eliminating the need for in‑person visits to NADRA offices. The feature is being rolled out in phases, beginning with a pilot that includes major importers and exporters who regularly interact with customs and trade authorities.

Under the new workflow, a trader logs into the PSW portal, initiates a verification request, and receives a secure link to the PakID app. The app then prompts the user to capture the required biometric sample, which is encrypted and transmitted to NADRA’s verification engine. Once NADRA confirms the match, the PSW system automatically updates the user’s status, allowing the transaction to proceed without manual intervention.

Officials say the remote capability reduces processing time from days to minutes, particularly for documents that previously required physical signatures and biometric capture at NADRA service centres. It also lowers travel costs for businesses located far from major cities, supporting the government’s broader agenda of digital transformation in public services.

The integration is expected to be fully operational for all registered users by the end of the quarter, with training materials and a helpdesk made available in Urdu and English. NADRA has emphasized that the biometric data will be stored only for the duration needed to complete the verification, after which it will be deleted in accordance with national data‑protection regulations.

Background and context

NADRA, Pakistan’s National Database and Registration Authority, has maintained a biometric database for national identity cards since the early 2000s. The PakID app, introduced in 2022, extended that capability to smartphones, allowing citizens to access a range of government services without visiting a physical office.

The Pakistan Single Window is a web‑based portal that consolidates import, export, and regulatory procedures into a single interface. Launched in 2021, it was designed to reduce duplication of effort across ministries and to speed up trade clearance. However, many of its processes still required manual identity verification, which created bottlenecks for high‑volume users.

Internationally, the use of remote biometric verification has grown as governments seek to maintain service continuity during disruptions such as pandemics or natural disasters. Countries like Estonia and Singapore have long used mobile‑based identity verification to support e‑government services, setting a benchmark that Pakistan is now aiming to meet.

What to know next

Users who wish to take advantage of the remote verification must first ensure that their PakID app is updated to the latest version and that their device’s camera meets the minimum quality standards set by NADRA. After updating, they will need to link their PakID account with their PSW profile by entering a one‑time password sent to their registered mobile number.

The government has indicated that additional public‑sector platforms, such as the tax filing system and the land registration portal, will be connected to the same biometric verification engine later in the year. This phased approach is intended to create a unified digital identity ecosystem across multiple services.

Privacy advocates have called for clear guidelines on data retention and third‑party access. NADRA has responded by publishing a detailed data‑handling policy that outlines encryption methods, audit trails, and user consent mechanisms. Stakeholders are encouraged to review the policy and to report any concerns through the official grievance portal.
