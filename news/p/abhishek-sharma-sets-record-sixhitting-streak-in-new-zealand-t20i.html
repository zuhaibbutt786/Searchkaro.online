Abhishek Sharma sets record six‑hitting streak in New Zealand T20I

Indian opener Abhishek Sharma set a new milestone by hitting six consecutive sixes in a T20I against New Zealand. The achievement has drawn praise from fans and analysts alike.

Latest developments

On 23 October 2026, during the second T20I of the India tour of New Zealand, the Indian batting lineup faced a challenging pitch in Christchurch. The match began with a cautious approach from both sides, but India’s top order struggled to build momentum.

Abhishek Sharma, batting at number four, broke the rhythm by striking six boundaries in a row. The feat made him the first player to hit six consecutive sixes in a T20I against New Zealand, a record that has not been replicated in any other international T20 encounter.

The innings also saw a record partnership with Sanju Samson, who joined Sharma in the middle of the innings. Together, they surpassed the previous high‑scoring partnership set by KL Rahul and Rohit Sharma, adding a new benchmark for India’s batting partnerships in the format.

A selfie taken by Sharma and teammate Vaibhav Sooryavanshi during the post‑match celebrations went viral on social media. The image, featuring the duo in a Punjabi pep talk in Christchurch, attracted millions of views and sparked memes across cricket forums.

While no official statements were released, commentators noted the significance of the record for India’s batting prospects. Analysts highlighted how Sharma’s aggressive approach could influence the team’s strategy in upcoming T20 series.

Why it matters

For cricket fans in Pakistan, Sharma’s record offers a source of excitement and a benchmark for their own players. The achievement demonstrates the evolving nature of T20 batting, where power hitting can change the course of a game.

The record also has implications for the upcoming ICC T20 World Cup. Pakistan’s selectors may look at Sharma’s performance when considering power hitters for the national squad, especially in high‑pressure matches.

Social media engagement has surged following the record. Fans across the subcontinent have shared clips of Sharma’s six‑six streak, and the hashtag #SharmaSixes has trended on Twitter, indicating the widespread impact of the moment.

Background

Abhishek Sharma made his international debut for India in 2018 and quickly established himself as a reliable middle‑order batsman. Known for his aggressive style, he has been a key contributor in both ODIs and T20Is.

Prior to the Christchurch match, Sharma had already set a personal best of 94 runs in a T20I, showcasing his ability to play long innings. His record six‑six streak builds on this reputation and adds a new dimension to his career.

Six‑hitting in T20 cricket is a rare skill that requires timing, strength, and confidence. Sharma’s ability to maintain a high strike rate while consistently clearing the boundary demonstrates his mastery of the format.

What happens next

India’s next fixture is a T20I against Bangladesh, where Sharma is expected to open the innings. Analysts predict that he will carry the aggressive approach from Christchurch into the new series.

The cricket board may also review the partnership record with Sanju Samson to identify strategies that can be replicated in future matches. The focus will likely be on building strong middle‑order collaborations.
