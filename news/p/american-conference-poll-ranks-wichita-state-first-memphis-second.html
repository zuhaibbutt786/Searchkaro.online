American Conference poll ranks Wichita State first, Memphis second

Wichita State has been named the favorite in the American Conference preseason poll, while Memphis follows in second place under new coach Penny Hardaway. The rankings set the tone for the upcoming media days in Tampa.

Latest developments

The American Conference released its 2026-27 men's basketball preseason poll on Monday, placing Wichita State at the summit with a unanimous vote from league coaches and media members. Memphis earned the second spot, a notable rise that reflects the impact of former NBA star Penny Hardaway, who took over as head coach last season.

Media days for the conference begin this week in Tampa, Florida, where teams will field questions from reporters and analysts. Memphis faces three key queries, according to a local sports outlet, focusing on Hardaway's system implementation, player development, and the team's ability to compete against top conference rivals.

Wichita State's top ranking follows a strong finish to the previous season, including a deep run in the conference tournament. Analysts point to the Shockers' balanced scoring and experienced roster as reasons for the high expectations.

Other programs, such as the University of North Texas and the University of Southern Mississippi, landed in the middle of the poll, indicating a competitive field that could produce surprises throughout the regular season.

Why it matters

The preseason poll shapes fan interest and media coverage across the United States, especially in regions where college basketball drives local economies. A top ranking for Wichita State may boost ticket sales, merchandise revenue, and television viewership for the Shockers' games.

For Memphis, the second-place finish under Hardaway signals a potential turnaround for a program that has struggled in recent years. The elevated ranking could attract higher‑profile recruits, increase alumni donations, and raise the university's national profile.

Beyond individual schools, the poll influences betting markets and conference scheduling decisions. Teams positioned near the top often receive more favorable television slots, which can affect advertising revenue and the broader exposure of the American Conference.

Background

The American Conference, a mid‑major Division I league, comprises 12 schools spread across the central United States. Each year, a preseason poll is compiled from votes by league coaches and selected media members, offering an early glimpse of how teams are expected to perform.

Penny Hardaway, a former NBA All‑Star, was hired as Memphis' head coach in 2024. His hiring generated significant buzz, and his first season saw the Tigers improve their win total. The current poll reflects optimism that his recruiting connections and professional experience will continue to elevate the program.

Wichita State, under longtime coach Eric Hollenbeck, has built a reputation for disciplined defense and efficient offense. The Shockers' recent success in the conference tournament and a strong non‑conference schedule contributed to their top ranking.

What happens next

The conference will hold its media days in Tampa this Thursday, where coaches will discuss strategies and answer press questions. Following media days, the regular season schedule will be released, confirming matchups and travel itineraries for each team.

Fans can expect the first conference games to tip off in early November, with Wichita State and Memphis slated to open their seasons against non‑conference opponents before diving into league play.
