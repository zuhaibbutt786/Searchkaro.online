Saudi Arabia Appoints New Joint Forces Commander in Royal Decree

A royal decree has reshaped the senior military leadership in Saudi Arabia, naming a new commander for the Joint Forces. The announcement highlights continued strategic adjustments within the Kingdom's defense structure.

Latest developments

Saudi Arabia has issued a royal decree that introduces significant changes to the top echelons of its military command structure. The primary focus of the announcement is the appointment of Major General Fayyad bin Hamad Al-Ruwaili as the new commander of the Joint Forces. This role is critical for coordinating operations across different military branches, ensuring unified strategic responses to regional security challenges.

The decree does not stop at the Joint Forces command. It also addresses the advisory structure surrounding the Ministry of Defense. According to reports from major Arab news outlets, including CNN Arabic and Al Arabiya, the decree appoints a senior officer to serve as an advisor to the Minister of Defense for military affairs. This dual appointment suggests a coordinated effort to streamline communication and decision-making processes within the defense establishment.

The timing of these appointments aligns with broader trends in the Kingdom's military modernization. By placing experienced officers in key command and advisory roles, the Saudi leadership aims to enhance operational readiness. The specific mention of Al-Ruwaili in multiple headlines indicates that his background and previous service record were likely key factors in this selection process.

Official statements from the Saudi Press Agency typically accompany such decrees, providing the formal legal basis for the changes. The transition of command is expected to be handled with standard military protocols to maintain operational continuity.
