India and New Zealand clash in Christchurch as Test series heats up

India and New Zealand meet at Hagley Oval in Christchurch for a crucial Test match. Pakistani viewers are tuning in as both sides look to shape the World Test Championship race.

Latest developments

The second Test of the India tour of New Zealand is set to begin at Hagley Oval in Christchurch, a venue that has produced memorable moments in recent years. Both teams entered the match with contrasting objectives: New Zealand hopes to defend its home advantage while India seeks to consolidate its position in the World Test Championship. The series follows a tightly contested first Test, and the outcome of the second match could determine which side gains momentum for the remainder of the tour.
