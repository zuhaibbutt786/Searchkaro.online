Background

Pakhtakor Tashkent, founded in 1956, is Uzbekistan’s most decorated club, having won multiple league titles and regularly representing the nation in continental competitions. Their last AFC Champions League appearance saw them reach the round of 16, where they were eliminated by a Japanese side. Al Qadsiah, based in Khobar, Saudi Arabia, has a storied history that includes a Saudi Professional League championship in 1997 and a King Cup triumph in 2010. The club’s recent resurgence has been driven by a new investment group that upgraded training facilities and recruited foreign coaches.

The two clubs have never faced each other in official competition, making this meeting a first‑time encounter. Historically, Uzbek clubs have struggled against Saudi teams in the AFC tournament, with a win‑loss record of 2‑5 in the past decade. However, Pakhtakor’s recent domestic dominance suggests they could narrow that gap.

Both teams qualified for the current group stage through their respective domestic leagues. Pakhtakor secured the top spot in the Uzbek Super League, while Al Qadsiah finished third in the Saudi Professional League, earning a place through the AFC’s slot allocation system.
