Leigh Francis confirmed as surprise late entrant on I’m a Celebrity 2026

Comedian Leigh Francis has been announced as a surprise late arrival on this year’s I’m a Celebrity jungle series. The move adds a new dynamic to the competition and has already ignited fan discussion online.

Background

I’m a Celebrity…Get Me Out of Here! first aired in 2002 and has become a staple of British summer television. The format places well‑known public figures in a remote jungle camp where they must complete challenges to earn food and avoid elimination. Over the years, the show has featured a wide range of participants, from musicians and athletes to reality‑TV alumni.

Surprise entrants are not new to the series; previous seasons have seen late arrivals such as a former footballer and a reality‑show star who joined after the first elimination round. These twists are designed to disrupt existing group dynamics and keep the audience engaged. The practice has been credited with revitalising the competition during its middle weeks.

Leigh Francis, born in 1973 in Beeston, Nottinghamshire, built his career through sketch comedy and character work. After the success of "Bo' Selecta!" he launched the Keith Lemon persona, which became a fixture on British daytime TV. His transition from scripted comedy to a reality‑show environment marks a new chapter in his professional trajectory, offering a chance to showcase his improvisational skills in a high‑pressure setting.
