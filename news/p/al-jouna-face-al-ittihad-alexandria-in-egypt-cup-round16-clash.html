Al Jouna Face Al Ittihad Alexandria in Egypt Cup Round‑16 Clash

Al Jouna and Al Ittihad Alexandria are set to clash in the opening round‑16 of the Egypt Cup on 18 November. The match, part of a growing handball calendar, will be televised nationwide.

Latest developments

On Wednesday 18 November, the handball competition of the Egypt Cup will feature a round‑16 match between Al Jouna and Al Ittihad Alexandria. The fixture is part of the tournament’s opening phase, following the announcement of the schedule by the Egyptian Handball Federation. Both clubs have qualified after successful performances in earlier rounds, and the match is scheduled to take place at the Cairo Sports Hall.

Al Jouna, known locally as "الجونة", has a history of strong defensive play in domestic competitions. Their roster includes several players who have represented the national team at international tournaments. Al Ittihad Alexandria, or "الاتحاد السكندري", is a club with a legacy of winning titles in the past decade and has recently rebuilt its squad to challenge for the cup.

The match is expected to draw significant attention from fans across the country, as it marks the first encounter between the two sides in the current edition of the cup. The Egyptian Handball Federation has confirmed that the game will be broadcast live on national television, providing an opportunity for supporters to follow the action from home. The fixture also coincides with the opening of the Super Cup for handball, adding to the excitement of the sporting calendar.

Why it matters

For the Egyptian handball community, the Al Jouna versus Al Ittihad Alexandria match represents a critical test of form ahead of the national league season. Clubs often use cup fixtures to assess squad depth and tactical flexibility, and the outcome can influence coaching decisions for the months ahead. Fans in both Cairo and Alexandria will be keen to see how their teams perform under the pressure of a knockout game.

The match also highlights the growing popularity of handball in Egypt, a sport that has traditionally been dominated by football. Increased media coverage and televised broadcasts are helping to raise the profile of handball, encouraging more youth participation and investment in club facilities. As a result, the national federation is optimistic that the cup will continue to attract larger audiences and sponsorship deals.

Background

Al Jouna, founded in the late 20th century, has steadily climbed the ranks of Egyptian handball. Their recent promotion to the top division was followed by a steady improvement in league standings, culminating in a semi‑final appearance in the previous cup edition. The club’s coaching staff has emphasized a disciplined approach, focusing on quick transitions and tight defensive structures.

Al Ittihad Alexandria, established in the early 1900s, is one of the oldest handball clubs in the country. The club’s name translates to "The Union of Alexandria," reflecting its origins as a gathering of local players. Over the years, the club has produced several national team players and has won multiple domestic titles, making them a perennial contender in cup competitions.

What happens next

The outcome of the round‑16 match will determine which club advances to the quarter‑finals of the Egypt Cup. Both teams will regroup in the days following the game to prepare for the next stage, with training sessions scheduled to focus on tactical adjustments based on the match’s findings. The federation has announced that the quarter‑finals will be played in early December, giving clubs a brief window to recover and plan.

For fans, the next steps involve following the live broadcast and engaging with club social media channels to stay updated on player performances and injury reports. The clubs’ official websites will also post post‑match analyses and statistical summaries, providing deeper insight into the game’s dynamics.
