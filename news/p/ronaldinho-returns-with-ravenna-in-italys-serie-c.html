Ronaldinho returns with Ravenna in Italy's Serie C

Brazilian star Ronaldinho has officially joined Italian third‑division club Ravenna, ending a ten‑year hiatus from professional football. The move has ignited interest among fans in Pakistan and across the football world.

Latest developments

Ronaldinho signed with Ravenna, an Italian Serie C club, on a short‑term contract. The agreement was announced on Monday by the club's sporting director. He will be registered for the remainder of the 2023‑24 season.

The signing makes him the highest‑profile player in Italy’s third tier in recent years. Ravenna’s management said the move is intended to boost the team’s visibility and attract sponsorship, while also giving the former World Cup winner a chance to train and potentially feature in competitive matches.

Media reports from Dawn and BBC confirm that Ronaldinho has been cleared to train with the squad and will travel with the team for its upcoming fixtures in the Serie C Group B schedule. ESPN India noted that the club has left the door open for him to appear on the matchday roster if fitness permits.

The Brazilian icon has not played professional football since leaving Brazilian club Fluminense in 2021 and has spent the last years involved in exhibition matches and charitable events. His return to a professional league marks a rare instance of a retired global star re‑entering competitive football.

Why it matters

Football is the most watched sport in Pakistan, and Ronaldinho’s name carries strong brand value. His arrival in Serie C generated a spike in online searches across the country, indicating high public curiosity.

Local broadcasters and sports channels are already planning special segments, and merchandise retailers anticipate increased demand for jerseys bearing his name. The move also highlights the growing connection between Pakistani fans and European lower‑division football, where affordable streaming options are expanding.

For aspiring Pakistani players, the story offers a tangible example of a world‑class talent choosing a modest club to restart his career, reinforcing the idea that football pathways are not limited to top‑flight leagues.

Background

Ronaldinho, born Ronaldo de Assis Moreira, rose to prominence with Barcelona and AC Milan, winning the FIFA World Player of the Year award twice in 2004 and 2005. He helped Brazil win the 2002 World Cup and retired from international duty in 2013.

After leaving Barcelona in 2008, he spent three seasons with Milan before returning to Brazil, where he played for Flamengo, Atlético Mineiro and Fluminense. He officially announced his retirement from professional football in early 2022, although he continued to appear in exhibition matches worldwide.

The decision to join Ravenna follows a period of speculation after the Brazilian star was spotted training with the club’s youth squad in early 2024. Club officials confirmed that the arrangement is based on a mutual desire to bring experience to the squad and to give Ronaldinho a platform to assess his fitness.

What happens next

Ravenna is scheduled to face Serie C rivals in the next two weeks, and Ronaldinho will be listed on the matchday squad if he passes the club’s medical assessment. The Italian Football Federation has granted a special registration for the player, allowing him to compete despite his age.

Observers expect the club to use his presence primarily for marketing and mentorship, while monitoring his on‑field contributions. If he makes an appearance, it could set a precedent for other retired stars considering similar comebacks.
