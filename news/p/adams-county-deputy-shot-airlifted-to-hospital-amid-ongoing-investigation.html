Adams County Deputy Shot, Airlifted to Hospital Amid Ongoing Investigation

A deputy with the Adams County sheriff’s office was wounded by gunfire during a routine patrol and taken to a regional medical center. Authorities have launched a manhunt for the individual believed to be responsible.

Latest developments

On Friday evening, a sheriff’s deputy in Adams County, Ohio, was shot while responding to a call on Faith Road. The incident occurred shortly after the officer arrived at the scene, and the deputy sustained a gunshot wound to the torso. Emergency responders provided immediate medical aid before the injured officer was airlifted to the University of Cincinnati Medical Center, where he remains under observation. Hospital officials have not released detailed information about his condition, but the sheriff’s office confirmed he is in stable condition.

The Adams County sheriff’s office released a statement confirming that the suspect fled the scene on foot and that a coordinated search effort is underway. Deputies from neighboring jurisdictions, including the Ohio State Highway Patrol, have been deployed to assist in the manhunt. Law enforcement officials have asked the public to avoid the area around Faith Road and to report any suspicious activity that could be linked to the shooting.

Investigators have collected forensic evidence at the location, including shell casings and surveillance footage from nearby residences. While the exact motive remains unclear, authorities have indicated that the suspect may have been armed and possibly involved in other criminal activity. The sheriff’s office has pledged to keep the community informed as new information becomes available.
