Shah Rukh Khan sends defamation notice over viral Ambani video

Shah Rukh Khan's legal team has served a notice to influencer Suvigya Sharma over a video that claimed the actor was told to stay silent about Anant Ambani. The dispute highlights growing tensions between celebrities and social‑media creato

Latest developments

On 26 November 2026, Shah Rukh Khan's legal team sent a formal notice to social‑media influencer Suvigya Sharma, alleging defamation over a short video that circulated online. The video featured a clip in which a voiceover told the actor to "keep quiet" regarding remarks made by Anant Ambani, the son of Reliance Industries founder Mukesh Ambani. Khan's representatives claim the content misrepresents his stance and damages his reputation. The notice, filed under Indian defamation law, demands that Sharma withdraw the video, issue a public apology, and refrain from further dissemination of similar material. The legal counsel also warned of potential civil action if the demands are not met within a stipulated period. Sharma, known for commenting on celebrity news, has not publicly responded as of the time of writing. The controversy erupted after the video went viral on platforms such as Instagram Reels and TikTok, accumulating hundreds of thousands of views within hours. Several users interpreted the clip as an attempt to pressure Khan into silence about the Ambani family's public statements on a recent business venture. Media outlets in Kerala reported the story, noting the rapid spread of the content across regional language channels. Law enforcement agencies have not opened a criminal case, and the matter remains a civil dispute. The police spokesperson confirmed that no FIR has been lodged against Sharma, and any investigation, if required, will be handled by the courts following the notice. The episode highlights the growing tension between Indian celebrities and online influencers over content that blurs fact and opinion.

Why it matters

The case underscores how high‑profile Indian personalities are increasingly using legal tools to protect their image in the digital age. Defamation suits can deter the rapid sharing of unverified claims, especially when they involve powerful business families like the Ambanis, whose public statements often attract intense scrutiny. For Indian internet users, the dispute raises questions about freedom of expression on social media and the responsibility of influencers to verify information before publishing. It also illustrates the potential financial and reputational risks for creators who engage in sensational commentary without solid evidence.

Background

Shah Rukh Khan, often referred to as the "King of Bollywood," has a career spanning three decades and a fan base that extends beyond India. He has previously faced legal challenges related to media coverage, including disputes over privacy and trademark issues. His stature makes any perceived attack on his reputation a matter of public interest. Anant Ambani, the youngest son of Mukesh Ambani, has recently been in the news for his involvement in the launch of new projects under Reliance Industries. His comments on various economic policies have been widely reported, and any suggestion that a celebrity was being silenced in relation to those remarks can quickly become a flashpoint in Indian media.

What happens next

The court will review the defamation notice and decide whether Sharma must comply with the demands or face a civil lawsuit. If the case proceeds, a hearing could be scheduled within weeks, and a judgment may set a precedent for similar disputes involving celebrities and digital content creators. Meanwhile, the video remains accessible on several platforms, though some users have reported it being flagged or removed after the notice. Observers expect that the outcome will influence how influencers approach politically or commercially sensitive topics in the future.

Summary
