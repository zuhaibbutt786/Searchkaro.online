Ireland clinches 55-run win over Namibia in Windhoek ODI

Ireland's cricket team defeated Namibia by 55 runs in the opening ODI of their 2026 tour of Windhoek. The win, highlighted by Paul Stirling’s half‑century, set new series records.

Latest developments

Ireland completed the first One Day International of their 2026 tour of Namibia with a 55‑run victory in Windhoek. The final score read Namibia 174 all out to Ireland 229 for 9, giving the tourists a comfortable margin. Paul Stirling anchored the chase with a composed 56, guiding the Irish side through the middle overs. Namibia’s Jack Brassell contributed a quick cameo of four runs off 18 balls, but the effort was insufficient to challenge the target. The win marked a record‑breaking start for the Irish team, as they set new benchmarks for runs scored in the opening match of an overseas series.
