Red Magic 12 Pro+ design and RGB cooling revealed ahead of Pakistan launch

Red Magic's upcoming 12 Pro+ gaming phone has been revealed with new colour options and an industry‑first RGB water‑cooling system. Reservations in Pakistan start on October 6 ahead of the October 15 launch.

Latest developments

The Chinese smartphone maker Nubia has unveiled the design details of its upcoming Red Magic 12 Pro+ gaming phone ahead of the official launch. The teaser images released on the company's website show a sleek chassis available in three colour schemes – a matte black, a metallic teal and a limited‑edition crimson variant. All models feature a reinforced frame and a rear panel that houses a distinctive RGB‑lit water‑cooling system, a first for any consumer smartphone. According to the product page, the Red Magic 12 Pro+ will be powered by the latest Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, paired with up to 16 GB of LPDDR5X RAM and a 512 GB UFS 4.0 storage option. The device retains the 6.8‑inch AMOLED display with a 165 Hz refresh rate and a peak brightness of 1800 nits, specifications that place it among the top tier of gaming phones currently on the market. The highlight of the announcement is the industry‑first RGB water‑cooling system that uses a micro‑fluidic loop to dissipate heat from the processor and GPU. Small LEDs embedded in the cooling channels can display a range of colours, allowing users to customise the phone’s look while it remains cool during intensive gaming sessions. A short video released by Nubia demonstrates the cooling module in action, showing temperature drops of up to 15 °C compared with a standard passive cooling design. Reservations for the Red Magic 12 Pro+ opened on 6 October via the official Nubia portal, with an early‑bird price set at PKR 119,999 for the base configuration. The reservation window will remain open until the scheduled launch on 15 October, after which the phone will be available through major Pakistani retailers and online marketplaces. Early reports suggest that the limited‑edition crimson colour will be allocated on a first‑come, first‑served basis.

Why it matters

Gaming smartphones have carved a niche in Pakistan’s mobile market, where a growing community of e‑sports enthusiasts seeks high‑performance devices without the expense of a PC. The Red Magic 12 Pro+ promises to raise the performance ceiling with its Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chip and advanced cooling, potentially attracting users who currently rely on foreign‑made gaming phones such as ASUS ROG or Xiaomi Black Shark. The introduction of RGB water‑cooling also adds a visual customisation element that resonates with the local youth culture, where personalised tech accessories are popular. If the device delivers on its thermal claims, it could set a new benchmark for mobile gaming comfort in the region, encouraging developers to optimise titles for higher frame rates on Android platforms. From a consumer‑price perspective, the PKR 119,999 reservation price places the Red Magic 12 Pro+ in the premium segment, yet still below many imported flagship phones. This price positioning may influence purchasing decisions for gamers who previously considered importing devices, potentially boosting local sales and after‑sales service demand.

Background

Nubia’s Red Magic line was launched in 2018 as a dedicated gaming smartphone series, competing with brands like ASUS ROG Phone and Lenovo Legion. Over the years, the series has introduced features such as shoulder triggers, high‑refresh‑rate displays and vapor‑chamber cooling, building a reputation among mobile gamers. The previous flagship, the Red Magic 11 Pro, featured a Snapdragon 8+ Gen 2 processor and a 144 Hz display, but its cooling relied on a passive graphite layer rather than an active liquid system. Industry analysts have noted that the shift to an RGB‑lit water‑cooling loop represents a significant engineering step, potentially narrowing the gap between mobile and handheld console performance.

What happens next

Nubia plans to begin shipping the Red Magic 12 Pro+ to Pakistani customers within a week of the 15 October launch, with initial stock allocated to major e‑commerce platforms such as Daraz and local telecom retailers. A software update scheduled for November is expected to introduce additional gaming‑mode optimisations and expand the RGB lighting profiles.
