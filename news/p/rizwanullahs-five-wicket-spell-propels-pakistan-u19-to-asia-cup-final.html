Rizwanullah's five-wicket spell propels Pakistan U19 to Asia Cup final

Rizwanullah's five‑wicket performance lifted Pakistan U19 past Bangladesh in the ACC Men's U19 Asia Cup semi‑final. The win sets up a final showdown for the young side.

Latest developments

Pakistan's Under‑19 cricket team clinched a spot in the ACC Men's U19 Asia Cup final after a dominant performance in the semi‑final against Bangladesh on November 12, 2026. Fast bowler Rizwanullah emerged as the standout player, taking five wickets for just 27 runs in his ten overs. His spell broke the momentum of the Bangladeshi batting line‑up early, dismissing key top‑order batsmen and forcing a collapse that left Bangladesh 112 for 7 at the end of their innings.

The match, played at Al Amarat, saw Pakistan chase a modest target of 150 runs. Guided by a steady partnership between the openers, the team reached the required total with six wickets in hand. Rizwanullah's contribution with the ball was complemented by disciplined fielding, which resulted in two catches and a run‑out. The victory was confirmed by the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) in an official release, highlighting the bowler's five‑wicket haul as the decisive factor.

Commentary from live‑blog platforms such as Cricbuzz noted the bowler's ability to swing the ball both ways, especially under the lights, making it difficult for the Bangladeshi batsmen to settle. The semi‑final scorecard posted on Cricinfo recorded Rizwanullah's figures as 10‑2‑27‑5, a performance that placed him among the tournament’s leading wicket‑takers.

Following the win, the PCB announced that the final will be contested on November 18, 2026, with the opponent to be decided after the other semi‑final between India U19 and Sri Lanka U19. The board praised the young squad’s composure and highlighted the importance of nurturing talent like Rizwanullah for the senior national side.

Fans across Pakistan have responded positively, seeing the result as a boost to the nation’s cricketing reputation in the region.

Background

The ACC Men's U19 Asia Cup, inaugurated in 2001, brings together the best under‑19 teams from across Asia every two years. The 2026 edition featured eight teams divided into two groups, with Pakistan placed in Group A alongside Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, and Nepal. Pakistan topped its group after winning all three matches, thanks in part to a balanced bowling attack and a solid top order.

Rizwanullah, a 19‑year‑old right‑arm fast bowler from Lahore, has been part of the national youth setup since the Under‑16 level. Prior to the tournament, he impressed in domestic under‑19 competitions, earning a place in the squad after a series of wicket‑taking performances. His development has been overseen by former international pacer Waqar Younis, who now serves as a mentor for the youth program.

Bangladesh, the opponent in the semi‑final, entered the match as a strong contender, having defeated Sri Lanka in the group stage. Their batting line‑up, led by captain Shakib Al‑Mansur, was considered one of the most aggressive in the tournament. However, the early breakthroughs by Rizwanullah disrupted their game plan, forcing Bangladesh to adopt a defensive approach that ultimately proved ineffective.

What happens next

The final of the ACC Men's U19 Asia Cup is scheduled for November 18, 2026, at the same venue in Al Amarat. Pakistan will await the winner of the other semi‑final, where India U19 faces Sri Lanka U19. Preparations are already underway, with the coaching staff focusing on refining fielding drills and fine‑tuning the bowling attack to counter the expected opposition.

Rizwanullah is expected to open the bowling attack in the final, and his performance will be closely watched by national selectors. A strong showing could accelerate his inclusion in Pakistan’s emerging players camp, a stepping stone toward senior international duty.
