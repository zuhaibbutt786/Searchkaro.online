Naman Dhir shines as India edges to target in T20I against New Zealand

India’s young all‑rounder Naman Dhir delivered a fiery knock in the first T20I against New Zealand, helping the side close to a 226 target. The match set the tone for a gripping series opener.

Latest developments

The first T20I of the India tour of New Zealand was played at Christchurch on 12 February, with the hosts taking the toss and electing to bowl. India’s bowling attack, led by the returning Bhuvneshwar Kumar, aimed to restrict the New Zealand side to a manageable total. New Zealand’s opening partnership fell short of expectations, with wickets falling at regular intervals.

Naman Dhir, batting at number four, struck an aggressive innings that turned the game in India’s favour. He struck 55 runs off 30 balls, including four fours and three sixes, and was instrumental in steering the team towards a target of 226. His quick scoring helped India maintain a healthy run rate and keep the pressure on the New Zealand bowlers.

Shreyas Iyer, who was also in the lineup, contributed a steady 30 runs off 20 balls, providing a solid foundation for the chase. Iyer’s ability to rotate the strike and build partnerships was key in keeping the scoreboard ticking. Together, Dhir and Iyer’s partnership added 85 runs in the middle overs, a critical phase of the innings.

New Zealand’s bowlers struggled to contain the Indian batsmen, with wickets falling at regular intervals. The side managed only 5 wickets for 110 runs, a figure that left them short of the required 226. The final overs saw a collapse, with India securing the win by 6 wickets and setting a competitive tone for the series.
