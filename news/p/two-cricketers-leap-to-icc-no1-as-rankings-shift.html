Two Cricketers Leap to ICC No.1 as Rankings Shift

The ICC released its latest player rankings, seeing Marco Jansen rise to the top of the Test bowling list and Abhishek Sharma reclaim the No.1 spot in T20I batting. The shifts have captured the attention of cricket followers across Pakistan

Latest developments

ICC announced new rankings after recent series. Marco Jansen of New Zealand climbed to No.1 in Test bowling, overtaking the previous leader. At the same time, India's Abhishek Sharma regained the top spot in the T20I batting rankings after a rapid century against New Zealand.

The change came after Jansen's consistent performances in the recent Test series, including a five‑wicket haul in the first innings of the New Zealand vs. England match. His average and strike rate improved, pushing him ahead of the former leader. Meanwhile, Sharma's 35‑ball century in the final T20I of the India‑New Zealand series boosted his rating points, allowing him to overtake the previous No.1.

The ICC also reported that two other players moved into the top ten of the ODI batting list, but the headline focus remained on the two No.1 changes. The rankings are calculated using a points system that reflects recent performances across formats.

Pakistan’s own players saw modest movement, with Babar Azam holding steady in the top three of the T20I batting chart and Shaheen Afridi remaining among the top five bowlers in Tests. The updates have been widely discussed on social media in Pakistan, where cricket is a major sport.

The ICC’s official website posted the full tables, and national cricket boards issued brief statements congratulating the players. The rankings are expected to be updated after each series.

Why it matters

For Pakistani fans, the rise of a New Zealand bowler and an Indian batsman highlights the competitive nature of international cricket and sets benchmarks for local talent. Young bowlers in Pakistan often look to Jansen’s swing and seam techniques as a model.

Sharma’s comeback also serves as a reminder of the importance of aggressive batting in the T20 format, a style that Pakistan’s T20 side aims to emulate. The rankings influence selection decisions and sponsorship interest, affecting the domestic cricket economy.

Media outlets in Pakistan have used the rankings to generate discussion about the national team's upcoming tours, especially the scheduled Test series against England and the T20 series against Afghanistan. The performance metrics provide a data‑driven way to assess readiness.

Background

The ICC ranking system was introduced in 2002 for Tests and later expanded to ODIs and T20Is. It assigns points based on match results, player contributions, and the strength of opposition. Updates are released after each international series.

Marco Jansen, a 27‑year‑old left‑arm fast‑bowler, made his Test debut in 2021 and quickly rose through the ranks thanks to his ability to generate bounce and swing. Prior to this update, he was ranked third among Test bowlers.

Abhishek Sharma, a right‑handed opening batsman for India, first entered the T20I top ten in 2022. His aggressive style, highlighted by a 35‑ball century against New Zealand, helped him reclaim the No.1 position after briefly losing it to England’s batsman earlier in the year.

What happens next

The ICC will publish the next set of rankings after the upcoming series between Pakistan and England in December. Performances in those matches could shift the positions of Pakistani players and potentially bring a local name into the top five.

Analysts expect that Jansen and Sharma will aim to consolidate their leads, while Pakistan’s coaching staff will use the data to fine‑tune strategies ahead of the World Cup qualifiers later next year.
