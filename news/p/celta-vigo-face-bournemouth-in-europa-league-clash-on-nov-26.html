Celta Vigo face Bournemouth in Europa League clash on Nov 26

Celta Vigo travel to Bournemouth for a decisive Europa League tie on 26 November. Analysts weigh recent performances, historical meetings and betting markets ahead of the match.

Latest developments

Celta Vigo will travel to the Vitality Stadium on Tuesday 26 November for the second leg of their Europa League tie against Bournemouth. The fixture follows a 2-2 draw in the first leg in Vigo, leaving the aggregate score level and setting up a decisive encounter. Both clubs have been preparing intensively, with Celta focusing on tightening their defence after conceding two goals at home, while Bournemouth aim to capitalize on the home advantage to secure a place in the next round.

Statistical analysis from the BBC highlights that the two sides have met only twice in European competition, with each side winning once. In domestic play, Celta have a win rate of 45% over their last ten La Liga matches, while Bournemouth have collected points in 60% of their recent Premier League outings. The head‑to‑head record in the current Europa League campaign shows both teams scoring two goals each, indicating a balanced contest.

Betting specialists at TNT Sports have released odds that suggest a narrow edge for Bournemouth, who are priced at 2.10 to win, compared with 3.30 for Celta Vigo. The over/under market is set at 2.5 goals, reflecting expectations of a tightly contested match. The bookmakers also note that both sides have struggled to keep clean sheets in the competition, which could influence the total goals line.

Sporting Life’s preview for Thursday, 26 November, adds that the match could be pivotal for the clubs’ European ambitions. A win for Bournemouth would mark their first progression beyond the group stage in recent years, while Celta Vigo would look to cement their reputation as a resilient side capable of advancing deep into the tournament. The preview also points out that weather conditions in Bournemouth are expected to be cool and damp, which may affect playing styles, particularly the passing game of the Spanish side.

The outcome will also influence the betting market, with many UK punters looking to back either side based on recent form and odds.

Background

Celta Vigo qualified for the Europa League after finishing fifth in La Liga last season, earning a place in the competition’s knockout phase. Their campaign began with a narrow victory over a Belgian side, followed by a hard‑fought draw against Bournemouth in the first leg. The Spanish club is known for its technical midfield play and has relied on a mix of experienced internationals and emerging talent to navigate the tournament.

Bournemouth earned their Europa League berth by finishing seventh in the Premier League, a historic achievement for the club. Their European debut has been marked by solid defensive organisation and a pragmatic approach to away matches. The Vitality Stadium, with a capacity of just over 11,000, provides an intimate atmosphere that can be intimidating for visiting teams. Both clubs have expressed a desire to make a deep run, viewing the competition as a platform to showcase their squads on a continental stage.

What happens next

The match will kick off at 20:00 GMT on 26 November. Should Bournemouth secure a win by a two‑goal margin or more, they will progress to the quarter‑finals without the need for extra time. If Celta Vigo either win outright or force a level aggregate, the tie will move to extra time and potentially a penalty shoot‑out, depending on the final scoreline. The winner will join the likes of Manchester United and Roma in the next round of the Europa League.
