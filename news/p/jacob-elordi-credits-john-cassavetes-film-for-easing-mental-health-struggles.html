Background

Jacob Elordi is best known for his role as Felix in the HBO series Euphoria, which has made him a household name in the US. Before that, he gained recognition for his work in Australian cinema and his role in the film The Kissing Booth. His career has seen a rapid ascent, placing him in the spotlight and subjecting him to the pressures that often accompany fame.

John Cassavetes was an American actor, director, and producer who is widely regarded as one of the most influential figures in independent cinema. His films, including Opening Night, are known for their improvisational style, raw emotional intensity, and focus on complex human relationships. Cassavetes’ work often challenged the conventions of Hollywood, offering a more realistic and sometimes uncomfortable look at life.

Opening Night, released in 1977, stars Gena Rowlands and Burt Reynolds. The film follows a famous actress who begins to unravel after a fan attacks her on stage. It is a deeply personal film for Cassavetes, dealing with themes of fame, identity, and mental instability. The film’s reputation for emotional depth makes it a fitting choice for someone reflecting on their own psychological state.
