Johnny Depp headlines London premiere of ‘Ebenezer’ amid mixed critic forecasts

Hollywood star Johnny Depp leads the new comedy‑thriller ‘Ebenezer’, which opened in London this week. Traders on the Kalshi platform predict a modest Rotten Tomatoes score, sparking debate among fans in Pakistan.

Latest developments

The comedy‑thriller ‘Ebenezer’ opened its first public screening on Monday at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square in London. The event was attended by the film's full cast, including Johnny Depp, who was photographed greeting fans and fellow actors on the red carpet. Organisers described the premiere as a "celebration of a fresh take on a classic character" and noted that the venue was sold out within hours of ticket release. Early audience reactions posted on social media highlighted the film's blend of dark humor and suspense, with many commenters noting Depp's energetic performance as the titular Ebenezer.

Johnny Depp's involvement in ‘Ebenezer’ marks his most prominent role since the high‑profile legal disputes that dominated headlines over the past two years. In a recent interview, Depp spoke about the personal significance of returning to a leading part, describing it as an opportunity to reconnect with his craft after a period of intense public scrutiny. Industry analysts have pointed to the film as a potential turning point in his career, suggesting that a successful reception could restore his standing in Hollywood.

Financial markets focused on the film's prospective critical reception after Kalshi traders posted odds indicating a predicted Rotten Tomatoes score of 36.8 percent, classifying the movie as likely to receive a "rotten" rating. The prediction reflects a cautious sentiment among investors who track entertainment outcomes, and it has been cited in several financial news outlets as an early indicator of the film's reception. While the exact methodology behind the odds was not disclosed, the figure has sparked discussion among cinema enthusiasts about the reliability of market‑based forecasts for artistic works.

The premiere also generated considerable buzz on social platforms. The post quickly amassed thousands of likes and comments, many of which came from users in Pakistan expressing curiosity about the film's availability on local streaming services. The online conversation has been amplified by the broader interest in Depp's comeback and the nostalgic appeal of the Ebenezer character.

Background

The name Ebenezer originates from Charles Dickens' 1843 novella "A Christmas Carol," where Ebenezer Scrooge is depicted as a miserly figure who undergoes a moral transformation. Over the years, the character has been reinterpreted in film, theatre, and television, often emphasizing themes of redemption and social critique. The new film departs from the traditional holiday setting, positioning Ebenezer in a contemporary comedy‑thriller narrative that blends satire with suspense.

Johnny Depp, known for his eclectic role choices, previously portrayed Scrooge in the 2009 musical adaptation "Scrooge: A Christmas Carol," bringing a flamboyant edge to the classic role. His recent legal battles, which involved a high‑profile defamation case, kept him largely out of major releases for an extended period. The decision to cast him in a leading role for ‘Ebenezer’ reflects both his personal desire to re‑establish his on‑screen presence and producers' confidence in his box‑office draw.

Such market‑based predictions have grown in visibility, offering an alternative perspective to traditional critic reviews.
