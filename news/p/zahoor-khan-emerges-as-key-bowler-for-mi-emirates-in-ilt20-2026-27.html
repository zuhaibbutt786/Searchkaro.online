MI Emirates, a franchise linked to the Indian Premier League's Mumbai Indians brand, entered the competition with a focus on blending experienced internationals with regional prospects.

Zahoor Khan, born in 1997 in Faisalabad, made his first‑class debut in 2018 and earned a reputation for delivering disciplined line and length. He broke into the national T20 side in 2022 and has since been a regular feature in Pakistan's limited‑overs squads. His inclusion in MI Emirates' roster reflects the franchise's strategy of securing proven Pakistani talent to complement its overseas signings.

Usha International, a leading consumer goods company, first partnered with MI Emirates in the 2024-25 season. The extension of this partnership for another two years signals confidence in the franchise's market reach and its ability to deliver brand exposure across South Asia.

The league's rule changes for 2026-27 were introduced after consultations with players' associations and broadcasters. The aim was to shorten match durations and increase the number of decisive moments, thereby enhancing spectator engagement both in stadiums and on digital platforms.
