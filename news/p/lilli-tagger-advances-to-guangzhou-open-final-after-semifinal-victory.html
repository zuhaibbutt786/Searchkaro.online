Lilli Tagger Advances to Guangzhou Open Final After Semifinal Victory

Background

The Guangzhou Open, officially known as the Galaxy Holding Group Guangzhou Open, is part of the WTA 250 series and takes place on hard courts in southern China. Established in 2004, the tournament has grown into a key stop on the Asian swing, offering players a chance to earn points and prize money before the season‑ending championships. Historically, the event has attracted a mix of rising stars and seasoned veterans, making it a proving ground for athletes looking to solidify their rankings.

Lilli Tagger, a 22‑year‑old from Germany, entered the tournament ranked just inside the top 50.
