Durefishan Saleem asks fans to respect Zaryab’s dress choice in TV drama

Durefishan Saleem has publicly defended Zaryab’s choice to appear in a sleeveless dress on the hit drama ‘Dar‑e‑Nijaat’. The comment has intensified discussions about costume standards in Pakistani media.

Latest developments

The latest episode of the drama ‘Dar‑e‑Nijaat’ featured lead actor Zaryab in a sleeveless dress, a visual choice that quickly became a flashpoint on social media. Viewers posted mixed reactions, with some praising the modern styling and others questioning its suitability for a family‑oriented series. Within hours, the clip was shared across Twitter, Instagram and Facebook, generating thousands of comments and memes that highlighted the divide among the audience.

In response, co‑star Durefishan Saleem issued a statement on her official Instagram account, urging fans to respect Zaryab’s artistic decision rather than judge it. She wrote that the focus should remain on the storyline and the performances, not on a single costume element. Her message was picked up by major Pakistani news portals, including Dawn and The Express Tribune, which reported her appeal for tolerance and highlighted the broader conversation about creative freedom.

Zaryab himself has not released a formal comment, but sources close to the production say he chose the outfit to reflect his character’s evolving confidence. The costume department explained that the sleeveless dress was intended to symbolize a turning point in the plot, where the character steps out of traditional expectations. Fans of the show split into two camps: one group applauded the bold visual cue, while another expressed discomfort, arguing that such attire clashes with cultural sensibilities.

Producers of ‘Dar‑e‑Nijaat’ have so far remained neutral, emphasizing that the series aims to portray contemporary issues faced by Pakistani families. However, a spokesperson confirmed that the team will monitor audience feedback closely as the season progresses. The controversy has also drawn attention from media analysts who note that similar debates have arisen in past Pakistani dramas when characters deviated from conventional dress codes.

Why it matters

The dispute touches on deeper questions about representation in Pakistani entertainment. Television remains a primary source of cultural influence, and costume choices can signal shifts in societal attitudes toward gender, modesty and modernity. By defending Zaryab’s outfit, Durefishan Saleem has highlighted the tension between artistic expression and audience expectations, prompting viewers to consider whether visual elements should dictate the merit of a story.

For advertisers and broadcasters, the debate is a reminder that content decisions can affect viewership ratings and brand perception. If a significant portion of the audience feels alienated, networks may face pressure to enforce stricter guidelines on on‑screen attire. Conversely, embracing progressive styling could attract younger demographics seeking fresh narratives. The outcome may shape future production choices across the Pakistani television industry.

‘Dar‑e‑Nijaat’ premiered earlier this year and quickly rose to become one of the most watched dramas on national channels. The series follows a family navigating social and economic challenges, with each episode tackling contemporary issues such as education, gender roles and personal ambition. Its popularity has made it a cultural touchstone, often referenced in public discourse.

Costume controversies are not new in Pakistani media. Past dramas have faced criticism for depicting characters in Western‑style clothing or for portraying relationships that clash with traditional values. What happens next

The production team is expected to address audience concerns in upcoming episodes, possibly by providing narrative context for the costume choice or by adjusting future wardrobe decisions.
