Namibia prepares for Cameroon clash as coach sits out

Namibia will meet Cameroon in a crucial African Cup of Nations qualifier while head coach Collin Benjamin is ruled out. The match will be shown live on beIN Sports, drawing interest from Pakistani viewers.

Latest developments

The African Cup of Nations qualification round brings Namibia and Cameroon together on 15 November 2026. Sports Mole’s preview outlines the expected line‑ups, noting that Namibia will likely field a mix of experienced internationals and emerging talent, while Cameroon is expected to rely on its seasoned attackers. A key development is the absence of Namibia’s head coach Collin Benjamin, who has been ruled out of the clash due to personal reasons, according to Pan Africa Football. The coaching vacancy will be filled by an interim manager who will oversee tactics on the day. Both nations are competing in the Group B stage of the AFCON 2027 qualifiers. The upcoming match therefore represents an opportunity for Namibia to test its progress against a traditionally stronger opponent, while Cameroon seeks to consolidate its position at the top of the group.
