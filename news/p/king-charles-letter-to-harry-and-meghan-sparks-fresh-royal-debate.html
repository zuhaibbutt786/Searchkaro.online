King Charles' letter to Harry and Meghan sparks fresh royal debate

King Charles sent a formal letter to Prince Harry and Meghan Markle stating they are no longer working royals. The correspondence has sparked widespread discussion about the couple's future and the monarchy's public image.

What is happening

King Charles issued a written statement to Prince Harry and Meghan Markle in which he confirmed that the couple are not considered working members of the royal household. The letter reiterated the expectations that senior royals must fulfill official duties and support the institution financially, and it noted that the Sussexes have chosen a different path.

According to multiple reports, the letter was delivered to Harry and Meghan with only an hour's notice before they were required to respond publicly. The short notice has been highlighted by commentators as a sign of the tension that exists between the palace and the Sussexes, who have been pursuing independent media projects since stepping back from senior roles.

British media outlets have framed the exchange as part of a larger narrative about the Sussexes' ongoing relationship with the press. Headlines emphasize the contrast between the royal family's traditional protocols and the couple's modern approach to public engagement, suggesting that the letter may influence future decisions about royal communications and public appearances.
