Bigg Boss Tamil 10: Anandhi's Father Passes Away, Eviction Sparks Shock

Bigg Boss Tamil is the Tamil‑language version of the global Bigg Boss franchise, airing on Star Vijay. The tenth season began in September 2026 and features a mix of celebrities and commoners competing in a house isolated from the outside world. Each season follows a similar format: contestants are evicted weekly based on audience votes and house nominations. The show has been popular for its blend of drama, social experiments, and celebrity appeal.
