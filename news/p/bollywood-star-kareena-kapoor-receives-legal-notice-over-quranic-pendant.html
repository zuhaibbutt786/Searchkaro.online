Bollywood star Kareena Kapoor receives legal notice over Quranic pendant

Kareena Kapoor was served a legal notice for wearing a pendant bearing Quranic verses. The incident has ignited a discussion on religious symbols in the Indian film industry.

Latest developments

On October 10, 2024, Kareena Kapoor, a leading Bollywood actress, received a legal notice from Congress leader Abbas, who cited a violation of religious sentiment after the star was photographed wearing a green pendant that displayed the Ayat‑ul‑Kursi, a verse from the Quran. The notice, sent through a registered post, demanded that Kapoor cease using the jewelry and publicly apologize for the perceived disrespect. Kapoor's publicist confirmed receipt of the notice but declined to comment on any forthcoming legal steps. Social media platforms quickly amplified the story, with users posting screenshots of the pendant and debating the appropriateness of religious text as fashion accessories. While some fans defended the actress's right to personal expression, others called for accountability, arguing that sacred verses should not be commercialized. The Congress party, through a spokesperson, clarified that the notice was a private legal action and not an official party directive, but emphasized the need for sensitivity towards religious symbols. Kapoor has not yet issued a formal response, though her team indicated that she is consulting legal counsel to assess the notice's validity under Indian law.

Why it matters

The controversy touches on two sensitive areas in India: freedom of expression and communal harmony. Legal experts note that while the Indian Constitution protects artistic freedom, it also prohibits actions that may incite religious disharmony. The case could set a precedent for how religious texts are used in popular culture, especially in an industry that reaches millions across diverse faiths. For the Indian audience, the incident raises questions about the boundaries of personal branding for celebrities and the responsibilities they bear when their choices intersect with deeply held beliefs. Moreover, the involvement of a political figure in a personal fashion dispute highlights the growing politicisation of cultural matters, which could influence future regulatory approaches to entertainment content.

Background

The pendant in question features a green enamel base with the Arabic inscription of Ayat‑ul‑Kursi, a verse often recited for protection in Islamic tradition. Similar incidents have occurred in the past, such as the 2021 backlash against a music video that displayed Hindu deities in a commercial context, which led to legal challenges and public protests. Indian law, particularly the Indian Penal Code sections dealing with hurting religious sentiments, has been invoked in several high‑profile cases involving celebrities. The entertainment industry has traditionally navigated these waters by consulting cultural advisors, but the rapid spread of digital media makes it harder to control public perception. The Congress leader who issued the notice, Abbas, is known for advocating stricter enforcement of religious respect, especially during festival seasons.

What happens next

Kareena Kapoor is expected to file a response within the statutory period, possibly seeking a stay on the notice while the matter is examined in court. Legal analysts predict that the case may be settled out of court if the actress agrees to remove the pendant from public appearances and issue a brief apology. If the dispute proceeds, it could result in a judicial ruling that clarifies the limits of religious expression in fashion, potentially influencing future guidelines for the film and advertising sectors.
