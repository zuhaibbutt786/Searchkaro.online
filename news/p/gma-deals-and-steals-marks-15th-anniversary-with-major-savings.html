GMA Deals and Steals Marks 15th Anniversary with Major Savings

Good Morning America is marking 15 years of its Deals and Steals segment with significant price reductions. Viewers can find up to 70 percent off on various consumer products through ABC Secret Savings.

Latest developments

Good Morning America has announced a special initiative to celebrate the 15th anniversary of its long-running consumer shopping segment, Deals and Steals. The milestone event features curated collections of products with enhanced discounts, aiming to provide viewers with substantial savings on everyday items. The segment, which has become a staple of the morning broadcast, is expanding its promotional reach to include a wider variety of categories.

According to recent announcements from ABC News, the anniversary celebration includes access to ABC Secret Savings, a platform that offers deep discounts on specific merchandise. Reports indicate that beauty products and fashion accessories are among the top categories featured in this promotional wave. Discounts of up to 70 percent are being advertised, reflecting a significant effort to attract budget-conscious shoppers during the anniversary period.

Video content accompanying the news highlights has showcased items from the home and kitchen sectors, further broadening the scope of the deals. These segments are designed to inform viewers of the specific products available at reduced prices, creating a direct link between the on-air broadcast and the online shopping experience. The integration of video previews allows audiences to see the items in context before making a purchase decision.

The promotion is being distributed across multiple ABC News channels, including local affiliates like ABC7 San Francisco, ensuring that the message reaches a diverse geographic audience. This multi-channel approach underscores the national scale of the anniversary campaign, moving beyond a single market to engage viewers across the United States.
