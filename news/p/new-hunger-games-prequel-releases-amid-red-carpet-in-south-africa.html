New Hunger Games Prequel Releases Amid Red Carpet in South Africa

The new Hunger Games prequel, 'Sunrise on the Reaping,' has opened to mixed reviews, while the original cast reunited on a South African red carpet. Fans and critics alike are watching how the story expands the franchise.

Latest developments

Released in early 2026, 'The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping' follows the origin story of the mentor figure Haymitch Abernathy, set before the original trilogy. The film is part of the expanding franchise that began with Suzanne Collins' novels. It was distributed by a major studio and premiered in several international markets, including South Africa, where the series has a dedicated fan base. A review in Variety described the prequel as less lethal than its predecessor, 'Songbirds & Snakes,' noting that the narrative focuses on character development rather than action. The critic highlighted the film's pacing as meandering but still watchable, praising the performances that bring depth to the early life of Haymitch. The Hollywood Reporter offered a contrasting view, calling the film a rousing prequel that successfully sets up the world of Panem. The review emphasized the director's ability to balance exposition with moments of tension, and commended the supporting cast for grounding the story. Meanwhile, a Yahoo article reported that the original cast, including Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson, gathered for a red‑carpet event in Johannesburg. The gathering was covered by local media and attracted fans who had followed the series since its debut. The reunion underscored the enduring popularity of the franchise in South Africa.

Background

The Hunger Games series, created by Suzanne Collins, debuted in 2008 and quickly became a cultural phenomenon. The story follows Katniss Everdeen as she navigates a dystopian society where children are forced to compete in televised death matches. In 2023, the franchise released 'The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes,' a prequel that explored the early days of the Games and introduced new characters. The film received mixed reactions, with some praising its ambition while others felt it lacked the intensity of the original trilogy. Building on that momentum, the latest prequel focuses on Haymitch Abernathy, the former victor who later becomes a mentor. The film aims to provide a deeper look at the mentor’s formative years and the political climate that shaped Panem.
