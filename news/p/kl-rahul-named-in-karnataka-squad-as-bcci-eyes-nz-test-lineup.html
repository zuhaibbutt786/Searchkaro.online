KL Rahul named in Karnataka squad as BCCI eyes NZ Test lineup

KL Rahul has been named in Karnataka's Ranji Trophy side for the opening match against Andhra. The selection comes as the BCCI reviews his form before the upcoming New Zealand Test series.

Latest developments

The Karnataka Cricket Association announced on Monday that KL Rahul and fast bowler Prasidh Krishna will feature in the team's opening Ranji Trophy fixture against Andhra Pradesh. The decision was confirmed in a squad list released by the association, which also highlighted the inclusion of three players from Mysuru, underscoring the region's growing contribution to the state side. At the same time, the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) has signalled that Rahul, along with Shubman Gill and Jasprit Bumrah, will be under close observation as the board prepares for the four‑match Test series against New Zealand later this year. Analysts note that Rahul's recent domestic performances could influence his placement in the Test XI, especially given the competitive nature of the selection pool. The upcoming Ranji match, scheduled for early October, will provide a platform for Rahul to demonstrate his readiness for the longer format ahead of the international fixtures.

Why it matters

Cricket fans in Pakistan follow Indian team selections closely, as the two nations share a historic rivalry and a large viewership across the subcontinent. Rahul's involvement in the Ranji opener signals that the BCCI is giving him ample opportunity to regain form before the high‑stakes New Zealand series, which could affect the balance of power in the World Test Championship. A strong performance by Rahul may also influence the strategic approach of Pakistan's own Test side, which will meet India in future series and will need to prepare for a potential opponent who combines aggressive batting with a flexible middle order. Moreover, the inclusion of Mysuru players highlights the depth of talent emerging from smaller cricketing hubs, a trend that could reshape talent pipelines in both countries.

Background

KL Rahul, a right‑handed batsman and occasional wicket‑keeper, has been a regular feature in India's limited‑overs squads but has faced intermittent selection in the Test arena. His last extended run in the Test side ended in 2023, after which he focused on domestic cricket to rebuild his credentials. The Ranji Trophy, India's premier first‑class competition, serves as a proving ground for players seeking national recall. Karnataka, a traditional powerhouse in the tournament, has a history of producing international stars, and its current squad blends experienced internationals with emerging talent from cities like Mysuru. The BCCI's current strategic focus includes ensuring a balanced Test lineup capable of competing for the World Test Championship final, a goal that places additional scrutiny on players like Rahul.

What happens next

The Karnataka team will face Andhra Pradesh in the Ranji opener later this week, with Rahul expected to bat in the top order. His performance, along with that of Prasidh Krishna, will be monitored by the national selectors ahead of the New Zealand Test series scheduled for December. If Rahul produces a substantial innings, he could be shortlisted for the Test squad, where he would compete with Shubman Gill and other established batsmen for a place in the playing XI.
