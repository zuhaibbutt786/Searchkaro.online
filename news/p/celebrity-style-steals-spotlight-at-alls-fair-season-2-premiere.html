Celebrity Style Steals Spotlight at 'All's Fair' Season 2 Premiere

Celebrity fashion dominated the 'All's Fair' Season 2 premiere in New York City. Kim Kardashian’s painstakingly crafted corset gown and Naomi Watts’ look were among the most talked‑about outfits.

Latest developments

The second season of the drama series "All's Fair" opened on Thursday at the upscale Cipriani Wall Street venue in Manhattan, drawing a crowd of industry insiders, press, and a roster of high‑profile guests. The event marked the first public screening of the season‑two pilot and featured a red‑carpet walk that quickly became a focal point for fashion commentary. Organisers highlighted the show's growing cultural relevance, noting that the series has been praised for its sharp dialogue and contemporary themes.

Kim Kardashian arrived in a custom‑made corset gown that required an estimated 500 hours of hand‑sewing by ASHI Studio, a detail confirmed by the studio’s lead designer. The garment, constructed from a blend of silk organza and hand‑stitched steel boning, featured a dramatic silhouette that emphasized the actress’s signature silhouette. Media outlets reported that the gown’s intricate latticework and sculptural back design set a new benchmark for red‑carpet couture, prompting several fashion analysts to predict a surge in similar high‑craft pieces for upcoming award seasons.

Among the other notable attendees, Naomi Watts wore a sleek, midnight‑blue sheath dress accented with minimalist jewelry, drawing praise for its understated elegance. The premiere also saw a surprising moment when veteran actress Glenn Close, accompanied by a six‑foot impersonator, made a brief appearance that prompted photographers to request a second shot. The duo’s playful interaction was covered by CNN as a light‑hearted highlight of the evening, underscoring the event’s blend of serious promotion and celebrity fun.

Social media platforms lit up with images and commentary within minutes of the curtain call. Trending hashtags such as #AllsFairNYC and #KardashianCouture amassed millions of impressions, while fashion blogs dissected the technical aspects of Kardashian’s gown and the overall styling choices of the night. The buzz extended beyond the entertainment sphere, with several luxury retailers noting an uptick in inquiries for custom‑made evening wear, suggesting the premiere’s influence may ripple through the broader apparel market.

Background

"All's Fair" debuted in 2024 as a contemporary drama exploring power dynamics in corporate America, earning critical acclaim for its sharp writing and strong ensemble cast. The first season concluded with a cliffhanger that left viewers debating the moral choices of its protagonists, prompting a surge in online discussion and solidifying the series as a talking point among both critics and casual viewers.

Season two, announced earlier this year, promises to deepen the narrative by introducing new characters, including Naomi Watts, and expanding the storyline into political intrigue. The show’s creators have emphasized a commitment to authentic set design and realistic dialogue, aiming to reflect current societal debates. The New York City premiere serves as both a marketing push and a cultural statement, aligning the series with the city’s reputation as a hub for artistic innovation.
