Latest developments

On September 29, 2026, Argentine defender Juan Foyth was omitted from the national team’s latest call‑up for upcoming friendly matches, according to a report from beIN Sports. The 27‑year‑old, who plays for Villarreal in Spain’s La Liga, remained with his club to focus on rehabilitation after a lingering muscle issue was identified during pre‑season training. The Argentine Football Association (AFA) confirmed that Foyth would not travel with the squad, citing the need for a full medical clearance before he can represent the country again. Earlier in the week, a separate alarm was raised by River Plate and Argentine medical officials after a training session revealed a more serious injury concern affecting multiple players, as reported by Yahoo Sports. The incident prompted the club’s medical team to conduct a comprehensive assessment of the squad, including the national team contingent that gathers at River’s facilities. The report highlighted that the injury could limit the availability of several key players for the friendlies, adding pressure on the coaching staff to adjust line‑ups. Despite the initial exclusion, later statements from Comuniate indicated that Foyth rejoined the national group after receiving clearance from both his club doctors and the AFA medical panel. However, his participation remains uncertain as Villarreal continues to evaluate his fitness for club duties. At the same time, Villarreal is awaiting a medical report on midfielder Comesana, whose injury status was also mentioned in the same Comuniate article, leaving his involvement in the upcoming fixtures pending. The Argentine side is scheduled to play a series of friendlies in Europe and South America over the next month, with matches against Portugal, Mexico and the United States on the calendar. These games are viewed as preparation for the 2026 World Cup and are expected to attract significant viewership in Pakistan, where Argentine football enjoys a growing fan base. The squad’s composition, therefore, is under close scrutiny by both local broadcasters and betting platforms.

Background

Argentina’s national team traditionally uses friendly matches to fine‑tune tactics and integrate younger talent ahead of major tournaments. The current cycle, leading up to the 2026 FIFA World Cup in the United States, sees head coach Lionel Scaloni balancing the inclusion of experienced European‑based players with home‑grown prospects. Juan Foyth, a product of the Argentine youth system, moved to Europe in 2016 and has been a regular fixture in the national side since his debut in 2020, making his fitness a key factor for the defensive setup. Villarreal, the Spanish club that signed Foyth in 2022, has been managing a spate of injuries across its squad, a situation not uncommon in a congested season that includes domestic league, cup and European commitments. Comesana, a promising midfielder signed by Villarreal in the summer, suffered a hamstring strain during pre‑season, and his recovery timeline has been subject to ongoing medical evaluation. Both players’ conditions have been closely monitored by the AFA, which maintains a liaison with clubs to ensure players are cleared for international duty.

What happens next

Coaching staff are expected to field a blend of seasoned veterans and emerging talents, while keeping a close eye on Foyth’s training progress. Villarreal is slated to release an official update on Comesana’s status within the next week, after which the AFA will confirm his availability for the remaining fixtures.
