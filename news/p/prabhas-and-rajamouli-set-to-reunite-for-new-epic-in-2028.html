Prabhas and Rajamouli Set to Reunite for New Epic in 2028

The duo of Prabhas and S. S. Rajamouli are poised to launch a new blockbuster in 2028 after the triumph of Baahubali 3. Fans and industry insiders are watching closely as production plans unfold.

Latest developments

Following the blockbuster success of Baahubali 3, director S. S. Rajamouli and actor Prabhas have confirmed a new joint venture set for a 2028 release. The film is described as a large‑scale epic that will feature extensive sets and advanced visual effects. Production is expected to commence in early 2025, with pre‑production work already underway.

A recent article by Chitrajyothy mentions a potential 2030 update for the project, but the primary focus remains a 2028 launch. The director has hinted at a fresh storyline that will build on the themes explored in their previous collaborations.

Disha Daily reports that the upcoming film will be a massive production, with a budget that rivals the biggest Indian movies of the decade. The release is being positioned as a major event for the film industry, with expectations of high box‑office returns.

Namasthe Telangana notes that the partnership between Prabhas and Rajamouli is expected to continue beyond 2028, suggesting a long‑term collaboration that could produce further high‑profile projects.

Why it matters

The new project is poised to generate significant box‑office revenue, potentially surpassing the earnings of Baahubali 3. It will also create employment opportunities for technicians, set designers, and other crew members across the country.

By investing in a high‑budget epic, the film industry signals confidence in large‑scale productions, encouraging other filmmakers to pursue ambitious projects. This trend could raise production standards and international visibility for Indian cinema.

For fans, the reunion of Prabhas and Rajamouli offers a sense of nostalgia and excitement, driving engagement on social media, merchandise sales, and fan events. The anticipation is already reflected in online discussions and pre‑sale ticket interest.

Background

Prabhas, whose real name is Uppalapati Venkata Suryanarayana, gained worldwide recognition with the Baahubali franchise, which became the highest‑grossing Indian film series at the time of its release. His portrayal of Amarendra Baahubali and Mahendra Baahubali cemented his status as a leading action star.

S. S. Rajamouli is celebrated for his visionary direction and mastery of visual effects. His films, such as Eega and Baahubali, have set new benchmarks for storytelling and technical execution in Indian cinema.

The duo's previous collaborations have consistently delivered commercial success and critical acclaim, establishing a brand that attracts large audiences and substantial investment.

What happens next

Production is slated to begin in early 2025, with the team finalising the script and casting additional roles. Key crew members, including the cinematographer and music director, are expected to join the project shortly after the pre‑production phase.

Marketing activities are planned to kick off in 2027, featuring teaser releases, promotional events, and partnerships with streaming platforms. The film’s official release is targeted for late 2028, aligning with the end‑of‑year holiday season.
