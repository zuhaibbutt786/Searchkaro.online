TV Star Nandish Sandhu Marries Kavita Banerjee in Bengali Wedding

TV actor Nandish Sandhu wed Kavita Banerjee in a Bengali ceremony held in Hooghly, West Bengal. The multiday celebrations have captured interest among viewers in Pakistan.

Latest developments

Indian television actor Nandish Sandhu, previously known for his role in the popular series 'Bade Achhe Lagte Hain', married Kavita Banerjee in a traditional Bengali ceremony on Saturday. The wedding took place at a family residence in Hooghly, a district in West Bengal, and followed the customary rituals of a Bengali wedding, including the 'Saat Paake' and 'Bidaai' ceremonies. Guests included colleagues from the entertainment industry, as well as members of the Bengali community. The celebrations extended over three days, featuring a reception, cultural performances, and a grand feast that highlighted regional cuisine. Media outlets reported that the couple exchanged vows in front of a small gathering before the larger public events began. Social media platforms lit up with congratulatory messages, and the couple’s Instagram accounts shared a series of photographs showcasing the bride’s red and white saree and the groom’s traditional dhoti. The hashtag #SandhuBanerjeeWedding trended locally, drawing comments from fans across South Asia.

Why it matters

Pakistani audiences have shown heightened interest in the wedding, as reflected by a surge in online searches for the couple’s names and for Bengali wedding customs. The event offers viewers in Pakistan a glimpse into the cultural practices of eastern India, which differ from the more widely televised North Indian ceremonies. Television channels and digital platforms in Pakistan have begun featuring short clips and photo galleries, catering to the curiosity sparked by the high-profile union. Moreover, the marriage highlights the growing cross‑border fascination with Indian entertainment personalities, a trend that influences advertising strategies and viewership patterns in the region. Fashion designers in Pakistan have already begun referencing the bridal attire in upcoming collections, indicating the wedding’s influence on regional bridal wear trends.

Background

The practice of Bengali weddings is known for its distinct rituals, such as the 'Shubho Drishti' where the bride and groom exchange glances, and the 'Mala Badal' ceremony involving the exchange of garlands. These customs have their roots in ancient Hindu traditions and have been adapted over centuries in West Bengal and Bangladesh. Nandish Sandhu, who rose to fame through Hindi television serials, previously married actress Rashami Desai in 2015; the couple divorced in 2022. His subsequent marriage to Kavita Banerjee, a non‑celebrity from Kolkata, marks his second marriage and reflects a personal choice to embrace regional traditions. The Hooghly venue, known for its historic temples, added cultural significance to the proceedings. In addition to the rituals, Bengali weddings are renowned for their elaborate music ensembles, known as 'Baul' performances, and for serving sweets such as 'Mishti Doi' and 'Rasgulla' to guests. These elements reinforce communal bonds and are often highlighted in media portrayals of the region’s cultural heritage.
