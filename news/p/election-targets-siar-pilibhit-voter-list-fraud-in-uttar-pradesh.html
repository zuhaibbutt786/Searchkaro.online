Election Targets SIAR, Pilibhit Voter List Fraud in Uttar Pradesh

The Election Commission has intensified its scrutiny of the State Election and Registration Authority after allegations of irregularities. Meanwhile, voter list fraud in Pilibhit and duplicate names in Kannauj are prompting administrative a

Latest developments

The Election Commission of India has opened a formal inquiry into the State Election and Registration Authority (SIAR) after a BBC report highlighted a series of administrative lapses and alleged irregularities in the conduct of local body elections. The commission cited concerns over the handling of voter rolls, the allocation of election resources, and the transparency of the candidate nomination process. If the findings confirm misconduct, SIAR officials could face suspension or removal, and the commission may impose stricter oversight for the next election cycle.

In Pilibhit district, the Election Commission has taken action against two Sub-Divisional Magistrates (SDMs) following allegations of voter list fraud that surfaced in an AajTak article. The Chief Electoral Officer issued a stern warning and suspended the officials, while a First Information Report (FIR) was filed against them. The case also involves a Member of Legislative Council (MLC) who is accused of facilitating the inclusion of duplicate names on the voter list, a move that could distort election outcomes.

Meanwhile, the city of Kannauj is conducting a campaign to scrub duplicate entries from its voter register, as reported by Dainik Bhaskar. Officials have announced that duplicate names will be removed by the end of the day, and a revised list will be published on 12 November. The initiative is part of a broader effort to ensure that the electoral roll accurately reflects the eligible voting population.

Why it matters

For voters across Uttar Pradesh, the investigations raise questions about the integrity of the electoral process. If voter rolls are found to be compromised, it could undermine confidence in the results of upcoming local elections and the legitimacy of elected representatives.

The scrutiny also signals a broader push by the Election Commission to tighten electoral norms in the country’s most populous state. By holding officials accountable, the commission aims to deter future misconduct and restore public trust in democratic institutions.

Background

The State Election and Registration Authority was established to oversee elections to local bodies and to maintain voter rolls. Over the past decade, several states have faced criticism for irregularities in the preparation and updating of electoral registers, prompting central intervention.

Uttar Pradesh has a history of electoral controversies, including allegations of vote rigging and manipulation of voter lists in previous assembly elections. The current investigations are the latest example of the central government’s effort to address systemic issues in the state’s electoral administration.

What happens next

The Election Commission plans to conduct a full audit of SIAR’s operations and will publish its findings within the next 30 days. In Pilibhit, the case is expected to be forwarded to the police for further investigation, and the commission may impose penalties if the officials are found guilty. The Kannauj voter list update will be monitored closely, with a final audit scheduled before the next local election.

Summary

- SIAR under investigation for alleged irregularities in local elections. - Pilibhit SDMs suspended over voter list fraud; FIR filed against officials and an MLC. - Kannauj to remove duplicate voter names; updated list due 12 November.
