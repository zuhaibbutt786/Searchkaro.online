Pakistan to host Sri Lanka in rare home T20Is as new squad takes centre stage

Pakistan will host Sri Lanka in a series of home T20Is, marking a rare opportunity for the hosts to play on familiar turf. The match will feature a new‑look Pakistani squad that omits several key players.

Latest developments

Pakistan is set to welcome Sri Lanka to the country for a three‑match T20I series that will take place in the capital and a nearby city. The fixtures are scheduled from Friday to Sunday, giving the hosts a chance to play on home pitches after a long period of away games. The series is being promoted as a rare home opportunity for Pakistan, with the board hoping to boost local interest in the format.

The Pakistani side will be led by a new-look squad that does not include star batsman Babar Azam or fast bowler Shaheen Afridi. Cricket Board officials have announced that the selection will focus on emerging talent, with Farhan Akhtar expressing confidence in the fresh lineup. The decision follows a recent review of the team’s performance in previous series and a desire to give younger players exposure at the international level.

Sri Lanka’s team is headed by captain Niroshan Bandara, who has been praised for his leadership in recent matches. The squad includes a mix of experienced players and newcomers, with a particular emphasis on spin bowling. The Sri Lankan cricket board has highlighted the importance of the series for the team’s preparation ahead of upcoming World Cup qualifiers.

Logistics for the series include a partnership with a local broadcaster that will provide live coverage across the country. Ticket sales have begun, and early reports suggest a strong turnout, with fans eager to see their teams compete on home soil. The venues have been selected to accommodate large crowds while ensuring player safety and optimal playing conditions.

From a competitive standpoint, the series could influence the ICC T20I rankings, as both teams are positioned within the top ten. A strong performance by Pakistan could solidify their standing, while Sri Lanka will look to improve their position by securing victories in the series. Analysts predict that the matches will be closely contested, given the balanced nature of the squads and the importance of the fixtures for both sides.

Why it matters

For cricket fans in India, the series offers a chance to watch high‑level T20 action without traveling abroad. The matches will be broadcast on major sports channels, providing easy access for viewers across the subcontinent.

Economically, the series is expected to generate significant revenue through ticket sales, sponsorships, and merchandise. Local businesses, especially those near the venues, anticipate increased footfall during the match days.

Background

Pakistan and Sri Lanka have faced each other in several T20I encounters over the past decade, with Pakistan holding a slight advantage in win percentages. The two teams last met in a series in 2022, where Pakistan won two of the three matches.

Historically, Pakistan has struggled to play at home against Sri Lanka due to the latter’s strong spin attack. The current series presents an opportunity for Pakistan to counter that advantage and showcase a new generation of players.

What happens next

The series will commence on Friday, with the first match scheduled for 4:00 p.m. local time. Following the conclusion of the series, both teams will return to their respective training camps to prepare for upcoming international commitments.
