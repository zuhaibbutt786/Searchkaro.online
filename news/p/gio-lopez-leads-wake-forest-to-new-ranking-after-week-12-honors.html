Gio Lopez Leads Wake Forest to New Ranking After Week 12 Honors

Gio Lopez was named the standout player in Wake Forest's Week 12 honors ceremony. The performance contributed to the team's rise in the national rankings.

Latest developments

Wake Forest University announced that senior quarterback Gio Lopez was the headline recipient of the program's Week 12 honors on Saturday. Lopez was recognized for his leadership and statistical output in the recent matchup, where he threw for over 300 yards and accounted for three touchdowns. The accolade coincided with the Demon Deacons moving up in the College Football Playoff (CFP) rankings, as reported by the Winston‑Salem Journal. The team advanced one spot, positioning itself closer to the top 25 threshold that influences bowl selections. While Wake Forest celebrated its progress, the broader college football community noted that Miami's running back Mensah was named a finalist for the Davey O'Brien Award, highlighting the competitive talent pool across the Atlantic Coast Conference. Both stories underscore a week of individual recognition that could shape postseason narratives.
