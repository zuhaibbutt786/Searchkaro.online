Sandeep Lamichhane Leads Nepal’s Squad to Qatar Rising Stars Asia Cup

Sandeep Lamichhane, Nepal’s star spinner, will lead the national side to the ACC Rising Stars Asia Cup in Qatar. The team departs after Sompal Kami withdraws due to injury.

Latest developments

The Nepal national cricket team has departed for Doha, Qatar, to compete in the ACC Men’s Asia Cup Rising Stars Tournament. The squad, assembled by the Cricket Association of Nepal, is set to play a series of matches over the coming week, with the opening game scheduled for the 12th of November. The departure follows the announcement that Sompal Kami, a key all‑rounder, will miss the competition due to a shoulder injury sustained during a domestic match. The injury has forced the selectors to adjust the squad, bringing in replacements to fill the void left by Kami’s absence.

Sandeep Lamichhane, the world‑famous leg‑spinner, has been named the captain of the team for the tournament. Lamichhane’s leadership comes at a time when Nepal is looking to build on its recent successes in regional cricket. His experience in franchise leagues across the globe, including the Big Bash League and the Caribbean Premier League, is expected to provide a tactical edge against stronger opponents.

The team’s itinerary includes practice sessions at the Khalifa International Stadium, where they will acclimatise to the hot and humid conditions typical of Qatar. The coaching staff has planned a series of net sessions focusing on spin bowling and batting under pressure. The squad will also meet with local cricket officials to discuss logistics and security arrangements.

While the team’s departure is a routine step for an international tournament, the absence of Sompal Kami has raised questions about the balance of the side. Kami’s injury has highlighted the need for depth in the squad, and the selectors have indicated that they will monitor the performance of the new players closely. The team’s management has expressed confidence that the squad will maintain its competitive edge despite the change in personnel.

Background

Sandeep Lamichhane was born on 30 October 1995 in Kathmandu, Nepal. He burst onto the international scene in 2014 when he made his One Day International debut against Bangladesh. Since then, Lamichhane has become Nepal’s most successful bowler, with a record of taking wickets in every major tournament the country has entered.

Lamichhane’s success in franchise cricket has been a source of pride for Nepal. He has played for teams such as the Melbourne Renegades, the Chennai Super Kings, and the St Lucia Zouks, earning recognition for his ability to bowl accurate leg‑breaks under pressure. His achievements have helped raise Nepal’s profile in the cricketing world and have encouraged investment in the sport back home.

The ACC Rising Stars Asia Cup is a relatively new tournament designed to give emerging cricket nations a chance to compete against each other. Nepal’s participation in the 2023 edition follows a series of strong performances in regional qualifiers, and the team is eager to build on its momentum.

What happens next

The Nepal team will play its first match against the United Arab Emirates on 12 November. The match will be followed by games against Hong Kong, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh over the next few days. The team’s performance in these matches will determine whether it can progress to the knockout stages.

Lamichhane is expected to bowl in the opening overs of the first match, with the coaching staff hoping to use his experience to set a strong foundation for the team. The selectors will also monitor the performance of the new players who have been brought in to replace Sompal Kami, assessing whether they can fill the gap left by the injured all‑rounder.
