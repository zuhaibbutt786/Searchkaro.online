South Korea to Host Venezuela in FIFA‑Date Friendly in Ulsan

South Korea and Venezuela will meet in a FIFA‑date friendly in Ulsan on 2 October. The match will be broadcast live on television and streamed for free online.

Latest developments

The South Korean Football Association confirmed that the national team will host Venezuela for a friendly match on 2 October in the coastal city of Ulsan. The fixture is part of the FIFA international match calendar and marks the second friendly for both sides this year. Organisers have scheduled the game for a prime‑time slot that will suit Asian audiences, and the venue will be the Ulsan Munsu Football Stadium, a venue that previously hosted World Cup matches. The match will be overseen by a FIFA‑appointed referee crew, and ticket sales are expected to open next week, with a portion of seats reserved for local fans and diplomatic delegations.

Fans looking for ways to watch the encounter can tune in to local broadcasters that have secured the rights for the Asian market. In Pakistan, the match will be shown on the sports channel PTV Sports, while a free online stream will be available through the official KFA YouTube channel and the FIFA streaming platform. Venezuela’s national side, known locally as "La Vinotinto" (the Red Wine), is approaching the friendly with a clear focus on building cohesion ahead of the upcoming World Cup qualifiers. The team’s coaching staff has used the first FIFA‑date friendly earlier in the month to test a new defensive formation, and the Ulsan match will serve as a second opportunity to evaluate attacking options. According to a report from Noticias Barquisimeto, the Venezuelan squad will travel to South Korea on 30 September, allowing a brief acclimatisation period before the game.
