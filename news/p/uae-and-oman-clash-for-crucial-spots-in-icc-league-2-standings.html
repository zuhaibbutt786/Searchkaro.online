Background

The ICC Cricket World Cup League 2 was introduced as part of the qualification structure for the 2027 Cricket World Cup. It features thirteen associate nations playing a series of One Day International (ODI) matches over several years. The top four teams at the end of the league automatically advance to the World Cup Qualifier, while the remaining teams must navigate a play‑off system that includes the likes of Jersey and Kuwait, who recently kept their hopes alive.

UAE entered the league with a reputation for strong home performances and a growing pool of talent, while Oman has steadily improved its ranking through consistent results against comparable opponents. Both teams have previously qualified for ICC events, but the current cycle presents a tighter race due to the rise of other associate nations.

The inclusion of Jersey and Kuwait in recent headlines underscores the depth of competition. Their advancement to the Qualifier play‑off demonstrates that no position in the league is secure, and that every point earned or lost can have a cascading effect on the final table.
