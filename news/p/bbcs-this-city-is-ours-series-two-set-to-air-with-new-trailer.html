BBC’s ‘This City Is Ours’ Series Two Set to Air with New Trailer

BBC’s latest drama ‘This City Is Ours’ is back with a new trailer and confirmed start date. Fans can expect the second series to air in the coming months.

Latest developments

BBC’s official media centre released a new trailer for the second series of the popular drama ‘This City Is Ours’, featuring updated artwork that hints at a darker tone for the upcoming episodes. The clip showcases the main cast returning to their roles, with a brief montage of the city’s streets and the characters’ personal struggles. In a statement accompanying the release, the broadcaster confirmed that the second season will begin airing in late autumn, aligning with the network’s fall schedule. Digital Spy reported that the series will launch on 15 September, giving viewers a precise date to anticipate. The announcement also noted that the production team will be filming on location in London, ensuring authenticity in the series’ setting.

The trailer’s release has generated excitement among fans, who have been waiting for a new season since the first series concluded last year. The artwork accompanying the clip features a stark, monochrome palette, suggesting a shift in the show’s visual style. BBC’s media centre highlighted that the new visuals will reflect the characters’ growing tensions and the city’s evolving atmosphere. The promotional material also includes a teaser of a new character, hinting at fresh storylines for the second season.

BBC’s official announcement states that the series will be available on the network’s flagship channel and on the streaming platform BBC iPlayer. Viewers will be able to stream the episodes as they air, with full episodes released the following day. The broadcaster also confirmed that the series will be available in both English and Welsh, catering to a wider audience across the UK. The new trailer has already been shared on social media, with the hashtag #ThisCityIsOurs trending on Twitter.

Digital Spy’s coverage of the release date added that the production team has been working on the second season for the past six months, with a focus on exploring new themes such as identity and belonging. The article also mentioned that the series will feature a new storyline that follows the characters as they navigate the city’s changing landscape. Fans can expect a mix of drama and suspense, with the show’s signature blend of character-driven storytelling.

Rayo’s announcement of the start date confirms that the second series will begin on 15 September, with the first episode airing at 9:30pm. The network’s website notes that the series will run for ten episodes, concluding in December. Viewers can expect a mix of new and returning characters, with the show’s core themes of community and resilience continuing to drive the narrative.
