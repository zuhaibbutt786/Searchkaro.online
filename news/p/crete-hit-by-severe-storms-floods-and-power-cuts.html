Crete hit by severe storms, floods and power cuts

Heavy rain and strong winds have caused significant disruption across the Greek island of Crete. Authorities report floods, landslides, and widespread power outages, with one fatality confirmed.

Latest developments

The Greek island of Crete is currently facing the aftermath of a severe weather system that brought intense rainfall and high winds to the region. The storm has left the island at the mercy of extreme conditions, resulting in localized flooding and multiple landslides that have damaged infrastructure and blocked roads. Emergency services have been working around the clock to manage the situation and assist residents stranded by the weather.

Tragically, the severe weather has claimed one life. An 80-year-old livestock farmer who had gone missing during the storm was found dead. His death highlights the dangers posed by the sudden and violent nature of the weather events affecting the area. Local authorities are continuing to search for any other individuals who may have been affected by the storms.

In addition to the physical damage, the power grid in parts of Crete has been severely impacted. Widespread power cuts have left many households and businesses without electricity, complicating relief efforts and daily life for residents. The combination of darkness and ongoing rain has increased the risk of further accidents and made rescue operations more difficult for emergency crews.

Educational institutions across the island have been forced to close their doors. Schools remain shut as a precautionary measure to ensure the safety of students and staff, given the hazardous conditions on the roads and the instability of some buildings due to the landslides. The closure is expected to continue until authorities deem the environment safe for normal activities to resume.

Background

Crete, the largest of the Greek islands, is known for its diverse geography, ranging from mountainous interiors to coastal plains. This varied terrain makes the island susceptible to landslides during periods of heavy rainfall, as water erodes the soil and destabilizes slopes. The recent storm system was part of a broader pattern of severe weather affecting the Aegean Sea and surrounding islands, including Evia, which also received rainstorm warnings.

Greece has experienced several significant weather events in recent years, with climate change contributing to more unpredictable and intense seasonal patterns. The Mediterranean region is increasingly prone to flash floods and strong gales, which can cause rapid and severe damage to infrastructure. Local meteorological agencies have issued warnings for gales in the Aegean, indicating that the severe weather conditions are not isolated to Crete but are affecting a wider area.

The island's economy relies heavily on tourism, and such weather events can have lasting impacts on the sector. Damage to roads, power lines, and buildings can deter visitors and require significant investment for repair. The closure of schools and public services further disrupts the daily life of residents, highlighting the vulnerability of communities to natural hazards.

What happens next

Authorities in Crete are expected to continue monitoring the weather conditions closely as the storm system moves through the region. Meteorologists will provide updated forecasts regarding the duration and intensity of the rain and winds, which will inform decisions on when to reopen schools and restore power services. Emergency services will remain on high alert to respond to any further landslides or flooding incidents.

Efforts to restore normalcy will focus on repairing damaged infrastructure, including roads and power lines. Local governments may issue further advisories to residents, urging caution and providing information on assistance available for those affected by the storms. The investigation into the death of the livestock farmer will proceed, with authorities working to determine the exact circumstances of the incident.
