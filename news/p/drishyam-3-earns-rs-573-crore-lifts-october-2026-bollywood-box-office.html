Drishyam 3 earns Rs 573 crore, lifts October 2026 Bollywood box office

Drishyam 3 has topped Rs 573 crore worldwide, extending its box‑office run into a fifth week. The earnings have helped make October 2026 the third‑strongest month for Bollywood revenues.

Latest developments

The film Drishyam 3 – subtitled The Conclusion – has closed its theatrical run with a reported worldwide gross of Rs 573 crore, according to box‑office data compiled by Pinkvilla. The figure places the Ajay Devgn‑led thriller among the highest‑earning Indian releases of the year and confirms a strong overseas response, particularly in markets with sizable South Asian diaspora. Industry analysts note that the collection includes earnings from domestic multiplexes, regional screens, and international territories such as the United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, and United States. The final tally also reflects the film’s extended run in several key cities, where it continued to attract audiences beyond its initial opening weekend. Data released on day 37 of the film’s release by NDTV Profit shows that Drishyam 3 maintained a steady daily average, adding modest but consistent revenue to its cumulative total. The report highlights that the movie’s earnings on the 37th day remained comparable to its performance in the preceding weeks, indicating sustained interest rather than a sharp drop‑off typical of many blockbusters. The steady run has been attributed to positive word‑of‑mouth, repeat viewings, and a limited number of competing releases in the October window. The film’s ability to hold its box‑office share for over five weeks underscores the effectiveness of its marketing push and the appeal of its narrative. The strong performance of Drishyam 3 has also contributed to Bollywood’s overall box‑office health in October 2026. ZoomTV Entertainment reported that the month recorded the third‑strongest box‑office earnings for the Indian film industry in the calendar year, with Drishyam 3 accounting for a significant share of the total revenue. The report cites the film’s contribution as a key factor in pushing the monthly gross beyond previous records set earlier in the year. This resurgence comes after a period of slower earnings, and the success of the thriller is seen as a catalyst for higher footfall in cinemas across the region. Beyond the numbers, the film’s success has had a ripple effect on ancillary markets. Merchandise sales, digital streaming rights, and satellite television deals have all seen an uptick following the theatrical run. Distributors in neighboring markets, including Pakistan, have reported increased demand for the film’s dubbed and subtitled versions, reflecting cross‑border interest in Indian cinema. The robust earnings have also encouraged producers to schedule releases of similar genre films in the upcoming quarter, hoping to replicate the momentum generated by Drishyam 3.

Why it matters

For viewers in Pakistan, the popularity of Drishyam 3 offers a rare glimpse into contemporary Indian storytelling that blends suspense with family drama. The film’s availability on local screens and streaming platforms has drawn sizable crowds, contributing to higher occupancy rates in major multiplexes in cities such as Karachi and Lahore. This cultural crossover supports a broader dialogue between the two film industries and provides Pakistani filmmakers with insights into audience preferences for tightly plotted thrillers. From an economic perspective, the box‑office surge generated by the film translates into increased revenue for cinema operators, concession vendors, and related service providers. The influx of patrons boosts ancillary sales such as snacks and merchandise, while the success of the film may influence future investment decisions by distributors seeking to bring high‑profile Indian titles to Pakistani markets. The ripple effect extends to advertising agencies that benefit from heightened media spend around blockbuster releases.

Background

The Drishyam franchise began with the 2013 Malayalam thriller directed by Jeethu Joseph, which was later remade in Hindi in 2015 starring Ajay Devgn and his son. The original story revolves around a man who engineers a perfect alibi to protect his family after a crime is committed. The success of the first two installments established the series as a benchmark for intelligent, low‑budget thrillers that rely on narrative twists rather than large‑scale action. Ajay Devgn returned to the role for the third installment, The Conclusion, reuniting with director Jeethu Joseph and co‑writer Abhishek Pathak. Production began in early 2025, with filming taking place across several Indian states and overseas locations to capture the story’s expanded scope. The sequel was marketed as the final chapter, promising to resolve lingering plot threads while delivering the same brand of suspense that defined its predecessors.

What happens next

While the subtitle The Conclusion signals the end of the main storyline, industry insiders suggest that the franchise could continue through prequels or spin‑off projects focusing on supporting characters. In the meantime, the success of Drishyam 3 is likely to influence the release calendar for the remainder of 2026, with producers eyeing similar genre films that can capitalize on audience appetite for tightly written thrillers.
