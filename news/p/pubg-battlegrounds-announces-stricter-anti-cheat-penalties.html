PUBG Battlegrounds Announces Stricter Anti-Cheat Penalties

Krafton has released a new weekly ban report for PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS, highlighting a crackdown on cheat distribution. Players are now warned that associating with known cheaters could lead to account suspensions.

Latest developments

PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS has published its weekly ban notice covering the period from November 2 to November 8. The report details the number of accounts suspended for violating the game's terms of service, specifically focusing on the use of unauthorized software. This regular update serves as a transparent record of the actions taken by the developers to maintain fair play across the global player base.

In a related development, the game's developers have emphasized a stricter stance on cheat distribution. Recent communications indicate that the focus is shifting beyond just the users of cheats to those who distribute or facilitate their use. This broader approach aims to dismantle the networks that supply these tools, rather than merely punishing individual players who use them.

Furthermore, a significant warning has been issued regarding premade teams. The developers have stated that playing alongside cheaters can also lead to serious penalties. This means that even if a player is not using cheats themselves, their account may be at risk if they are found in a squad with individuals who are. This policy change is designed to discourage the formation of groups that knowingly include malicious actors.

The anti-cheat measures are part of a broader effort to improve the gaming experience for legitimate players. By targeting both the supply and demand sides of the cheating ecosystem, the developers hope to reduce the prevalence of unfair advantages in matches. The weekly ban notices provide a clear metric of the scale of this ongoing battle against cheating.
