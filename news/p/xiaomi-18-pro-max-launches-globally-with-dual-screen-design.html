Xiaomi 18 Pro Max launches globally with dual-screen design

Xiaomi has officially brought its latest flagship smartphone to international markets, marking a significant expansion for its unique hardware design. The device features a secondary rear display and high-resolution imaging capabilities.

Latest developments

Xiaomi has confirmed the global availability of the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max, a move that extends the reach of its most distinctive hardware innovation beyond its home market. According to reports from TechNode, the manufacturer is taking its rear-screen flagship phones overseas, with the starting price set at €1,399. This pricing position places the device firmly in the premium segment, competing directly with other top-tier Android flagships from major global competitors.

The device has drawn significant attention from tech publications, including GSMArena, which has published a detailed review of the global variant. The review highlights the phone’s core specifications, which remain consistent with previous regional releases. Android Police has also noted the device’s place in a broader trend where Android flagships are doubling down on larger screens and high-megapixel cameras, specifically citing the 200MP sensor found in this model.

The global rollout signifies a strategic shift for Xiaomi, which has previously kept its most experimental hardware designs limited to specific regions. By launching the 18 Pro Max internationally, the company is testing consumer appetite for dual-display configurations in markets such as Europe and North America. The €1,399 entry point suggests that Xiaomi is aiming to capture users who are willing to pay a premium for unique form factors and advanced camera systems.

Industry observers note that the simultaneous coverage from multiple major tech outlets indicates strong interest in how this specific hardware configuration performs in real-world usage. The focus on the rear screen suggests that Xiaomi is targeting users who value utility and novelty in their flagship devices, moving beyond the standard single-screen paradigm that dominates the current market.

Xiaomi has established itself as a major player in the global smartphone market, known for offering high-specification devices at competitive price points. The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max represents the culmination of these experimental efforts, bringing a design previously seen in limited markets to a global audience.
