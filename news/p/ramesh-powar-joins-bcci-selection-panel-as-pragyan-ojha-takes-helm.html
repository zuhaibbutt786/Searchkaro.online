Ramesh Powar Joins BCCI Selection Panel as Pragyan Ojha Takes Helm

Ramesh Powar has joined the BCCI selection panel following Pragyan Ojha's appointment as chief selector. The move signals a shift in the committee's composition amid ongoing discussions about cricket governance.

Latest developments

On 19 October 2026, the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) announced that Pragyan Ojha would serve as the new chief selector, succeeding Ajit Agarkar. Ramesh Powar, a former first‑class cricketer and long‑time selector, was added to the senior selection committee. Powar's induction follows a series of appointments aimed at refreshing the panel. The decision was made during a press briefing where BCCI officials highlighted the need for a balanced mix of experience and fresh perspectives. Powar's reputation for analytical rigor and his prior involvement in domestic talent scouting were cited as key factors. The new committee will oversee the selection of the national team for upcoming Test, ODI and T20I series, including the 2027 World Cup qualifiers. Officials confirmed that Powar will sit on the panel that meets monthly to evaluate player performance, fitness and suitability for international duty. While the announcement was welcomed by many cricket analysts, some observers noted that Powar's addition could bring continuity to the selection process. The BCCI has faced scrutiny over selection transparency in recent years, and the appointment is seen as part of a broader effort to restore confidence among players and fans.

Why it matters

For Indian cricket fans, the reshuffle signals potential changes in team composition for the next few years. Powar's track record in spotting emerging talent could influence the selection of young players who may become mainstays of the national side. The appointment also reflects the BCCI's attempt to address criticisms about selection bias. By bringing in a selector with a strong domestic background, the board aims to demonstrate that merit remains the primary criterion for national representation. For players, the new committee structure may offer clearer pathways to international selection. Those performing well in the Ranji Trophy and other domestic tournaments will now have a panel that is reputed to value consistent performance.

Background

Ramesh Powar played first‑class cricket for Maharashtra between 2000 and 2008, scoring over 4,000 runs and taking 200 wickets. After retiring, he transitioned into coaching and selection roles, serving on the Maharashtra selection panel for several seasons. Powar's expertise in talent identification earned him a place on the BCCI's senior selection committee in 2019. He has been involved in selecting squads for the India A team and has contributed to the development of several players who later made their international debuts. Pragyan Ojha, a former Indian spinner, was appointed chief selector after Ajit Agarkar stepped down. Ojha's experience as a player and selector positions him to guide the committee through a period of transition.
