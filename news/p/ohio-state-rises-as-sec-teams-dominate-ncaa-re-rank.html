Ohio State rises as SEC teams dominate NCAA Re-Rank

The latest NCAA Re-Rank highlights a strong performance by Ohio State and continued dominance by Southeastern Conference teams. Georgia maintains its position at the top of the Coaches Poll as the season progresses.

This comprehensive ranking provides a broader view of the competitive field beyond the traditional Top 25 polls.

Background

The NCAA Re-Rank is a unique ranking system maintained by USA Today that includes all FBS teams, not just the top 25. It uses a proprietary formula that considers game outcomes, opponent strength, and other factors to create a comprehensive list. This system is often used by media outlets and fans to track the performance of mid-major and Group of Five teams that do not appear in the AP or Coaches Polls.

The Coaches Poll is one of the two major polls used to determine the College Football Playoff teams. It is voted on by a panel of head coaches and is released weekly. The AP Poll, voted on by media members, is the other major poll. Both polls are essential for determining the final four teams that compete for the national championship.

The 2026 season has been marked by high-quality play and competitive matchups. The strength of the SEC has been a recurring theme, with several teams finishing the regular season undefeated or with minimal losses. This depth has made the race for the top spots in the polls particularly tight and competitive.
