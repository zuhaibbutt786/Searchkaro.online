Kulgam CRPF Attack: Security Operations Intensify in Jammu and Kashmir

Intense search operations are underway in Kulgam after terrorists attacked a CRPF team on a motorcycle. The incident has heightened security alerts across the region, drawing attention to ongoing political and security dynamics.

Latest developments

Security forces in Jammu and Kashmir have launched a massive search operation in the Kulgam district following a violent attack on a Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) team. The incident, which occurred on a busy route, saw terrorists arriving on a motorcycle and opening fire on the security personnel. CCTV footage obtained by local media outlets shows the moment the attackers opened fire, highlighting the brazen nature of the assault. The attack has triggered a high-security alert across the valley, with additional troops deployed to key locations.

The search operation entered its second day, with security teams combing through forests and rural areas in and around Kulgam. Officials have stated that the operation is focused on tracking down the perpetrators and any potential accomplices. The use of motorcycles for such attacks has become a recurring tactic, allowing attackers to strike quickly and escape into difficult terrain. Local residents have been advised to stay indoors and cooperate with security forces during the ongoing sweep.

Media reports indicate that the attack has intensified scrutiny on local militant networks. One specific individual, Latif Butt, has been identified in news reports as a key figure associated with the Lashkar-e-Tayyiba group. Described as a former student who turned to militancy, he is currently the subject of a significant bounty. The police are intensifying efforts to locate him, viewing his capture as crucial to restoring stability in the region.

The incident has also drawn attention to the broader security situation in the Kashmir Valley. While the immediate focus is on the Kulgam attack, the event underscores the persistent threat posed by armed groups in the area. Security agencies are working closely with local intelligence to prevent similar incidents in other districts.
