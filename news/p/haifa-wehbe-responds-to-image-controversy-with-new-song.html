Haifa Wehbe Responds to Image Controversy with New Song

Lebanese pop icon Haifa Wehbe has taken to music to counter a wave of online criticism, dropping her latest single "Look Look". The singer's response has sparked renewed debate across Saudi social media.

Latest developments

In recent weeks a social media campaign has targeted Lebanese singer Haifa Wehbe, accusing her of misrepresenting herself and spreading false narratives. The allegations were amplified by a series of posts that circulated widely across Saudi platforms, prompting a wave of criticism and calls for her to clarify her stance. Wehbe countered the backlash by releasing a new single titled "Look Look" on September 30, 2026. The track, which blends pop and traditional Arabic melodies, is accompanied by a music video that features the singer addressing the accusations directly. In her public statement, the artist used a sarcastic tone, referring to the critics as "jealous and their work". She framed the controversy as a product of envy rather than genuine concern, urging fans to focus on her music instead of the rumors. The response sparked a divided reaction online. While a segment of her followers praised her boldness and the song's message, others accused her of deflecting from the original allegations and called for a more transparent dialogue.

Why it matters

The incident underscores the power of celebrity influence in the Gulf region, where public figures often become focal points for social commentary. Wehbe's swift musical rebuttal demonstrates how artists can shape narratives by leveraging popular media channels. For Saudi audiences, the controversy highlights the tension between traditional values and modern expressions of self. The debate over Wehbe's image reflects broader discussions about gender roles, artistic freedom, and the responsibility of public personas. Moreover, the episode illustrates how social media can amplify reputational risks for celebrities, turning isolated comments into nationwide conversations that can affect careers and public perception.

Background

Haifa Wehbe, born in 1978 in Beirut, rose to prominence in the early 2000s with a string of hit singles that blended Arabic pop with Western influences. Her bold fashion choices and outspoken personality quickly made her a household name across the Arab world. Over the past decade, Wehbe has faced several controversies, including disputes over contractual obligations and public feuds with fellow artists. Despite these challenges, she has maintained a loyal fan base and continues to release music that charts across regional streaming platforms. Her Lebanese heritage and pan-Arab appeal have positioned her as a cultural bridge between the Levant and Gulf audiences, allowing her to navigate diverse musical tastes and social expectations.

What happens next

Legal experts suggest that the defamation claims could lead to formal complaints filed by Wehbe's management, potentially resulting in court proceedings or settlement negotiations. In the meantime, the singer is expected to promote her new single through televised interviews and live performances, aiming to reinforce her public image and quell lingering doubts.
