Kenneth Walker III Thrives with Chiefs After Seahawks Release

Kenneth Walker III has become a key contributor for the Chiefs after being released by Seattle. Upcoming games could shape his future and the Seahawks' roster plans.

Latest developments

After being released by Seattle, running back Kenneth Walker III signed with the Kansas City Chiefs. In his first few games, he has posted solid rushing yards and contributed on special teams. Coaches praised his work ethic and adaptability, noting his ability to fit into the Chiefs’ offensive scheme. The move has sparked discussion among analysts who compare his early impact with his time in Seattle. The Seahawks’ decision to part ways with Walker earlier this season has been examined by several sports outlets. ESPN highlighted that the team’s depth at running back and a shift toward a pass‑heavy approach left limited opportunities for Walker. Critics argue that Seattle may have misjudged his potential, especially as the Chiefs have found a complementary role for him alongside their star back. Ahead of the upcoming matchup between the Chiefs and Seahawks, Yahoo Sports reported a concerning prediction related to Walker’s performance. The article noted that betting markets and some pundits expect Walker to face a tough defensive front, potentially limiting his production. Nevertheless, the Chiefs’ coaching staff has indicated confidence in his ability to exploit gaps and contribute in the passing game. Bleacher Report detailed new NFL rumors surrounding a possible “split” between Walker and the Seahawks, suggesting that the former team might consider a trade or future acquisition once the season progresses. The speculation is fueled by the “revenge game” narrative, where Walker could face his former teammates and test the Seahawks’ defensive adjustments. Sources close to the Chiefs said they are monitoring the situation but have no immediate plans to move him.

Why it matters

Walker’s resurgence with Kansas City illustrates how player movement can reshape team dynamics in the NFL. For fans in the United States, especially those following the Pacific Northwest and Midwest markets, his success provides a tangible example of how a change in scheme can unlock a player’s skill set. The situation also impacts the Seahawks’ roster strategy. If Walker continues to excel, Seattle may need to reassess its depth chart and consider future trades or free‑agency moves to address the running back position. The broader league sees this as a case study in talent evaluation and the importance of aligning player strengths with coaching philosophy.

Background

Kenneth Walker III entered the league as a third‑round pick out of Michigan State in 2022. He quickly became a fan favorite in Seattle, known for his burst and vision. During his rookie season, he recorded over 1,000 rushing yards and earned a Pro Bowl nod. However, the 2023 season brought coaching changes and a shift toward a spread offense, reducing his carries. By mid‑season, the Seahawks placed him on the trade block, and he was subsequently released. The Kansas City Chiefs, seeking depth behind their lead back, signed him to a one‑year deal.

What happens next

The upcoming Chiefs‑Seahawks game will be the first high‑profile test of Walker’s new role. Observers expect his performance to influence whether Kansas City retains him beyond the current contract. Seattle’s front office will watch closely to gauge any future interest, while the Chiefs will decide if his contributions merit a longer‑term commitment.
