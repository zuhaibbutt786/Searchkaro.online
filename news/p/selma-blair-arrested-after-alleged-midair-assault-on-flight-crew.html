Selma Blair arrested after alleged mid‑air assault on flight crew

Selma Blair was taken into custody after an alleged altercation with airline staff on a recent flight. The actress says she never intended to cause harm, her publicist says.

Latest developments

On 12 October 2026, actress Selma Blair was detained by U.S. authorities after an incident on a commercial flight that sparked a mid‑air confrontation. According to reports from TMZ, the altercation began when Blair allegedly confronted a flight attendant, leading to a physical struggle that was captured by other passengers. The airline crew reported that Blair attempted to open an aircraft door, prompting the pilot to alert ground control and request immediate assistance upon landing.

The flight, which was en route from New York to a destination in the United States, made an emergency landing at its scheduled arrival airport where law enforcement boarded the aircraft. Blair was placed under arrest on the tarmac and later transferred to a local detention facility. The airline issued a brief statement confirming that the incident was being investigated and that the safety of passengers and crew remained its top priority.

In a statement to the BBC, Blair said she "did not intend to harm anyone" and expressed regret for the disruption caused to fellow travelers. She emphasized that her actions were not meant to endanger the aircraft or its occupants, and she apologized for any distress caused. The actress also indicated that she was experiencing a medical episode that may have contributed to her behavior, though she did not elaborate on the specifics.

Dawn newspaper reported that Blair's publicist, on her behalf, clarified that the actress did not try to open the aircraft door or cause any physical injury to crew members. The publicist stressed that Blair's intent was not malicious and that she was cooperating fully with investigators. The statement also highlighted that the actress has a history of health challenges, which could have played a role in the incident, and that she is seeking appropriate medical assistance.
