Alison Hammond abruptly leaves 'This Morning' studio, sparking viewer concern

Alison Hammond walked out of the This Morning studio mid‑show, leaving co‑host Dermot O'Leary to present alone. Viewers expressed concern and the programme issued a brief explanation.

Latest developments

During the live broadcast of ITV's This Morning on 2 October, presenter Alison Hammond was seen rushing out of the studio after a brief segment. The incident occurred shortly after a pre‑recorded interview, and cameras captured Hammond hurrying past the set before the show continued with Dermot O'Leary handling the remaining items alone.

Social media users reacted instantly, with many posting screenshots and videos of the moment. The LADbible reported that viewers were "worried" as Hammond disappeared, noting a spike in comments asking whether she was ill. The Daily Star later described the scene as a "rush out of the studio" and suggested an unexpected issue forced her departure.

ITV released a short statement saying the presenter had to leave the studio for personal reasons and that the programme would resume its normal format after a brief pause. No further details were provided at the time.

The Sun added that Hammond appeared to be "pulled off air" and that she seemed to be "falling sick", though the outlet clarified that no official medical confirmation had been made public.

Within minutes, the hashtag #AlisonHammond trended on Twitter in the United Kingdom, with fans sharing concerns and urging the network to update the audience on her wellbeing.

Why it matters

This Morning is a flagship daytime programme with an average audience of over two million viewers in the UK. An unexpected absence of a popular co‑presenter like Hammond can disrupt the show's flow and affect viewer satisfaction, potentially influencing ratings for the remainder of the broadcast day.

Moreover, the incident highlights the pressures faced by live television presenters, especially in a high‑visibility environment where any personal issue becomes public instantly. The audience's reaction underscores a growing expectation for transparency from broadcasters when on‑air talent encounters health or personal emergencies.

Background

Alison Hammond has been a regular face on This Morning since 2020, known for her upbeat personality and audience interaction. Prior to her role on the show, she gained fame on the reality series Love Island and later transitioned to presenting, earning a dedicated fan base across daytime TV.

The programme itself, launched in 1988, combines news, lifestyle features and interviews, and relies heavily on the chemistry between its presenters. Any disruption to this dynamic is noteworthy, as the show’s success is built on the rapport between hosts and the audience’s familiarity with their on‑screen personas.
