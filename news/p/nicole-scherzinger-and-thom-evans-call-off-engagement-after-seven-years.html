Nicole Scherzinger and Thom Evans Call Off Engagement After Seven Years

Nicole Scherzinger and her longtime partner Thom Evans have called off their engagement after seven years together. The split was confirmed by both parties in statements released this week.

Latest developments

The entertainment media reported on Monday that Nicole Scherzinger and her fiancé Thom Evans have ended their engagement, bringing an abrupt close to a relationship that began in 2017. The People.com article cited sources close to the couple who said the decision was mutual and that the pair had been discussing the future for several weeks before reaching a final agreement. Both Scherzinger, known for leading the Pussycat Dolls and her solo music career, and Evans, a former rugby player turned model, had been publicly engaged since 2020.

Thom Evans later confirmed the breakup on his Instagram account, posting a brief statement that thanked fans for their support and expressed gratitude for the years spent together. The HuffPost UK coverage highlighted that Evans’ post avoided specific details, emphasizing respect for privacy while acknowledging the end of a significant chapter in both of their lives.

The Independent added context by noting that industry insiders suggested the couple’s demanding schedules and divergent career priorities may have contributed to the split. While no official reason was given, reports indicated that Scherzinger’s upcoming music projects and Evans’ modeling commitments required extensive travel, creating logistical challenges that strained the relationship.

Fans reacted across social media platforms, with many expressing disappointment but also wishing the pair well. Representatives for both individuals declined to comment further, reinforcing the desire to keep the matter private as the two move forward separately.
