Judas Priest bassist Ian Hill announces retirement from band

The legendary Judas Priest bassist has stepped down after decades of service. The band has confirmed that Uriah Heep's Dave Rimmer will take over his role.

Latest developments

Ian Hill, the iconic bassist of the British heavy metal band Judas Priest, has officially announced his retirement from the group. The announcement marks the end of a significant chapter in the band's history, as Hill has been a central figure in their sound and identity for many years. In a statement shared with the music press, Hill expressed his deep gratitude to the fans who supported him throughout his career. He described the experience as a life he was privileged to live, thanking the audience for their enduring loyalty.

The news also revealed that the band is not leaving the position vacant. Dave Rimmer, the well-known bassist from the classic rock band Uriah Heep, has been named as Hill's successor. Rimmer is a respected figure in the metal and rock scene, having performed with Uriah Heep for several decades. His appointment suggests that Judas Priest intends to continue touring and performing with a seasoned musician who understands the demands of the genre.
