Gerwyn Price withdraws from exhibitions through 2027, cites personal reasons

Latest developments

Gerwyn Price confirmed on Monday that he will withdraw from every scheduled exhibition match for the rest of the 2026 calendar year and the entirety of 2027. The announcement was posted on his official social media channels and echoed by Live Darts, which reported the decision as being driven by personal reasons. Price’s management team also released a brief statement indicating that the break will allow him to focus on family commitments and a new business venture.

The personal reasons cited include the recent purchase of a farm in his native Wales, a move that the Sun described as a ‘grandchild‑imminent’ lifestyle change. According to the report, Price intends to spend more time on the property, which he plans to develop into a small‑scale agricultural operation. The farm acquisition was confirmed by local media, and Price’s representatives said the venture will run alongside his professional darts career, but not interfere with his participation in PDC ranking tournaments.

The decision has prompted a mixed response from the darts community. Exhibition promoters expressed disappointment, noting that Price’s high‑profile appearances often draw large crowds and generate significant revenue. However, several fellow players offered support, emphasizing the importance of work‑life balance and applauding his commitment to family. The South Wales Argus highlighted that the break will extend through 2027, meaning that fans will be without his presence at charity and promotional events for at least two full seasons.

Despite stepping back from non‑ranking events, Price has not indicated any intention to miss upcoming PDC World Championship qualifiers or the 2026 World Matchplay. His recent form on the professional circuit remains strong, and he retains a place among the sport’s top earners. Analysts suggest that the hiatus from exhibitions may even extend his competitive longevity by reducing travel fatigue.

Why it matters

Gerwyn Price is one of the most recognizable figures in modern darts, and his exhibition appearances have become a staple of the sport’s outreach programmes. By withdrawing for an extended period, promoters lose a reliable draw that helps introduce the game to new audiences, particularly in regional venues and charity events.

The move also highlights the growing need for professional athletes to balance demanding schedules with personal well‑being. As the darts calendar becomes increasingly crowded with televised tournaments and promotional tours, Price’s choice may encourage other players to consider similar breaks, potentially reshaping how the sport manages player workloads.

Background

Price, a former World Champion, has built a reputation for his intense on‑stage demeanor and powerful throwing style. Over the past decade he has amassed multiple major titles, including the PDC World Championship in 2021, and has regularly featured in high‑profile exhibition circuits across the UK and Europe.

Exhibition matches have long served as a revenue stream for players and a promotional tool for sponsors. In recent years, the format has expanded to include charity nights, fan‑interaction sessions, and televised specials, with Price often headlining due to his popularity and marketability.

What happens next

Price is expected to remain active on the PDC circuit, with his next major appearance slated for the 2026 World Championship qualifiers in early November. Fans will watch for updates on his farm project, and organizers will seek alternative headline players to fill the exhibition calendar through 2027.
