Sara Ali Khan’s Upcoming Film Faces Release Block in Nepal

The Bollywood film 'Udta Teer', starring Sara Ali Khan and Ayushmann Khurrana, has been denied release permission in Nepal. The decision has raised questions about film distribution and censorship in the region.

Latest developments

The Hindi-language film 'Udta Teer', featuring Sara Ali Khan alongside Ayushmann Khurrana, was slated for a theatrical release in Nepal in early 2024. Production companies had secured distribution rights and announced a premiere date in Kathmandu. However, the Nepal Film Development Board denied the film a release permit, citing concerns over content that could be deemed politically sensitive.

Officials in Nepal argued that the movie contains scenes that might influence public opinion during the upcoming election cycle. The board’s decision was published in a press release on March 5, 2024, and was supported by a statement from the Ministry of Culture, which emphasized the need to maintain social harmony.

The filmmakers responded by stating that the film is a romantic drama with no political agenda. They expressed disappointment at the denial and warned that the move could set a precedent for future foreign productions seeking entry into the Nepali market.

Legal experts suggest that the producers may appeal the decision through Nepal’s court system, while diplomatic channels between India and Nepal could also be involved. Until a resolution is reached, the film’s release in Nepal remains uncertain.

Why it matters

For audiences in Pakistan, the denial highlights the challenges faced by Bollywood releases in neighboring countries. Pakistani viewers who follow Sara Ali Khan’s work often rely on regional screenings to access new films before they appear on streaming platforms.

The incident also raises questions about the consistency of censorship policies across South Asian nations. If a film can be blocked in Nepal, it may face similar hurdles in other markets, potentially affecting the financial returns of the production companies.

Background

Sara Ali Khan, the daughter of Bollywood veteran actor Saif Ali Khan, made her debut in 2018 and has since appeared in several commercially successful movies. Her partnership with Ayushmann Khurrana, a well-known comedian-turned-actor, was expected to bring a fresh dynamic to the screen.

'Udta Teer' was announced in 2022 as a joint venture between an Indian production house and a Nepalese distributor. The film’s storyline revolves around a love story set against a backdrop of cultural diversity, aiming to appeal to a broad audience across the region.

Prior to this incident, the film had completed principal photography and was in post‑production. No earlier controversies were reported, making the release denial an unexpected development.

What happens next

The producers have indicated that they will file an appeal against the board’s decision. Simultaneously, they are exploring alternative release windows in other countries, including a planned digital launch in India and Pakistan.

If the appeal is successful, the film could resume its planned theatrical run in Nepal later in the year. Otherwise, the release may shift entirely to online platforms, which could alter its revenue model.
