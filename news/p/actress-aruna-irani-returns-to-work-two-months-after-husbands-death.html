Actress Aruna Irani Returns to Work Two Months After Husband's Death

Veteran actress Aruna Irani posted a heartfelt video thanking fans for their love after her husband Kuku Kohli passed away. She has now returned to work, marking her first public appearance since the tragedy.

Latest developments

Aruna Irani, a veteran of Hindi cinema, posted a video on social media on Tuesday, expressing gratitude for the "overwhelming love and support" she has received since the death of her husband, film producer Kuku Kohli, two months ago. In the clip, she raised her hands in a traditional gesture of prayer and asked her followers to stay by her side "until the last breath." The video quickly went viral, accumulating millions of views and a flood of supportive comments.

Following the emotional post, Irani confirmed that she has resumed filming for an upcoming project. Sources close to the production say she arrived on set on Wednesday, appearing focused and determined. The move marks her first professional engagement since Kohli's passing, and industry insiders view it as a sign of her resilience.

Fans and fellow actors have praised her decision to return, highlighting the importance of normalcy in the grieving process. The actress also hinted that she may dedicate future work to her late husband, though no specific details were disclosed.

Why it matters

Irani's return resonates with a broad audience in India, where public figures often become symbols of personal struggle. Her open acknowledgment of grief and request for continued support offers a rare glimpse into how celebrities cope with loss, potentially encouraging fans to discuss their own bereavement openly.

The episode also underscores the tight-knit nature of Bollywood's community, where colleagues frequently rally around a grieving member. By sharing her emotions publicly, Irani contributes to a growing conversation about mental health and emotional well‑being in the entertainment industry, topics that have gained prominence in recent years.

Moreover, her comeback may influence upcoming productions, as producers and directors consider the emotional climate on set when working with artists facing personal challenges.

Background

Aruna Irani began her career as a child actress in the 1960s and has since appeared in over 300 films, earning multiple awards for her versatile performances. Known for roles ranging from dramatic to comedic, she remains a respected figure in Hindi cinema.

Kuku Kohli, a film producer, was married to Irani for several decades. While details of his death have not been publicly disclosed, his passing was reported in early August, prompting an outpouring of condolences from the film fraternity.

The couple was known for maintaining a low‑profile personal life, rarely discussing private matters in the media. Irani's recent public statements therefore represent a departure from her usual discretion, reflecting the depth of her recent loss.

What happens next

Industry sources indicate that Irani will continue shooting for the current project, which is slated for release later this year. She is also expected to appear in a charity event honoring Kuku Kohli's contributions to cinema, though official confirmation is pending.

Observers anticipate that her renewed visibility will lead to additional interview requests and possibly new film offers, as producers seek to align with her enduring popularity.
