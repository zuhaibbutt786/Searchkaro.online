Oppo Unveils Find X10 Series: Launch Date, Colors, and Specs

Oppo has announced the release details for its flagship Find X10 series, including launch date, color palette, and storage options. The lineup promises a new 200‑megapixel camera setup and competitive pricing.

What is happening

Oppo announced the official launch date for its flagship Find X10 series during a virtual event on 10 July 2024. The company confirmed that the devices will be available in the market on 15 August 2024, with pre‑orders opening a week earlier. The lineup will include three models: the standard Find X10, the Find X10 Pro, and the Find X10 Pro Max. Each model will be offered in four color options—Midnight Black, Ocean Blue, Pearl White, and Sunset Red—catering to a broad range of consumer preferences. Storage configurations will start at 128 GB and go up to 1 TB. The base model will feature 128 GB of internal memory, while the higher tiers will offer 256 GB, 512 GB, and 1 TB options, all paired with 12 GB of RAM. Key hardware specifications include a 200‑megapixel triple‑camera array, a 6.7‑inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate, a 5000 mAh battery, and 65 W fast charging support. The devices will run on the latest Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset. Availability in Pakistan will be through official Oppo stores and authorized retailers, with the first units expected to ship to major cities such as Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad on launch day.

Why people are searching this now

The release of the Find X10 series follows closely on the heels of Apple’s iPhone 18 Pro Max launch, sparking a wave of comparisons among premium smartphone enthusiasts. The timing has amplified interest as consumers look for alternatives that offer advanced camera technology. The 200‑megapixel camera promise is a significant draw, as high‑resolution sensors continue to be a key selling point in the premium segment. This feature has generated considerable online discussion and speculation about its real‑world performance. In Pakistan, the search volume has risen due to the anticipation of local retailers announcing pricing and availability. Many consumers are also evaluating how the Find X10 series will compete with established brands such as Samsung and Xiaomi. Overall, the convergence of a new launch date, advanced camera specifications, and competitive pricing has made the Oppo Find X10 series a hot topic among tech communities worldwide.

Background and context

Oppo’s flagship Find X series has historically been a benchmark for camera innovation and design elegance. The previous generation, the Find X9 series, set a high standard with its 108‑megapixel sensor and 5G capabilities. The company’s strategy focuses on delivering high‑performance photography, sleek aesthetics, and value pricing. This approach has positioned Oppo as a strong contender in the global premium smartphone market. The smartphone industry is witnessing a steady shift toward 5G connectivity and advanced imaging, with consumers increasingly prioritizing camera quality and display smoothness. Oppo’s new chipset, the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, supports these trends with improved processing power and energy efficiency. Regulatory approvals for the new chipset and 5G modules have been secured in major markets, ensuring a smooth rollout of the Find X10 series across regions, including Pakistan.

What to know next

Pricing for the Find X10 series will start at $999 for the base model, with higher configurations priced proportionally. Oppo has indicated that the pricing strategy aims to remain competitive while reflecting the advanced features of the device. The official launch in Pakistan is scheduled for 15 August 2024. Consumers can expect to find the devices in Oppo stores and through authorized resellers, with pre‑orders opening on 8 August. After‑sales support will include a standard one‑year warranty and an extended service network. Oppo has announced that service centers across major cities will be ready to handle repairs and maintenance from the day of launch.

Key takeaways
