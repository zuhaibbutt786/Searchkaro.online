Makhachev Announces Next Fight May Be One of His Last

UFC champion Islam Makhachev has hinted that his next fight could be one of the last in his career. The announcement comes ahead of a scheduled bout against Prates-Morales.

Latest developments

In a recent interview, UFC welterweight champion Islam Makhachev revealed that his forthcoming bout could be one of the final contests of his professional career. The Russian fighter, who has held the title since 2023, expressed a sense of closure as he approaches the later stages of his journey.

Makhachev is scheduled to face Brazilian prospect Prates-Morales in a fight that is set to take place at UFC 295 on 18 November. The match is expected to be a high‑stakes encounter, with both fighters looking to cement their positions in the division.

The announcement has drawn attention from sports journalists across the globe. Analysts note that Makhachev’s decision comes after a string of dominant performances, and many speculate that he may be preparing for a post‑competition career.

For the UFC, the potential retirement of a reigning champion could trigger a reshuffle in the welterweight rankings. Organisers are already discussing how to maintain interest in the division while ensuring that the title remains contested.

Why it matters

South African fans have long followed Makhachev’s rise, and his potential retirement may influence local interest in MMA. The prospect of a top‑tier athlete stepping away could affect viewership numbers and the popularity of the sport in the region.

The economic impact extends beyond viewership. Pay‑per‑view sales, sponsorship deals, and local promotions could see a shift as fans adjust to a new champion. Businesses that rely on MMA events may need to adapt their marketing strategies.

Moreover, the welterweight division is poised for a transition. With Makhachev’s possible exit, contenders such as Leon Edwards, Colby Covington, and others will have a clearer path to the title, reshaping the competitive landscape for the next few years.

Background

Born in 1995, Islam Makhachev began training in combat sports at a young age. His blend of grappling and striking earned him a reputation as a formidable opponent, leading to a rapid rise through the UFC ranks.

Makhachev has previously hinted at retirement after a title defense in 2025, but he returned to the octagon with renewed vigor. His recent victories have reinforced his status as one of the premier fighters in the welterweight division.

Currently, he holds the UFC welterweight title and has defended it successfully against several high‑profile opponents. His performance record remains impressive, with a 20‑1 win‑loss ratio.

What happens next

The fight against Prates-Morales is scheduled for 18 November at UFC 295, with the bout expected to take place in Las Vegas. Both fighters have prepared extensively, and the event is anticipated to draw significant attention.

Following the fight, Makhachev may confirm a formal retirement announcement or announce a final series of bouts. The UFC will likely schedule a title unification or interim fight to maintain momentum in the division.
