Pollard leads UAE Bulls to Abu Dhabi T10 triumph

The Abu Dhabi T10 tournament concluded on 27 November 2026 with the final played at the Yas Marina Circuit. The UAE Bulls, captained by West Indies veteran Kieron Pollard, faced the home‑based Yas Lions. After a rain‑affected group stage, both sides entered the decider with momentum on their side. The Bulls posted a competitive total of 124 runs for the loss of five wickets in their allotted ten overs, setting a target that tested the Lions’ chase.

Pollard contributed a quick‑fire 45‑run innings, striking at a strike rate of 150, and claimed two wickets with his medium‑pace deliveries. His all‑round display earned him the Player of the Match award. The innings included four boundaries and a six that lifted the Bulls past the 120‑run mark. In the field, Pollard’s sharp catches helped dismiss key Lions batters, reinforcing his influence throughout the match.

Cricbuzz’s player‑match highlights captured the turning points of the final. Yas Lions opener Ahmed Raza started strongly but was trapped in front of the wicket by Pollard’s slower ball. The Lions’ middle order struggled against the Bulls’ death‑over bowlers, who delivered three wides and two no‑balls in the final over, adding crucial runs. The Bulls’ bowler Mohammad Naveed finished with figures of 3 for 18, a performance highlighted in the video clips.

Social media in Pakistan lit up with celebratory posts, many praising Pollard’s leadership and the Bulls’ aggressive brand of cricket. Local T10 franchises noted the result as a benchmark for building competitive squads. The victory also raised the profile of the Abu Dhabi T10 league, which has attracted increasing viewership from South Asian markets over the past two years.

Background

Kieron Pollard, a former West Indies international, has built a reputation as a powerful hitter and a shrewd captain in franchise cricket. After retiring from international play in 2022, he turned his focus to T20 and T10 leagues, where his experience and charisma have made him a valuable asset. The Abu Dhabi T10 tournament, launched in 2017, has grown into a high‑profile event that attracts players from across the cricketing world.

The UAE Bulls entered the 2026 edition with a balanced squad of seasoned overseas professionals and promising local talent. Prior to this season, the team had reached the semi‑finals twice but had never secured the title. Pollard’s appointment as captain earlier in the year was seen as a strategic move to bring leadership and finishing power to the side. His previous stints with other T10 franchises have yielded multiple Player of the Tournament awards, underscoring his impact on the format.

Following the victory, the UAE Bulls will participate in a series of exhibition matches across the Middle East, providing further exposure for the league and its players.
