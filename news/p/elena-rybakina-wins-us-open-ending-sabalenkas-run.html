Elena Rybakina wins US Open, ending Sabalenka's run

Elena Rybakina claimed her first US Open crown by beating Aryna Sabalenka in the final. The result sparked a wave of online searches, especially in Pakistan.

What is happening

Kazakhstani player Elena Rybakina secured her first Grand Slam championship at the 2024 US Open, defeating Belarusian Aryna Sabalenka in straight sets. The final, played on the hard courts of Flushing Meadows, ended 6-4, 6-3 in Rybakina's favour, marking a decisive moment in her career.

Rybakina entered the tournament as the fourth seed, having reached the semifinals in previous majors but never advancing beyond that stage. Her powerful serve and aggressive baseline play carried her through a series of tough matches, including a four-set victory over world-number-one Iga Swiatek in the quarterfinals.

Sabalenka, the defending champion, struggled with a series of unforced errors in the final, a contrast to her dominant performance in the previous year. Following the match, tournament officials fined her $7,500 for the racket-smashing incident that occurred on court after the loss, a penalty reported by multiple outlets.

The win also earned Rybakina a substantial boost in ranking points, moving her closer to the top of the WTA leaderboard. Her victory was celebrated by the Kazakhstan tennis federation, which highlighted the achievement as a milestone for the nation's growing presence in international tennis.

Why people are searching this now

The US Open final attracted worldwide television coverage, and the unexpected result generated a spike in online queries. In Pakistan, the match was broadcast on popular sports channels, and social media discussions highlighted Rybakina's rise, prompting a surge in searches for her name.

Local interest is also driven by the broader appeal of women's tennis in the region, where recent performances by Asian and Eurasian players have resonated with fans. The combination of a dramatic final and the accompanying controversy around Sabalenka's fine amplified curiosity, leading to the current trend.

Background and context

Elena Rybakina was born in Moscow in 1996 and switched allegiance to Kazakhstan in 2018 after receiving support from the national sports program. Since then she has claimed several WTA titles, including the 2022 Wimbledon championship, which marked her first major win.

Her playing style relies on a powerful first serve, often exceeding 115 mph, and a willingness to dictate points from the baseline. Rybakina's climb up the rankings has been steady, and she has become known for her composure under pressure, traits that were evident throughout the US Open run.

What to know next

Following her US Open triumph, Rybakina is expected to compete in the upcoming WTA Finals, where the top eight players of the season will face off. Analysts suggest that her confidence will be a key factor as she prepares for the indoor hard-court season later in the year.

Sabalenka, meanwhile, will serve a suspension of her ranking points due to the equipment-abuse fine, and she plans to regroup for the Asian swing of tournaments. Both players are likely to feature prominently in the race for the year-end No. 1 ranking.
