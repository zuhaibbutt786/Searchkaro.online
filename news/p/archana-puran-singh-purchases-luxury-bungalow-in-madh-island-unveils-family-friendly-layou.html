Archana Puran Singh purchases luxury bungalow in Madh Island, unveils family-friendly layout

Actress and television host Archana Puran Singh has acquired a new multi‑crore bungalow on Mumbai's Madh Island. A house tour revealed a spacious layout with a dedicated area for her daughters‑in‑law, sparking interest across the country.

Latest developments

Archana Puran Singh, a veteran of Indian television and film, confirmed the purchase of a new bungalow on Madh Island, a coastal enclave in Mumbai known for its upscale residences. Sources close to the star reported that the property is valued at several crores, reflecting the high demand for premium waterfront homes in the region. The house, built on a sizable plot, features contemporary architecture blended with traditional Indian design elements, a combination that appeals to many affluent buyers in the city.

The interior of the bungalow was revealed through a series of photographs released by local media outlets. The tour highlighted a master bedroom with a walk‑in wardrobe, a spacious living area with floor‑to‑ceiling windows, and a modern kitchen equipped with high‑end appliances. Notably, the layout includes a separate wing designed specifically for the family’s daughters‑in‑law, a feature that aligns with cultural expectations of privacy for married women in joint families. The dedicated space comprises a bedroom, an attached bathroom, and a modest sitting area, allowing the women to maintain their own routines while remaining close to the main family hub.

Family members, including Archana’s husband Parmeet Sethi, were reportedly present during the house tour, preparing the home for an upcoming Griha Pravesh ceremony—a traditional house‑warming ritual. The event is expected to bring together relatives and close friends, marking the first formal entry of the daughters‑in‑law into the new residence. Media outlets have highlighted the celebratory atmosphere, noting that the family appears eager to showcase the new home as a symbol of stability and growth.

The purchase quickly attracted public attention, with search interest in Archana Puran Singh’s name spiking on major platforms. News portals such as Aaj Tak, ABP News, and Navbharat Times ran detailed photo galleries and reports, emphasizing the property’s price tag and the unique family‑centric design. Social media users have shared the images widely, commenting on the blend of luxury and practicality that the bungalow represents for a modern Indian household.

By choosing Madh Island, Archana joins a growing list of public figures who prefer coastal neighborhoods that offer privacy away from the bustling city centre while still providing easy access to Mumbai’s entertainment hubs.

The inclusion of a separate wing for daughters‑in‑law reflects evolving attitudes toward joint‑family living. While traditional Indian homes often allocate shared spaces, the dedicated area signals a shift toward accommodating the privacy and autonomy of married women, a change that may influence how future homes are designed in similar cultural contexts. Real‑estate developers could see increased demand for such layouts, prompting architects to incorporate flexible, multi‑generational spaces in upcoming projects.

Archana Puran Singh began her career as a film actress in the early 1990s before transitioning to television, where she became a household name through popular shows like "The Kapil Sharma Show" and "Comedy Circus." Over the years, she has built a reputation for her comedic timing and charismatic presence, earning numerous awards and a loyal fan base. Her public image combines professional success with a relatable family life, often highlighted in interviews and media appearances.

Prior to the Madh Island purchase, Archana and her husband Parmeet Sethi owned a residence in the upscale suburb of Bandra, a neighbourhood known for its vibrant nightlife and proximity to the film industry. The couple’s previous home was frequently featured in lifestyle magazines, showcasing a blend of modern décor and personal memorabilia. The decision to move to Madh Island marks a strategic shift toward a quieter, more spacious environment, aligning with the couple’s desire for a larger family setting as their children grow older.

Madh Island itself has a storied history as a retreat for Mumbai’s elite. Once a fishing village, it has transformed over the past few decades into a sought‑after residential zone, offering sea‑facing plots, gated communities, and a relatively low density compared to the city’s core. The area attracts celebrities, business leaders, and high‑net‑worth individuals seeking a blend of seclusion and urban connectivity.
