Crvena Zvezda Upset Barcelona 103-100 in EuroLeague Thriller

Crvena Zvezda defeated Barcelona 103-100 in a tightly contested EuroLeague match. The win gives the Serbian club a lead in the best‑of‑three series and may spark greater EuroLeague viewership in Pakistan.

Latest developments

Serbian club Crvena Zvezda secured a narrow 103-100 victory over Spanish giants FC Barcelona on Tuesday night in the EuroLeague round of 16. The match, played at Barcelona's Palau Blaugrana, saw the Serbian side overturn a first‑half deficit and hold on in the final minutes despite a late surge from the Catalan team. Tyson Carter led the scoring for Crvena Zvezda with 23 points, while Kristijan Koloko contributed a double‑double of 14 points and 12 rebounds. Barcelona relied heavily on Chima Moneke, who finished with 22 points, but the team fell short after missing several key free throws in the closing seconds. The game turned on a sequence of defensive stops in the last two minutes. With Barcelona trailing by three, Moneke drove to the basket and was fouled, converting both free throws to bring the score to 100-99. On the subsequent possession, Crvena Zvezda executed a fast break that resulted in a layup by Carter, extending the lead to 102-100. Barcelona attempted a final three‑point shot, but the attempt rimmed out, sealing the upset. The win puts Crvena Zvezda ahead 2-1 in the best‑of‑three series, giving them a chance to close the tie at home in Belgrade. Post‑match statistics highlighted the efficiency of the Serbian side in transition. Crvena Zvezda shot 48% from the field and 38% from beyond the arc, compared with Barcelona's 44% and 31% respectively. Turnovers also favored the visitors, who forced eight more than they committed. Coach Sasha Djordjevic praised his team's composure, noting that the players stuck to the game plan despite the hostile atmosphere. Fans in the arena were vocal throughout, with Barcelona supporters chanting after each missed shot, while a contingent of Serbian supporters celebrated each successful play. The atmosphere remained tense until the final buzzer, when the Serbian bench erupted in celebration.

Background

FC Barcelona, founded in 1926, is one of the most successful basketball clubs in Spain and Europe, with multiple EuroLeague titles. Crvena Zvezda, known in English as Red Star Belgrade, was established in 1945 and has a strong domestic record, winning numerous Serbian championships and two EuroLeague titles in the 1970s. Both clubs entered the 2023‑24 EuroLeague season with high expectations. Barcelona finished the regular season in the top four, while Crvena Zvezda secured a fifth‑place finish, earning a spot in the knockout phase. The round‑of‑16 series pits the two teams against each other in a best‑of‑three format, with the first game having taken place in Barcelona. The EuroLeague, Europe's premier club basketball competition, features 18 teams from across the continent and is widely followed in South Asia, including Pakistan, where basketball participation is on the rise.
