SBI Clerk Prelims Result 2026: Mains Exam Dates Confirmed

Latest developments

The State Bank of India (SBI) has officially announced the results for the Junior Associate (Customer Support and Sales) recruitment drive for 2026. The preliminary examination results are now available on the bank's official website, allowing successful candidates to proceed to the next stage of the hiring process. This announcement follows a period of high anticipation among applicants who sat for the online preliminary test earlier this year.

Alongside the result declaration, SBI has confirmed the schedule for the Mains examination. The post-basic examination is set to be conducted on November 26 and 27, 2026. This timeline provides candidates with a clear window to prepare for the more comprehensive assessment that determines final selection. The bank has emphasized that the admit cards for the Mains phase will be released in the coming days, so applicants should monitor their registered email addresses and the official portal regularly.

Applicants can now download their scorecards in PDF format from the SBI careers section. The scorecard indicates the marks obtained in each section of the preliminary exam, including English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability. While the exact cut-off marks may vary based on the category and region, the release of individual scores allows candidates to gauge their performance relative to the qualifying standards set by the bank.

The timing of the result release has been a focal point for online discussions, with many candidates checking for updates daily. The confirmation of the Mains dates helps alleviate uncertainty, enabling aspirants to structure their study plans effectively. SBI has urged all candidates to ensure their personal details are accurate in the system to avoid any discrepancies during the document verification stage later in the process.

Background

The SBI Clerk recruitment process typically involves three stages: a Preliminary Examination, a Mains Examination, and a final Interview or Personality Test. The preliminary stage is a qualifying exam designed to filter candidates based on their performance in objective-based questions. Only those who meet the minimum qualifying marks in each section and overall are eligible to appear for the Mains exam.

The Mains examination is more detailed and carries higher weightage in the final selection. It usually includes sections on English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, General/Banking/Financial Awareness, and Reasoning Ability. The total marks for the Mains phase are significantly higher than the preliminary stage, making it a critical determinant of the final merit list. Candidates must perform consistently well in both stages to secure a position.

In recent years, SBI has streamlined its recruitment process to handle the large volume of applications efficiently. The shift to online examinations and digital result announcements has reduced processing times and improved accessibility for candidates across different regions of India. This digital approach also minimizes errors and ensures that results are available to all eligible candidates simultaneously.

What happens next

Following the release of the preliminary results, SBI will publish the admit cards for the Mains examination. Candidates should download these cards from the official website once they become available, as they will be required to enter the exam centers. The admit cards will contain the specific date, time, and venue for the Mains test, which will be held on November 26 and 27, 2026.

After the Mains examination, the bank will evaluate the combined scores of the preliminary and Mains stages to create a merit list. Candidates who meet the cut-off criteria will be called for the final interview or personality test. This final stage assesses the candidate's communication skills, general awareness, and suitability for the role. The final list of selected candidates will be published on the SBI website after the completion of all stages.
