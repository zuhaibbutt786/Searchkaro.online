Bhubaneswar Kumar's World Cup hopes and recent BCCI decision

Bhubaneswar Kumar recently expressed his ambition to return to the Indian squad for the upcoming World Cup. The BCCI's swift decision on his status has driven a surge in online searches.

In a recent interview, he highlighted his fitness levels and readiness to contribute, emphasizing that the tournament remains his primary goal.

Bhubaneswar Kumar made his international debut in 2018 and quickly earned a reputation for steady middle‑order contributions. Although he was part of the squad that reached the 2019 World Cup final, he did not feature prominently in the latter stages, a fact he later described as a source of lingering disappointment.

Since then, Kumar has focused on domestic cricket, delivering several notable innings in the Ranji Trophy and the Indian Premier League.
