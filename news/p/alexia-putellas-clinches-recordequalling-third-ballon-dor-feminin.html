Alexia Putellas clinches record‑equalling third Ballon d’Or Feminin

Barcelona’s Alexia Putellas has secured a third Ballon d’Or Feminin, equalling the all‑time record. The win caps a night where the Catalan club dominated women’s football honors.

Latest developments

Barcelona midfielder Alexia Putellas was announced as the winner of the 2026 Ballon d’Or Feminin at a ceremony in Paris on Saturday, securing a third award and matching the record held by former winners. The accolade, presented by France Football, recognized her outstanding performances for both club and country over the past season, including leading Barcelona to a domestic double and a deep run in the UEFA Women’s Champions League. The ceremony also saw Barcelona sweep the majority of the women’s football categories, reinforcing the club’s dominance in the sport.

The voting process for the Ballon d’Or Feminin involves journalists, national team coaches and captains, each casting a ballot based on a set of criteria. Former voters, speaking to The New York Times, explained that there is no single perfect model for evaluating candidates; they consider individual statistics, impact in key matches, consistency, and contribution to team success. The lack of a universally accepted formula means that each voter balances quantitative data with qualitative judgement, a nuance that often shapes the final outcome.

Reactions from the football community were swift. ESPN India highlighted that Barcelona’s clean sweep of the women’s awards underscored the club’s strategic investment in its female squad, from youth development to senior team resources. Fellow players and coaches praised Putellas for her technical skill, leadership on the pitch, and resilience after returning from a serious injury two years earlier. Social media platforms lit up with tributes, and fans in Spain and beyond celebrated the milestone as a testament to her sustained excellence.

The win also resonated beyond Europe. In Pakistan, where interest in women’s football has been rising, broadcasters announced plans to feature highlights of the ceremony and Putellas’s career highlights on national sports channels. The record‑equalling achievement is expected to inspire young female athletes in the region, offering a visible example of success at the highest level of the sport.

Background

The Ballon d’Or Feminin was introduced in 2018 to recognise the world’s best female footballer each year. Since its inception, the award has celebrated a range of talents, from Marta’s record five wins to recent breakthroughs by players from the United States and Europe. Alexia Putellas first claimed the trophy in 2021, following a season in which she helped Barcelona secure a treble. She repeated the feat in 2022, cementing her status as one of the sport’s leading figures.

Barcelona’s women’s team has risen rapidly over the past decade, benefitting from the club’s overall infrastructure and investment. The side has claimed multiple league titles and consistently reached the latter stages of European competition. This sustained success has positioned its players, including Putellas, as regular contenders for individual honours. However, the voting system has faced scrutiny for its reliance on subjective assessments, prompting former voters to call for clearer guidelines, as reported by The New York Times.

In recent years, the award ceremony has become a platform for broader discussions about gender equity in football, with organisers emphasizing the need for equal media coverage and prize money. Putellas’s third win adds to the narrative of growing recognition for women’s achievements on the global stage.

Her performance there will likely influence the shortlist for the 2027 Ballon d’Or Feminin. Meanwhile, Barcelona will aim to retain its domestic dominance and push for a Champions League title, further solidifying its players’ candidacy for future individual awards.
