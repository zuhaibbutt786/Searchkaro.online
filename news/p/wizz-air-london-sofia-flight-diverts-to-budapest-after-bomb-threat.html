Wizz Air London-Sofia flight diverts to Budapest after bomb threat

A Wizz Air passenger jet traveling from London Luton to Sofia was forced to divert to Budapest after a bomb threat was received. Hungarian fighter jets escorted the aircraft to the airport, where all passengers and crew were safely evacuate

Latest developments

A Wizz Air flight operating from London Luton to Sofia in Bulgaria made an emergency landing at Budapest Ferenc Liszt International Airport on Sunday. The diversion was necessitated by a bomb threat received by the airline, prompting immediate safety protocols. The aircraft, which was en route to its destination in southeastern Europe, was redirected to the nearest suitable airport to ensure the safety of those on board.

Upon receiving the threat, air traffic control coordinated with Hungarian authorities to manage the situation. The flight was escorted by military fighter jets as it approached Budapest, a standard security measure for such incidents. The aircraft landed safely, and emergency services were on standby at the tarmac to respond to any potential hazards. Passengers and crew were disembarked via emergency slides and guided to a secure area for further processing.

The incident caused significant disruption to the airline's schedule, with subsequent flights likely to face delays while the aircraft undergoes a thorough security inspection. Wizz Air has not yet released a detailed statement regarding the specific nature of the threat, but confirmed that the safety of passengers and crew was the primary concern. The Bulgarian and Hungarian aviation authorities are expected to coordinate on the next steps for the passengers.

No injuries were reported during the landing or evacuation. The passengers, who were traveling for both leisure and business purposes, are currently being assisted by airline staff and local authorities. The incident highlights the rigorous security measures in place for commercial aviation in Europe, which prioritize rapid response to any potential threats to flight safety.
