Good Morning America launches 'Deals & Steals' sale, up to 85% off

Good Morning America has rolled out a massive holiday promotion called ‘Deals & Steals’, promising deep discounts on a variety of gifts. Early reports show shoppers can find items for $20 or less and savings as high as 85 percent.

Latest developments

Good Morning America (GMA) announced the launch of its annual "Deals & Steals" holiday promotion on Monday, positioning the sale as a centerpiece of the network’s seasonal shopping guide. The campaign, hosted on the GMA website and promoted through its morning broadcast, aggregates offers from a range of partner retailers, including major department stores, online marketplaces and boutique brands. The network’s digital storefront lists more than 1,000 items, each marked with a clear discount percentage and a price tag that often falls below the $20 threshold.

The promotion highlights discounts ranging from modest 20 percent cuts to steep reductions of up to 85 percent. Items span categories such as electronics, home décor, beauty products and toys, with a particular emphasis on affordable gift ideas for holiday shoppers on a budget. Several listings feature price points under $20, catering to consumers seeking value without sacrificing quality. The "Deals & Steals" page also includes a "Holiday Gifting Extravaganza" section that aggregates the deepest markdowns for high‑ticket items like smart speakers and kitchen appliances.

Media outlets across the United States have begun covering the sale. Good Morning America’s own feature story titled "GMA Deals & Steals Holiday Gifting Extravaganza" outlines the top offers and provides shopping tips. Philadelphia station 6abc ran a piece called "GMA Deals & Steals on Gifts $20 & Under," emphasizing the low‑cost options available to local shoppers. International coverage, such as the article on Demokratis.co.id, noted that the promotion includes seven highlighted deals with discounts up to 85 percent, underscoring the global interest in American holiday sales trends.

Within hours of the launch, the GMA website reported a surge in traffic, with visitors spending an average of eight minutes per session browsing the curated deals. Social media platforms reflected the buzz, as users shared screenshots of steep price cuts and tagged the network’s official accounts. Early sales data, though not yet released by GMA, suggest that the promotion is resonating with price‑sensitive consumers who are planning their holiday purchases ahead of the traditional Black Friday rush.

Why it matters

The "Deals & Steals" promotion arrives at a critical moment for US consumers who are still navigating the lingering effects of inflation and supply‑chain disruptions. By offering deep discounts on everyday items and gifts, GMA provides a low‑cost alternative to the high‑priced promotions typically seen at major retailers during the holiday season. This can help families stretch limited budgets while still participating in the cultural tradition of gift‑giving.

Retail analysts note that media‑driven sales events, such as those hosted by television networks, can shift consumer attention away from traditional e‑commerce giants like Amazon and Walmart. The GMA campaign leverages the network's trusted brand and its large morning‑viewership, potentially redirecting a portion of holiday spend toward the featured partners. This redistribution of consumer dollars may influence pricing strategies across the broader retail landscape, prompting competitors to deepen their own discount offers.

Furthermore, the emphasis on sub‑$20 items aligns with a growing segment of shoppers who prioritize value over brand prestige. By curating affordable options, GMA taps into a demographic that is increasingly influential in shaping holiday sales trends, especially among younger consumers who rely heavily on digital media for shopping inspiration.

Background

Good Morning America, a flagship morning program on ABC, has a history of integrating lifestyle content with consumer-oriented segments. Over the past decade, the network has periodically introduced shopping guides and curated product round‑ups, leveraging its national platform to influence purchasing decisions. The "Deals & Steals" initiative builds on this tradition, expanding the concept from brief on‑air mentions to a full‑scale online marketplace.

Historically, holiday shopping in the United States peaks during the weeks leading up to Thanksgiving, with Black Friday and Cyber Monday serving as the primary discount events. However, recent years have seen a fragmentation of the holiday sales calendar, as retailers launch pre‑Black Friday promotions to capture early‑bird shoppers. GMA’s timing—mid‑November—positions its sale to capture both early planners and those who prefer to avoid the chaos of the traditional shopping weekend.

The promotion also reflects a broader media trend where broadcasters monetize their audience through e‑commerce partnerships. By hosting a dedicated shopping portal, GMA can generate affiliate revenue while providing viewers with a convenient, trusted source for deals. This model mirrors similar efforts by other networks and lifestyle brands that have entered the online retail space.

What happens next

GMA has indicated that the "Deals & Steals" sale will run through the end of December, with weekly updates that introduce new products and refreshed discount levels. Consumers can expect additional spotlight features on emerging gift categories, such as sustainable home goods and tech accessories, as the holiday season progresses. Retail partners have signaled willingness to extend select offers based on performance metrics, suggesting that the promotion could evolve in response to shopper demand.

Industry watchers will monitor sales data released by GMA in early January to assess the campaign's impact on overall holiday spending. If the promotion proves successful, it may set a precedent for future collaborations between broadcast media and retail partners, potentially reshaping how holiday deals are marketed to American audiences.
