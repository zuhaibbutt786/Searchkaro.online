Get premium Udemy courses for free

Use instructor 100% OFF coupons. Lifetime access when enrollment succeeds.

  1. Browse the free course list.
  2. Click Enroll Now Free — opens Udemy with the coupon applied when possible.
  3. Check the price shows Free / $0 / 100% off.
  4. Add to cart and complete checkout (total should be zero).
  5. Find the course under Udemy → My Learning.

Note: Coupons can expire or hit a redemption limit (often ~1,000 uses). If a course is not free, the coupon is gone — pick another.
