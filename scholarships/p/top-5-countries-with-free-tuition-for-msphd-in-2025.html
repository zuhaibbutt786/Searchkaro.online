Top 5 Countries with Free Tuition for MS/PhD in 2025

Discover five European countries that offer free or minimal tuition for international MS and PhD students. Norway, Austria, Germany, Finland, and Sweden provide diverse scholarship schemes and living support. Students from Pakistan and India can pursue advanced degrees abroad without hefty tuition fees. Find out how to qualify, the benefits, and the application steps in this guide.

Eligibility

  • International students with a valid passport
  • Applicants holding a master’s degree or equivalent for PhD programs
  • Proof of academic excellence and language proficiency (English or German)

Benefits

  • No tuition fees or very low tuition rates
  • Comprehensive living stipends or cost-of-living allowances
  • Access to world‑class research facilities and academic networks

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Identify the university and program that offers free tuition and check its official website for eligibility criteria.
  2. Step 2: Prepare application documents—transcripts, recommendation letters, statement of purpose, and language test scores.
  3. Step 3: Submit the application through the university’s online portal and monitor the status via email notifications.

FAQs

Do I need to pay any tuition fees in these countries?

Most universities in Norway, Austria, Germany, Finland, and Sweden waive tuition fees for international students, but some may charge a small semester contribution. Always verify the exact fee structure on the university’s official site.

Will I receive a stipend for living expenses?

Many programs provide a monthly stipend or a cost‑of‑living allowance, especially in Germany and Finland. The amount varies by university and program, so check the specific scholarship details for each institution.
