Commonwealth PhD Scholarships for Pakistan & India Students

The Commonwealth Scholarships program offers fully funded opportunities for students from developing Commonwealth countries to pursue PhD studies in the UK. It supports research in fields critical to the development of the applicant's home country. Candidates from Pakistan and India can access this prestigious funding to advance their academic careers globally.

Eligibility

  • Citizens of a developing Commonwealth country, including Pakistan and India.
  • Must have a first-class or upper second-class undergraduate degree.
  • Must be applying for a full-time PhD program at a UK university.

Benefits

  • Full tuition fees and a monthly stipend for living expenses.
  • Return airfare to the UK and visa fees.

How to apply

  1. Secure a formal offer from a UK university for a PhD program.
  2. Complete the online Commonwealth Scholarship application form.
  3. Submit required documents, including transcripts and a research proposal.

FAQs

Are students from Pakistan and India eligible?

Yes, Pakistan and India are member states of the Commonwealth and are eligible to apply for these scholarships.

Does the scholarship cover tuition fees?

Yes, the scholarship typically covers full tuition fees and provides a stipend for living expenses.
