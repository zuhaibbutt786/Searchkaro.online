Commonwealth PhD Scholarships for Pakistan & India Students

The Commonwealth Scholarships support high-potential students from developing member countries pursuing postgraduate studies at UK universities. These awards cover tuition fees and living expenses for Master's and PhD candidates. Applicants must demonstrate strong academic records and a commitment to returning to their home country to contribute to development. This is a prestigious opportunity for researchers in Pakistan, India, and other Commonwealth nations.

Eligibility

  • Citizens of a developing Commonwealth country, including Pakistan and India.
  • Holding a first-class or upper second-class Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) for Master's, or a Master's degree for PhD.
  • Must have been born in and be a resident of a developing Commonwealth country.

Benefits

  • Full tuition fees for the duration of the course.
  • Monthly living allowance and return airfare to the UK.

How to apply

  1. Register on the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission website and create a profile.
  2. Select your preferred UK universities and courses that accept Commonwealth Scholars.
  3. Submit your application form, academic transcripts, and required references before the deadline.

FAQs

Are students from Pakistan and India eligible for Commonwealth Scholarships?

Yes, Pakistan and India are developing Commonwealth countries, so their citizens are eligible to apply for these scholarships.

Do I need to apply to the university separately?

Yes, you must apply to the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission and also apply directly to your chosen UK university for admission.
