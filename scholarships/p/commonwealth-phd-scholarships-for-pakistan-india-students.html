Commonwealth PhD Scholarships for Pakistan & India Students

The Commonwealth Scholarships fund postgraduate study for citizens of developing Commonwealth countries. These awards support high-caliber students pursuing Master's or PhD degrees at UK universities. The program aims to build leadership capacity and foster international cooperation. It is a prestigious opportunity for students from Pakistan and India to study abroad.

Eligibility

  • Citizenship of a developing Commonwealth country, including Pakistan and India.
  • Admission to a full-time postgraduate Master's or PhD program at a UK university.
  • Demonstrated academic excellence and potential to lead in your field.

Benefits

  • Full tuition fee coverage for the duration of the course.
  • Monthly maintenance grant to cover living expenses in the UK.
  • Return airfare between the home country and the UK.

How to apply

  1. Secure an unconditional offer from a participating UK university.
  2. Complete the online application form on the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission website.
  3. Submit required documents, including transcripts and a personal statement.

FAQs

Are students from Pakistan and India eligible?

Yes, citizens of Pakistan and India are eligible as both are developing Commonwealth countries.

Does the scholarship cover tuition fees?

Yes, the award typically includes full tuition fees and a maintenance stipend.
