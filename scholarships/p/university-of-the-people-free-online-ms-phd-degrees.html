University of the People: Free Online MS & PhD Degrees

University of the People offers fully online, tuition‑free MS and PhD degrees that are accessible to students in Pakistan, India, and around the globe. The programs are designed to fit busy schedules, allowing learners to study at their own pace while earning a recognized credential. Admission is open to anyone who meets the basic academic and English proficiency requirements.

Eligibility

  • High school diploma or equivalent with a minimum GPA requirement.
  • Proof of English proficiency (TOEFL, IELTS, or equivalent).
  • Valid government ID and proof of residency.

Benefits

  • Zero tuition fees – only a modest application fee.
  • Fully online courses that fit any schedule.

How to apply

  1. Fill out the online application form on the University of the People website.
  2. Upload transcripts, English proficiency scores, and identification documents.
  3. Submit the application and wait for the admission decision via email.

FAQs

Are there any hidden costs for the program?

No. The only fee is a small application fee. All course materials and instruction are provided free of charge.

Can I study while working?

Yes. The flexible online format allows you to balance coursework with employment or other commitments.
