UNICAF Scholarships for Online MS & PhD Degrees at UK Universities

UNICAF offers partial scholarships to help students from Pakistan, India, and globally pursue online Master's and PhD programs at accredited UK institutions. These awards reduce tuition costs while allowing you to study remotely without relocating. The program is designed for working professionals and students seeking flexible, high-quality postgraduate education.

Eligibility

  • Hold a relevant undergraduate degree for MS or a Master's degree for PhD applications.
  • Demonstrate strong academic performance and professional experience.
  • Be a citizen of an eligible country, including Pakistan and India, or meet specific regional criteria.

Benefits

  • Significant reduction in tuition fees for online postgraduate studies.
  • Access to accredited UK university degrees with global recognition.

How to apply

  1. Create an account on the UNICAF website and select your desired online MS or PhD program.
  2. Submit your academic transcripts, CV, and a statement of purpose through the application portal.
  3. Await the scholarship decision and enroll if selected, paying the remaining tuition fees.

FAQs

Are UNICAF online degrees recognized in Pakistan and India?

Yes, UNICAF partners with accredited UK universities, so the degrees are generally recognized by employers and educational bodies in Pakistan, India, and globally.

Do I need to relocate to the UK to study?

No, these are online programs, allowing you to complete your MS or PhD from your home country while working.
