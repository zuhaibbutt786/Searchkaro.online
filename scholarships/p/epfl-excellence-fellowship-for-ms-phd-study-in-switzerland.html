EPFL Excellence Fellowship for MS & PhD – Study in Switzerland

The EPFL Excellence Fellowship enables outstanding students from Pakistan and India to pursue full‑time MS or PhD programmes at EPFL, one of Europe's leading research universities. The award covers tuition and provides a living stipend, allowing scholars to focus on cutting‑edge research. Candidates are selected based on academic excellence, research potential, and leadership qualities. This fellowship opens doors to world‑class facilities, international networks, and a vibrant campus life in Switzerland.

Eligibility

  • Citizenship of Pakistan or India (or permanent residency).
  • Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) with a minimum GPA of 3.0/4.0.
  • Strong academic and research record with relevant publications or projects.
  • Proficiency in English (IELTS ≥ 6.5 or TOEFL ≥ 90).

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver for the entire MS or PhD programme at EPFL.
  • Living stipend that covers accommodation, food, and daily expenses.
  • Access to EPFL’s state‑of‑the‑art labs, libraries, and mentorship from leading researchers.
  • Travel grant for arrival and departure to Switzerland.

How to apply

  1. Create an EPFL admission profile and secure admission to an eligible MS/PhD programme.
  2. Complete the EPFL Excellence Fellowship online form and upload your CV, research proposal, academic transcripts, and two recommendation letters.
  3. Submit the application before the EPFL deadline and wait for the selection committee’s decision.

FAQs

Can I apply if I am currently enrolled in a master’s programme?

Yes. The fellowship is open to both new MS entrants and PhD candidates. You must have a confirmed admission offer from EPFL before the fellowship deadline.

Will the fellowship cover health insurance and visa costs?

The fellowship includes a travel grant for airfare and a living stipend that can be used for health insurance and visa fees, but these costs are not item‑by‑item listed.
