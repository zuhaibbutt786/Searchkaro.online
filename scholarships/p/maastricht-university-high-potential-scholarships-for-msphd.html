Maastricht University High Potential Scholarships for MS/PhD

Maastricht University offers the High Potential Scholarship to attract exceptional international talent for Master's and PhD programs. This merit-based award supports students from Pakistan, India, and worldwide who demonstrate academic excellence. The scholarship aims to reduce financial barriers for high-achieving candidates pursuing advanced degrees in the Netherlands.

Eligibility

  • Applicants must be admitted to a full-time Master's or PhD program at Maastricht University.
  • Candidates must demonstrate high academic potential and a strong track record in their field of study.
  • Open to international students, including those from Pakistan and India, who meet the university's entry requirements.
  • Applicants must not be Dutch citizens or have Dutch nationality.

Benefits

  • Provides a substantial reduction in tuition fees for the first year of study.
  • Enhances the financial feasibility of studying in the Netherlands for international students.

How to apply

  1. First, apply and gain admission to a full-time Master's or PhD program at Maastricht University.
  2. Once admitted, submit a separate application for the High Potential Scholarship via the university's online portal.
  3. Provide supporting documents, such as transcripts and a motivation letter, highlighting your academic achievements and potential.

FAQs

Is the High Potential Scholarship available for students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, the scholarship is open to all international students, including those from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic criteria and are admitted to a qualifying program.

Do I need to apply for the scholarship before getting admission?

No, you must first be admitted to a Master's or PhD program at Maastricht University before you can apply for the High Potential Scholarship.

Does the scholarship cover living expenses?

The scholarship primarily focuses on reducing tuition fees. It does not typically cover living expenses or health insurance, so students should budget for these separately.
