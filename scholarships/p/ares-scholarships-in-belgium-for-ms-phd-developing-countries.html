ARES Scholarships in Belgium for MS & PhD: Developing Countries

The ARES Scholarship Program supports students from developing countries pursuing Master's or PhD degrees in Belgium. It aims to foster academic exchange and capacity building in regions like Pakistan and India. This program covers tuition and living expenses to help international researchers succeed in European institutions.

Eligibility

  • Citizens of eligible developing countries, including Pakistan and India
  • Applicants must be admitted to a Belgian university for an MS or PhD program
  • Candidates must not hold Belgian or EU citizenship

Benefits

  • Covers full tuition fees and registration costs
  • Provides a monthly living allowance and travel support

How to apply

  1. Secure admission to a Belgian university for an MS or PhD program
  2. Submit the application form via the ARES online portal
  3. Upload required documents including transcripts and passport copy

FAQs

Is the ARES scholarship available for Pakistani and Indian students?

Yes, the program is open to citizens from eligible developing countries, which typically includes Pakistan and India.

Does the ARES scholarship cover tuition fees?

Yes, the scholarship generally covers tuition fees, registration, and provides a monthly living allowance.
