NL Scholarship for MS at Erasmus Rotterdam: 2027 Guide

The NL Scholarship, formerly known as the Holland Scholarship, offers €5,000 in funding for the first year of a Master's degree at Erasmus University Rotterdam. This opportunity is specifically designed for non-EEA students, making it highly relevant for applicants from Pakistan and India. Deadlines vary by faculty, so prospective students must verify specific dates for their chosen department. Courses are scheduled to start in the 2027/28 academic year.

Eligibility

  • Applicants must be from outside the European Economic Area (non-EEA), including Pakistan and India.
  • Must be admitted to a full-time Bachelor's or Master's program at Erasmus University Rotterdam.
  • Must not have previously received a Dutch government scholarship for the same study level.

Benefits

  • Covers €5,000 of tuition fees for the first year of study.
  • Enhances the financial feasibility of studying in the Netherlands for international students.

How to apply

  1. Secure admission to a qualifying Master's program at Erasmus University Rotterdam.
  2. Check the specific application deadline for your faculty, as dates vary by department.
  3. Submit the scholarship application via the university's portal before the faculty-specific deadline.

FAQs

Is the NL Scholarship available for Pakistani and Indian students?

Yes, the scholarship is open to all students from outside the EEA, which includes Pakistan and India.

How much does the NL Scholarship cover?

It provides a fixed amount of €5,000 towards tuition fees for the first year of your Master's degree.

When is the application deadline for 2027 intake?

Deadlines vary by faculty. For example, the Institute of Housing and Urban Development (IHS) has a deadline of 25 April 2027. Always check your specific program's date.
