University of Twente Scholarships (UTS) for MS/PhD students in Pakistan and India

Explore University of Twente Scholarships (UTS) for Pakistani and Indian MS/PhD students seeking opportunities abroad. Discover eligibility criteria, benefits, and comprehensive application process steps.

Eligibility

  • Nationals of Pakistan or India
  • MS/PhD level
  • Excellent academic record
  • English proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL or equivalent)

Benefits

  • Full or partial tuition fee waivers
  • Monthly stipend
  • Living expenses allowance
  • Insurance coverage
  • Opportunities for research collaboration

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Check program requirements and eligibility criteria on the University of Twente website
  2. Step 2: Prepare your academic documents, English proficiency certificates, and research proposal
  3. Step 3: Submit your application through the University of Twente portal, along with required supporting documents
  4. Step 4: Attend interviews (if applicable), and await scholarship decision
  5. Step 5: Accept the offer and start your studies at the University of Twente

FAQs

Can I apply for UTS if I'm not from Pakistan or India?

No, the University of Twente Scholarships (UTS) are specifically designed for Pakistani and Indian nationals at the MS/PhD level.

What are the scholarship deadlines?

Please refer to the University of Twente website for the latest scholarship deadlines, which may vary each year. Ensure to apply well before the deadline to allow sufficient time for application preparation and review.
