TU Delft MS/PhD: Justus & Louise van Effen Scholarships

The Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarships support outstanding international students pursuing Master's or PhD programs at Delft University of Technology. This prestigious award covers tuition fees for the first year of study. It is a highly competitive opportunity for candidates from Pakistan, India, and other global regions. Applicants must demonstrate academic excellence and leadership potential.

Eligibility

  • Applying for a full-time Master's or PhD program at TU Delft
  • Non-EU/EEA nationals with a strong academic record
  • Must not have previously received this specific scholarship

Benefits

  • Full coverage of tuition fees for the first year
  • Enhanced CV with a prestigious Dutch scholarship title

How to apply

  1. Submit your application for the TU Delft program first
  2. Complete the separate Justus & Louise van Effen scholarship form
  3. Upload required documents including transcripts and motivation letter

FAQs

Is this scholarship available for students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, it is open to all non-EU/EEA international students, including those from Pakistan and India.

Does the scholarship cover living expenses?

No, it primarily covers tuition fees. Students must secure separate funding for living costs.
