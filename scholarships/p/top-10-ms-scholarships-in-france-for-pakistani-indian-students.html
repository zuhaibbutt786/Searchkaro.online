Top 10 MS Scholarships in France for Pakistani & Indian Students

France is a leading destination for Master's studies, offering world-class education and diverse funding opportunities. This guide highlights the top 10 scholarships available for international students, specifically targeting applicants from Pakistan and India. We cover key eligibility criteria and application processes to help you secure your spot for the 2026 academic year. Explore these programs to reduce your study costs and gain global exposure.

Eligibility

  • Holding a recognized Bachelor's degree from a Pakistani or Indian university
  • Demonstrating strong academic performance and relevant work experience
  • Meeting specific language requirements (English or French) for the chosen program

Benefits

  • Covers tuition fees and provides a monthly living stipend
  • Includes health insurance and travel expenses for international students

How to apply

  1. Shortlist programs and verify specific university or government portal requirements
  2. Prepare essential documents including transcripts, CV, and statement of purpose
  3. Submit applications before the official deadlines via the designated online portals

FAQs

Are these scholarships open to students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, most of the listed scholarships are open to all international students, including those from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic criteria.

Do I need to know French to apply for an MS in France?

Not necessarily. Many Master's programs are taught in English, but knowing basic French can be an advantage for daily life and some specific scholarship requirements.

When should I start applying for the 2026 intake?

It is recommended to begin preparing your documents and submitting applications at least 6-12 months before the start of the academic year, as deadlines vary by institution.
