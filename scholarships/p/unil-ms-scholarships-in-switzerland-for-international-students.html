UNIL MS Scholarships in Switzerland for International Students

The University of Lausanne (UNIL) offers fully funded master's scholarships for students worldwide, including Pakistan and India. These grants cover tuition, living expenses, and travel costs, enabling scholars to study in Switzerland's vibrant academic environment. Applicants benefit from world‑class research facilities and a multicultural campus. The program aims to attract top talent and foster international collaboration.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) with a strong academic record (typically minimum 3.0 GPA).
  • Proficiency in English (IELTS/TOEFL) or French, depending on the program language.
  • Citizenship of Pakistan, India, or any other country; applicants must be non‑Swiss residents.

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver for the entire master’s program.
  • Monthly living stipend and a travel grant for round‑trip airfare.

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Review the UNIL scholarship portal and confirm you meet all eligibility criteria.
  2. Step 2: Prepare required documents (transcripts, language test scores, recommendation letters, statement of purpose).
  3. Step 3: Submit the online application before the deadline and await the selection committee’s response.

FAQs

Can students from Pakistan and India apply for the UNIL master’s grant?

Yes, the scholarship is open to applicants of any nationality, including Pakistani and Indian students.

Is there a deadline for the scholarship application?

UNIL typically opens applications in the autumn for the following academic year; check the official portal for the exact dates each cycle.
