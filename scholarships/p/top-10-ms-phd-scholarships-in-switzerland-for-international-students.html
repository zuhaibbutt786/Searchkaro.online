Top 10 MS PhD Scholarships in Switzerland for International Students

Switzerland offers a range of scholarships that enable international students to pursue Master's and PhD programmes at world‑class universities. These awards often cover tuition fees and may include a living stipend, making Swiss education more accessible. Leading Swiss institutions and research bodies provide these opportunities to attract top talent worldwide. Students from Pakistan and India can apply alongside candidates from other countries.

Eligibility

  • Strong academic record (minimum GPA or equivalent).
  • Proof of English or French proficiency, depending on the programme language.
  • A clear research proposal or statement of purpose for PhD applicants.

Benefits

  • Full or partial tuition fee waiver.
  • Monthly stipend to support living expenses during the study period.

How to apply

  1. Identify the scholarship(s) that match your field and level, and note the specific university requirements.
  2. Prepare required documents: transcripts, language test scores, recommendation letters, and a research proposal or study plan.
  3. Submit the online application before the deadline and monitor your email for any follow‑up requests.

FAQs

Can students from Pakistan and India apply for these Swiss scholarships?

Yes, applicants from Pakistan, India and any other country are eligible, provided they meet the academic and language criteria.

Do these scholarships cover living costs?

Many of the scholarships include a monthly stipend for living expenses, though the amount varies by programme and sponsor.
