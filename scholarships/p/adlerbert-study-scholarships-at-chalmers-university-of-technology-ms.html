Adlerbert Study Scholarships at Chalmers University of Technology (MS)

Apply for the Adlerbert Study Scholarships at Chalmers University of Technology for MS studies in Sweden. These scholarships are open to international students from Pakistan and India, offering opportunities for global career growth. Deadline: 15 January 2027. Course starts August 2026.

Eligibility

  • International students from Pakistan and India
  • MS degree applicants
  • Applicants must have a bachelor's degree from an accredited institution

Benefits

  • Financial support for tuition fees and living expenses
  • Opportunity to study at a renowned European university
  • Networking with a diverse community of scholars

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Research and apply for the Adlerbert Study Scholarships at Chalmers University of Technology
  2. Step 2: Prepare your application materials (CV, academic transcripts, research proposal)
  3. Step 3: Submit your application before the deadline (15 January 2027) and wait for the outcome

FAQs

Can I apply for other scholarships in addition to the Adlerbert Study Scholarships?

While we encourage you to explore other scholarship opportunities, please ensure that you meet the eligibility criteria of both the Adlerbert Study Scholarships and the additional scholarships you are applying for.

Do I need to speak Swedish to study at Chalmers University of Technology?

Chalmers University of Technology offers courses in English, so you do not need to speak Swedish to study there. However, basic knowledge of the Swedish language may be beneficial for your overall experience.
