Reach Oxford Scholarships for MS/PhD Students

The Reach Oxford Scholarship is a prestigious award for students from developing countries who want to pursue a Master's or PhD at Oxford University. It provides a fully funded pathway to world‑class education, covering tuition, living costs and related expenses. The program is highly competitive and aims to nurture academic excellence and cultural diversity. Successful scholars are expected to return to their home country and contribute to its development.

Eligibility

  • Citizen of a developing country such as Pakistan or India
  • Accepted into an Oxford University MS or PhD program
  • Strong academic record and commitment to return home after graduation

Benefits

  • Full tuition fees covered
  • Living allowance, travel and other study‑related costs

How to apply

  1. Check eligibility and choose an Oxford MS/PhD course
  2. Apply to Oxford University via the online admissions portal
  3. Complete the Reach Oxford Scholarship form and submit required documents

FAQs

Who can apply for the Reach Oxford Scholarship?

Students who are citizens of developing countries—including Pakistan and India—who have secured an offer for an Oxford MS or PhD program.

What expenses does the scholarship cover?

The scholarship funds full tuition, a living stipend, travel to and from Oxford, and other essential study‑related costs.
