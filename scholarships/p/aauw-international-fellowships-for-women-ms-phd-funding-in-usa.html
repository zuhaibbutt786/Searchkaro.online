AAUW International Fellowships for Women – MS & PhD Funding in USA

The AAUW International Fellowships support women worldwide who wish to pursue graduate study in the United States. They are open to Pakistani, Indian and other non‑U.S. citizens aiming for an MS or PhD. Fellows receive financial assistance that helps cover tuition, fees, and living expenses while they advance their research and career goals. The program also connects scholars to a global network of women leaders.

Eligibility

  • Female applicants who are non‑U.S. citizens or permanent residents.
  • Enrolled or planning to enroll in a full‑time MS or PhD program at an accredited U.S. university.
  • Demonstrated academic excellence and a commitment to advancing women’s issues.

Benefits

  • Financial support that can cover tuition, fees, and living expenses for the duration of the graduate program.
  • Access to AAUW’s mentorship, professional development resources, and a worldwide network of women scholars.

How to apply

  1. Review the eligibility criteria and gather required documents (transcripts, recommendation letters, research proposal, etc.).
  2. Create an account on the AAUW website and complete the online application form.
  3. Submit the application before the deadline and monitor your email for confirmation and any follow‑up requests.

FAQs

Can Indian or Pakistani students apply if they are already studying in the USA?

Yes, the fellowship is open to any non‑U.S. citizen or permanent resident, regardless of whether you are currently enrolled in the United States or plan to start your program there.

What types of expenses does the AAUW fellowship cover?

The award can be used for tuition, mandatory fees, books, research costs, and reasonable living expenses such as housing and meals.
