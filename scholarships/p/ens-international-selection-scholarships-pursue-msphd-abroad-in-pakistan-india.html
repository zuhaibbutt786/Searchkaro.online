ENS International Selection Scholarships: Pursue MS/PhD Abroad in Pakistan & India

Experience world-class education with ENS International Selection Scholarships. Apply for MS/PhD programs abroad while exploring opportunities in Pakistan and India. These scholarships cater to international students seeking funding.

Eligibility

  • Nationals of Pakistan or India
  • Currently studying in Pakistan or India
  • Enrolled in a recognized Pakistani or Indian university
  • Pursuing MS or PhD programs at a foreign institution
  • Meet the required academic and language proficiency criteria

Benefits

  • Funding for MS/PhD programs abroad
  • Global networking opportunities
  • Access to renowned educational institutions
  • Cultural immersion in Pakistan and India
  • Development of research skills

How to apply

  1. Visit the official ENS International Selection Scholarships website
  2. Check eligibility criteria and requirements
  3. Download and fill out the application form
  4. Submit the completed application along with required documents
  5. Attend a scholarship interview (if selected)
  6. Receive funding for MS/PhD programs abroad

FAQs

When is the scholarship application deadline?

Please refer to the official ENS International Selection Scholarships website for updated application deadlines. Keep in mind that application deadlines may vary each year, so ensure you check the latest information before applying.

What are the stipends provided?

The scholarship stipend amount may vary based on each applicant's profile. Applicants will be awarded funding for MS/PhD programs abroad. For more detailed information, please visit the ENS International Selection Scholarships website.
