University of the People Tuition-Free MS & PhD Online Degrees

University of the People offers fully online, tuition-free master's and doctoral programs for students worldwide. While there are no tuition fees, students pay a small assessment fee per course. This makes advanced education accessible for professionals in Pakistan and India without high costs.

Eligibility

  • Hold a relevant bachelor's degree for MS programs or a master's degree for PhD programs.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in English through standardized tests or prior academic work.
  • Be a legal resident of any country, including Pakistan and India.

Benefits

  • No tuition fees, only low-cost assessment fees per course.
  • 100% online format allows study while working in Pakistan or India.

How to apply

  1. Create an account on the University of the People website.
  2. Submit your academic transcripts and English proficiency proof.
  3. Complete the application form and pay the initial assessment fee.

FAQs

Is University of the People accredited?

Yes, it is accredited by the Distance Education Accrediting Commission (DEAC) in the USA.

Can students from Pakistan and India apply?

Yes, the university accepts applications from students globally, including those in South Asia.
