10+ Scholarships in Canada for MS & PhD International Students

Canada offers a range of merit‑based scholarships for international graduate students. Whether you aim for a Master's or a PhD, top Canadian universities provide tuition waivers, living allowances, and research funding. These awards are open to applicants from Pakistan, India, and worldwide. Explore the options and start your journey toward a world‑class education without financial burden.

Eligibility

  • Citizenship of Pakistan, India, or any non‑Canadian country
  • Accepted admission offer for a full‑time MS or PhD program at a Canadian university
  • Minimum GPA of 3.0 (or equivalent) and demonstrated academic or research excellence

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver plus a yearly stipend for living expenses
  • Access to research labs, mentorship, and networking opportunities with industry partners

How to apply

  1. Secure an admission letter from a Canadian university offering the scholarship
  2. Complete the scholarship application form and upload transcripts, CV, and recommendation letters
  3. Submit the application before the university‑specified deadline and await the selection decision

FAQs

Can I apply for more than one Canadian scholarship at the same time?

Yes. You may submit separate applications to each scholarship program, but you can only accept one award per academic year.

Do I need to prove English or French proficiency?

All scholarships require proof of language proficiency, typically IELTS, TOEFL, or TEF scores that meet the university’s minimum standards.

Will the scholarship cover health insurance and travel costs?

Most awards cover tuition and a living stipend; health insurance and airfare are usually excluded, though some programs may offer additional allowances.
