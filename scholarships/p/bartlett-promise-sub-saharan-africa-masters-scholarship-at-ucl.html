Bartlett Promise Sub-Saharan Africa Master’s Scholarship at UCL

2027-28 Bartlett Promise Sub-Saharan Africa Master's Scholarships are open to nationals and residents of eligible Sub-Saharan African countries in financial need. The scholarships cover full overseas tuition fees, a £18,348 stipend, flights and visa costs for a Master's at the Bartlett, UCL's Faculty of the Built Environment in the UK.
