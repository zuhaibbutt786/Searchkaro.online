5+ Chancellor/Vice Chancellor Scholarships for MS & PhD Students

Chancellor and Vice‑Chancellor scholarships are among the most respected awards offered by top UK and Australian universities. They provide full or partial tuition waivers and often include a living stipend for outstanding international candidates. These scholarships target MS and PhD programmes and are open to students from Pakistan, India and other countries. Learn how to qualify, apply, and maximise your chances of receiving this elite funding.

Eligibility

  • Citizens of Pakistan, India, or any other country with a valid passport.
  • Academic excellence demonstrated by a minimum 3.5 GPA/percentage or equivalent in the most recent degree.
  • Admission offer (unconditional or conditional) for a full‑time MS or PhD programme at a participating university.

Benefits

  • Full or partial tuition fee waiver for the entire duration of the MS/PhD programme.
  • Monthly stipend or bursary to cover living expenses, travel allowances may also be offered.

How to apply

  1. Secure an unconditional or conditional admission offer from a participating university.
  2. Complete the university’s scholarship application form and upload required documents (transcripts, CV, research proposal, recommendation letters).
  3. Submit the application before the university‑specified deadline and await the selection committee’s decision.

FAQs

Can students from Pakistan or India apply for these scholarships?

Yes, the Chancellor and Vice‑Chancellor scholarships are open to all international applicants, including students from Pakistan and India.

Do I need to have a research proposal to apply?

A research proposal is required for PhD applicants and strongly recommended for MS candidates, as it helps the selection panel assess academic potential.
