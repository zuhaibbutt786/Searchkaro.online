Clarendon Fund Scholarships for MS & PhD at Oxford

The Clarendon Fund Scholarships are prestigious, full-time awards at the University of Oxford supporting postgraduate research and taught degrees. These scholarships cover tuition fees and provide a generous maintenance stipend to support living costs. They are open to students from all countries, including Pakistan and India, who demonstrate exceptional academic potential. Applicants must first secure a place on a relevant Oxford course before being considered for funding.

Eligibility

  • Must be a new postgraduate student starting a full-time course at Oxford
  • Applicants from any country, including Pakistan and India, are eligible
  • Must have already applied for and been offered a place on a relevant Oxford course
  • Demonstrates exceptional academic ability and potential for research or professional excellence

Benefits

  • Covers full tuition fees for the duration of the course
  • Provides a maintenance stipend to cover living expenses in Oxford

How to apply

  1. Apply for your desired MS or PhD course at Oxford via the graduate admissions portal
  2. Indicate your interest in Clarendon Fund Scholarships in your application or separate nomination form
  3. Submit required supporting documents, including transcripts and references, by the course-specific deadline

FAQs

Is the Clarendon Fund Scholarship available for Pakistani and Indian students?

Yes, the scholarship is open to students of all nationalities, including those from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic criteria.

Do I need to apply separately for the Clarendon Fund Scholarship?

You must apply for the Oxford course first. Depending on the specific course, you may need to indicate your interest in the Clarendon Fund in your application or submit a separate nomination form.
