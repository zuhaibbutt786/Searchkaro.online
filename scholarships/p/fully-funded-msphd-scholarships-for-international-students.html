Fully Funded MS/PhD Scholarships for International Students

Fully funded scholarships enable students from Pakistan, India and around the world to pursue MS or PhD programmes abroad without tuition costs. These awards typically cover tuition, living expenses, health insurance and travel allowances. Recipients join a global network of scholars, gaining academic and professional exposure. The scholarships are open to high‑achieving graduates seeking advanced research or professional training.

Eligibility

  • Citizens of Pakistan, India or any other country with a valid passport
  • Completed a bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA (or equivalent) for MS programmes, or a master's degree for PhD programmes
  • Proof of English proficiency (e.g., TOEFL, IELTS) as required by the host university

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver for the entire duration of the programme
  • Monthly stipend covering living costs, health insurance and travel allowances

How to apply

  1. Research universities and programmes that offer fully funded scholarships and shortlist those matching your academic goals
  2. Prepare required documents – academic transcripts, letters of recommendation, statement of purpose, CV and language test scores
  3. Submit the scholarship application through the university’s online portal before the announced deadline

FAQs

Can students from Pakistan and India apply for these scholarships?

Yes, the scholarships are open to all international applicants, including students from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic and language requirements.

What expenses are covered by a fully funded scholarship?

Typically the scholarship covers tuition fees, a monthly living stipend, health insurance, and a travel grant for arrival and departure.
