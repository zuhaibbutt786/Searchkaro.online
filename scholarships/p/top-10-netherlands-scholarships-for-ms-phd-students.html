Top 10 Netherlands Scholarships for MS & PhD Students

The Netherlands ranks among Europe’s top study destinations alongside the UK and Germany. Dutch universities and the government offer a wide range of scholarships for international MS and PhD candidates. This guide highlights the ten most competitive awards, covering tuition waivers, living stipends, and research grants. Learn how to boost your chances and start your European academic journey today.

Eligibility

  • Citizenship of Pakistan or India (or other non‑EU countries).
  • Accepted admission offer for a full‑time MS or PhD program at a Dutch university.
  • Academic excellence – typically a minimum GPA of 3.0/4.0 or equivalent.
  • Proficiency in English (IELTS/TOEFL) or Dutch, depending on the program.

Benefits

  • Full or partial tuition waiver for the entire study period.
  • Monthly living allowance, health insurance, and travel grant (where applicable).

How to apply

  1. Secure an admission letter from a Dutch university offering your chosen MS/PhD program.
  2. Prepare required documents: academic transcripts, CV, research proposal (for PhD), language test scores, and recommendation letters.
  3. Submit the scholarship application through the university portal or the Dutch government scholarship portal before the announced deadline.

FAQs

Can I apply for more than one Dutch scholarship at the same time?

Yes. Applicants may submit multiple applications, but each scholarship has its own eligibility rules and selection process. Ensure you meet the criteria for each.

Will the scholarship cover my travel expenses to the Netherlands?

Some scholarships include a one‑time travel grant, while others focus on tuition and living costs only. Check the specific award details for travel coverage.

Do I need to know Dutch to study on a scholarship?

Most MS and PhD programs for international students are taught in English, so Dutch is not mandatory. However, learning basic Dutch can help with daily life and integration.

What is the typical duration of these scholarships?

MS scholarships usually cover 1‑2 years, while PhD awards can extend up to 4 years, matching the standard length of the respective programs.
