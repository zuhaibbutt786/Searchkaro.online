UNICAF MS & PhD Scholarships for Online UK Degrees

UNICAF offers fully funded scholarships for Pakistani and Indian students to pursue MS or PhD online degrees from leading UK universities. These scholarships cover tuition and provide additional support such as mentorship and career services. Applicants can study from anywhere, gaining an internationally recognised qualification without relocating. The program aims to make high‑quality education accessible and affordable for South Asian scholars.

Eligibility

  • Citizen of Pakistan or India with a valid passport
  • Hold a bachelor's degree (2:1 or equivalent) for MS or a master's degree for PhD
  • Proficient in English (IELTS 6.0 or equivalent) and meet the university's entry requirements

Benefits

  • Full or partial tuition coverage for accredited UK online programmes
  • Access to UNICAF mentorship, career services and a global alumni network

How to apply

  1. Create a free UNICAF account and complete your personal and academic profile
  2. Select the desired UK university programme and submit the scholarship application
  3. Upload required documents (transcripts, English test, passport) and wait for the selection decision

FAQs

Can I apply for both MS and PhD scholarships at the same time?

Yes, you may submit separate applications for each level, but you can only accept one scholarship offer.

Do I need to travel to the UK for any part of the programme?

No. All UNICAF‑partner programmes are fully online, allowing you to study from Pakistan, India, or any location with internet access.
