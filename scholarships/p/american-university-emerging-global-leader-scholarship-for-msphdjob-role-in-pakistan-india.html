American University Emerging Global Leader Scholarship for MS/PhD/Job Role in Pakistan & India

American University offers the Emerging Global Leader Scholarship for international students, including Pakistan and India, pursuing MS, PhD, or job roles. This scholarship provides financial assistance for undergraduate studies at American University in Washington DC, USA. Apply before January 15, 2027, for courses starting Fall 2027.

Eligibility

  • Eligible for MS, PhD, or job role in Pakistan & India
  • Diverse and underrepresented backgrounds
  • International students
  • Students pursuing undergraduate studies at American University in Washington DC, USA

Benefits

  • Financial assistance for undergraduate studies
  • Fully funded or partial scholarships
  • Opportunity to study at American University in Washington DC, USA

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Research the scholarship and eligibility criteria

  3. Step 3: Submit the scholarship application before January 15, 2027, for courses starting Fall 2027.
