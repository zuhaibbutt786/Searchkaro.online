EPFL Excellence Fellowships for MS Students in Pakistan and India

EPFL Excellence Fellowships offer opportunities for Pakistani and Indian MS students to pursue their studies at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne in Switzerland. Applications for the 2026-27 fellowships are expected to open soon, with deadlines in December 2025 or April 2026 for courses starting in September 2026. These fellowships cover tuition fees and provide a monthly stipend.

Eligibility

  • Eligible for MS studies at EPFL
  • Currently studying in Pakistan or India
  • Hold a valid visa to study in Switzerland

Benefits

  • Tuition fee coverage
  • Monthly stipend provided
  • Opportunity to study at EPFL in Switzerland

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Research and apply for a valid visa to study in Switzerland
  2. Step 2: Research and apply for admission to MS programs at EPFL
  3. Step 3: Monitor EPFL's website for fellowship application details and submit your application before the deadline

EPFL Excellence Fellowships for MS students in Pakistan and India are expected to open for the 2026-27 academic year, with deadlines in December 2025 or April 2026 for courses starting in September 2026.
