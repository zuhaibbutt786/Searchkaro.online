University of Twente Scholarships: MS & PhD Funding Guide

The University of Twente offers competitive scholarship opportunities for international Master's and PhD candidates. These awards aim to attract talented students from diverse academic backgrounds to the Netherlands. The program supports higher education goals by reducing financial barriers for global applicants.

Eligibility

  • Admission to a full-time Master's or PhD program at the University of Twente.
  • Strong academic record with relevant prior degree qualifications.
  • Non-Dutch nationality and valid passport for international students.

Benefits

  • Partial or full coverage of tuition fees for the first year.
  • Enhanced profile for future academic and career opportunities in Europe.

How to apply

  1. Secure admission to a qualifying Master's or PhD program at the University of Twente.
  2. Prepare required documents including transcripts, CV, and motivation letter.
  3. Submit the scholarship application via the official UT portal before the deadline.

FAQs

Are scholarships available for Pakistani and Indian students?

Yes, the University of Twente Scholarships are open to international students, including those from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic criteria.

Do I need to apply for the scholarship separately?

Yes, you must first secure admission to the program and then submit a separate application for the scholarship through the university's official website.
