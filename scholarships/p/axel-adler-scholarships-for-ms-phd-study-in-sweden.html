Axel Adler Scholarships for MS & PhD – Study in Sweden

The Axel Adler Scholarships, offered by the University of Gothenburg, support outstanding international students pursuing Master's (MS) or Doctoral (PhD) degrees. Recipients receive tuition coverage and a living stipend, enabling a focus on research and academic excellence. The program welcomes applicants from Pakistan, India and all over the globe. It is an ideal pathway for ambitious scholars aiming to study in Sweden's vibrant research environment.

Eligibility

  • Citizenship of Pakistan, India, or any other country (global eligibility).
  • Accepted admission offer for a full‑time MS or PhD program at the University of Gothenburg.
  • Strong academic record (minimum GPA equivalent to 3.0/4.0) and relevant research experience.
  • Proficiency in English (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 80+).

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver for the duration of the MS or PhD program.
  • Monthly living stipend covering accommodation, food, and travel expenses.

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Secure admission to an eligible MS or PhD program at the University of Gothenburg.
  2. Step 2: Complete the online Axel Adler Scholarship application form and upload required documents (transcripts, CV, research proposal, recommendation letters).
  3. Step 3: Submit the application before the university‑specified deadline and await the selection committee’s decision.

FAQs

Can I apply for the Axel Adler Scholarship if I am already enrolled in a Swedish university?

No. The scholarship is only available to newly admitted international students who have received an offer to start an MS or PhD program at the University of Gothenburg.

Is there a limit on the number of scholarships awarded to students from Pakistan or India?

The scholarship does not set a country‑specific quota; selection is based on academic merit and research potential, regardless of nationality.

Will the scholarship cover health insurance and travel costs?

The award includes tuition and a monthly living stipend. Health insurance and initial travel expenses must be arranged by the student, though the stipend can be used for these costs.

Can I extend the scholarship if my research takes longer than the planned study period?

Extensions are considered on a case‑by‑case basis and require approval from the university’s graduate school, supported by progress reports.
