Adlerbert Study Scholarships for MS at Chalmers University, Sweden

The Adlerbert Study Scholarships enable international students to pursue a Master’s degree at Chalmers University of Technology in Sweden. Open to applicants from 143 countries, the scholarship covers tuition, living expenses, and travel costs. The deadline for the 2027/2028 cycle is January 15, 2027, with courses beginning in August 2026.

Eligibility

  • International students from 143 countries, including Pakistan and India
  • Accepted into a Master’s program at Chalmers University of Technology
  • Meeting the academic and language proficiency requirements

Benefits

  • Full tuition coverage
  • Living stipend and travel allowance
  • Access to Chalmers’ research facilities and network

How to apply

  1. Gather academic transcripts, language certificates, and recommendation letters
  2. Complete the online application portal and upload required documents
  3. Submit before the January 15, 2027 deadline and await admission decision

FAQs

Who is eligible for the Adlerbert Study Scholarships?

Students from any of the 143 participating countries, including Pakistan and India, who are accepted into a Master’s program at Chalmers University of Technology.

What is the application deadline for the 2027/2028 cycle?

The deadline is January 15, 2027. Applications received after this date will not be considered.
