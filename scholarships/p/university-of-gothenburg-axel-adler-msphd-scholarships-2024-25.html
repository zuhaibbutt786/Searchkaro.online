University of Gothenburg Axel Adler MS/PhD Scholarships 2024-25

The University of Gothenburg offers the Axel Adler Scholarship to support outstanding international students pursuing Master's or PhD degrees. This prestigious award is designed to attract top talent from around the world, including candidates from Pakistan and India. It aims to enhance the academic experience by providing financial support for tuition fees. Students must be admitted to a specific program to be considered for this scholarship.

Eligibility

  • Must hold a recognized bachelor's degree for MS or master's degree for PhD admission.
  • Must be an international student, including applicants from Pakistan and India.
  • Must have been admitted to a full-time Master's or PhD program at the University of Gothenburg.

Benefits

  • Covers 100% of tuition fees for the duration of the study program.
  • Provides financial support to focus on academic research and coursework.

How to apply

  1. Secure admission to a full-time Master's or PhD program at the University of Gothenburg.
  2. Submit a separate scholarship application via the university's online portal.
  3. Upload required documents, including transcripts, CV, and a motivation letter.

FAQs

Is the Axel Adler Scholarship open to students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, the scholarship is available to international students worldwide, including those from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic criteria.

Do I need to apply for the scholarship separately?

Yes, you must first be admitted to a program and then submit a separate application for the Axel Adler Scholarship.
