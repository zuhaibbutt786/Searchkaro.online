Oxford Pershing Square Graduate Scholarships for MS/PhD

The Oxford Pershing Square Graduate Scholarships support outstanding candidates pursuing Master's or Doctoral degrees at the University of Oxford. This prestigious award is open to students from all over the world, including Pakistan and India. It aims to foster a diverse academic community by funding high-achieving graduate students.

Eligibility

  • Applicants must be enrolled in a full-time Master's or Doctoral program at the University of Oxford.
  • Open to students of any nationality, including those from Pakistan and India.
  • Candidates must demonstrate exceptional academic merit and potential for leadership.

Benefits

  • Covers full tuition fees for the duration of the study.
  • Provides a maintenance stipend to cover living expenses in Oxford.

How to apply

  1. Submit your application for the Oxford graduate program through the official portal.
  2. Indicate your interest in the Pershing Square Scholarship during the application process.
  3. Ensure all required supporting documents and references are uploaded before the deadline.

FAQs

Is the Oxford Pershing Square Scholarship available for Pakistani and Indian students?

Yes, the scholarship is open to international students from all countries, including Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic criteria.

Does this scholarship cover living costs?

Yes, the award typically includes a maintenance stipend to help cover living expenses while studying in the UK.
