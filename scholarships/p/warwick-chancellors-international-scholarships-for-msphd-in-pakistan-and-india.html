Warwick Chancellor's International Scholarships for MS/PhD in Pakistan and India

Explore Warwick Chancellor's International Scholarships for Pakistani and Indian students pursuing MS or PhD degrees worldwide. These scholarships offer significant benefits and a competitive application process. Learn more and apply today.

Eligibility

  • Nationals of Pakistan or India
  • Applying for MS or PhD programs at Warwick University
  • Meet the academic and English language requirements

Benefits

  • Full tuition fee waiver
  • Monthly stipend for living expenses
  • Access to world-class facilities and resources at Warwick University

How to apply

  1. Visit the Warwick University website
  2. Find and apply for the Warwick Chancellor's International Scholarships program
  3. Submit your application along with required documents and essays by the specified deadline
