Gates Cambridge Scholarships for MS & PhD – Pakistan & India Applicants

The Gates Cambridge Scholarship is a world‑renowned award that funds graduate study at the University of Cambridge. It covers full tuition, a generous living stipend, and travel costs for selected MS and PhD candidates. Applicants from Pakistan and India are strongly encouraged to apply, as the program seeks diverse global talent. The scholarship aims to develop future leaders who will make a positive impact in their home countries and beyond.

Eligibility

  • Citizenship of Pakistan, India, or any other non‑UK country.
  • Accepted offer for a full‑time MS or PhD programme at the University of Cambridge.
  • Outstanding academic record, leadership potential, and a commitment to improving the lives of others.

Benefits

  • Full tuition fees, a monthly stipend for living expenses, and travel costs to and from Cambridge.
  • Access to a global network of Gates Cambridge scholars, mentors, and alumni.

How to apply

  1. Secure an unconditional offer of admission from Cambridge and obtain a reference from your academic supervisor.
  2. Complete the online Gates Cambridge application form and upload required documents (transcripts, personal statement, etc.).
  3. Submit the application before the deadline and await the selection panel’s decision.

FAQs

Can I apply for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship if I am studying a non‑science subject?

Yes. The scholarship is open to all disciplines offered at Cambridge, including arts, humanities, social sciences, engineering, and natural sciences.

Do I need to be a resident of Pakistan or India at the time of application?

Residency is not required; you only need to hold citizenship of Pakistan, India, or any other non‑UK country.
