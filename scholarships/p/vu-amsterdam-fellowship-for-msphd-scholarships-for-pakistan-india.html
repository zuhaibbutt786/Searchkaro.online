VU Amsterdam Fellowship for MS/PhD: Scholarships for Pakistan & India

The VU Amsterdam Fellowship Programme supports talented international researchers pursuing Master's or PhD degrees in the Netherlands. It aims to attract high-caliber candidates from diverse global backgrounds, including South Asia. This prestigious opportunity helps cover tuition costs and fosters academic excellence at one of Europe's top universities.

Eligibility

  • Applicants must be admitted to a full-time Master's or PhD programme at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.
  • Candidates must hold a strong academic record and demonstrate research potential or professional experience.
  • Applicants must be non-EU/EEA citizens and not have previously studied in the Netherlands.
  • Specific programme requirements may apply, so check individual department guidelines.

Benefits

  • Covers full tuition fees for the duration of the study programme.
  • Provides a monthly stipend to support living expenses in Amsterdam.
  • Enhances CV with prestige associated with a top-ranked European university.

How to apply

  1. First, apply and secure admission to your chosen Master's or PhD programme at VU Amsterdam.
  2. Once admitted, check if your programme is eligible for the VU Fellowship and submit the specific fellowship application.
  3. Prepare required documents, including transcripts, CV, and motivation letter, and upload them via the VU portal before the deadline.

FAQs

Is the VU Fellowship available for Pakistani and Indian students?

Yes, the fellowship is open to international students from all eligible countries, including Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic criteria.

Do I need to apply for the fellowship separately?

Yes, admission to the programme is the first step. You must then submit a separate application for the VU Fellowship if your programme is eligible.

Does the fellowship cover accommodation?

The fellowship typically covers tuition and provides a living stipend, but does not directly pay for housing. You must arrange your own accommodation in Amsterdam.
