AAUW International Fellowships for Women – MS & PhD in USA

The AAUW International Fellowships support women worldwide who wish to undertake graduate study (MS or PhD) in the United States. Applicants from Pakistan and India are eligible to receive funding for tuition, living costs, and travel. These competitive awards recognize academic excellence and a commitment to advancing women in their fields. The fellowship helps you launch a research‑oriented career while studying at top U.S. universities.

Eligibility

  • Female applicants who are citizens of Pakistan, India, or any non‑U.S. country.
  • Enrolled or planning to enroll in a full‑time MS or PhD program at an accredited U.S. institution.
  • Demonstrated academic merit, a clear research proposal, and proficiency in English.

Benefits

  • Financial support that can cover tuition, fees, living expenses, and travel costs.
  • Access to a global network of AAUW scholars and professional development resources.

How to apply

  1. Verify eligibility and identify a U.S. graduate program that aligns with your research goals.
  2. Gather required documents: academic transcripts, letters of recommendation, a research statement, and proof of citizenship.
  3. Submit the online application through the AAUW portal before the deadline and monitor your email for confirmation.

FAQs

Can I apply if I am currently studying in Pakistan or India?

Yes. You may apply while enrolled in a university in Pakistan or India, provided you plan to start your MS or PhD program in the USA.

Will the fellowship cover the full cost of my graduate program?

The award is flexible and can be used for tuition, fees, living expenses, and travel, but the exact amount varies each year and may not cover all costs.
