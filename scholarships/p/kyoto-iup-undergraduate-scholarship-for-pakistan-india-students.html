Kyoto iUP: Undergraduate Scholarship for Pakistan & India Students

The Kyoto iUP is a fully English-taught undergraduate program at Kyoto University, one of Japan's most prestigious institutions. It offers a unique opportunity for international students to study in a global environment without needing prior Japanese language proficiency. This program is highly competitive and attracts top talent from around the world, including strong applicants from South Asia. Graduates receive a recognized Bachelor's degree from Kyoto University.

Eligibility

  • High school graduates or those expected to graduate by the start of the academic year.
  • Demonstrated proficiency in English (e.g., TOEFL, IELTS, or equivalent).
  • Strong academic record with competitive grades in relevant subjects.

Benefits

  • Access to world-class education at a top-ranked Japanese university.
  • Fully English-medium instruction, removing the language barrier for non-Japanese speakers.

How to apply

  1. Review the official Kyoto University iUP admission guidelines and prepare required documents.
  2. Submit your online application via the university's designated portal before the deadline.
  3. Prepare for the entrance examination and interview if shortlisted.

FAQs

Do I need to know Japanese to apply for Kyoto iUP?

No, the Kyoto iUP is taught entirely in English. While learning Japanese is encouraged for daily life, it is not a prerequisite for admission.

Is the Kyoto iUP open to students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, the program is open to international students worldwide, including those from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic and language requirements.
