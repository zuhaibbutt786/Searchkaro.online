Emile Boutmy Scholarships for Non-EU MS/PhD at Sciences Po

The Emile Boutmy Scholarship supports exceptional non-EU students admitted to master's or doctoral programs at Sciences Po. It is designed to foster international diversity and academic excellence. This funding is crucial for students from countries like Pakistan and India seeking top-tier European education. Applicants must demonstrate high academic merit and a strong commitment to their field.

Eligibility

  • Must be a non-EU citizen admitted to a Sciences Po MS or PhD program
  • Demonstrate exceptional academic record and potential
  • Show commitment to Sciences Po's values of social responsibility

Benefits

  • Covers tuition fees for the duration of the program
  • Provides a monthly living allowance to support studies

How to apply

  1. Secure admission to a Sciences Po MS or PhD program
  2. Submit the scholarship application via the Sciences Po portal
  3. Provide academic transcripts and a motivation letter

FAQs

Can students from Pakistan and India apply?

Yes, the scholarship is open to all non-EU students, including those from Pakistan and India.

Does it cover living expenses?

Yes, it typically includes a monthly allowance alongside tuition coverage.
