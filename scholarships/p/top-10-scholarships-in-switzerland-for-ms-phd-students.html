Top 10 Scholarships in Switzerland for MS & PhD Students

Switzerland offers world‑class education and generous funding for talented international scholars. This guide highlights the ten most reputable scholarships for MS and PhD programmes, suitable for students from Pakistan, India and other countries. Each award covers tuition, living costs or research expenses, and provides access to cutting‑edge facilities. Follow the outlined steps to boost your chances of securing a Swiss scholarship and advancing your academic career.

Eligibility

  • Citizens of Pakistan, India or any other country seeking a full‑time MS or PhD in Switzerland
  • Bachelor’s degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0 (or equivalent) from a recognized university
  • Proficiency in English (TOEFL/IELTS) and, where required, German or French

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver and a living allowance (where the scholarship includes it)
  • Access to leading research labs, industry partners, and an international academic network

How to apply

  1. Research eligible Swiss universities and identify the scholarship programmes that match your field of study
  2. Prepare required documents: academic transcripts, CV, research proposal, recommendation letters, and language test scores
  3. Submit the online application before the announced deadline and monitor your email for any follow‑up requests

FAQs

Can students from Pakistan and India apply for these Swiss scholarships?

Yes, the scholarships are open to all international applicants, including those from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic and language criteria.

What language tests are required for Swiss scholarships?

Most programmes require proof of English proficiency (TOEFL or IELTS). Some programs taught in German or French may also ask for DELE or TestDaF scores.

Do I need to have a job offer in Switzerland to receive a scholarship?

No. These scholarships are purely academic awards and do not require a prior job offer. However, some may include internship opportunities with partner companies.
