Gates Cambridge Scholarships for MS & PhD International Students

The Gates Cambridge Scholarship supports exceptional students from outside the UK in pursuing full-time postgraduate study at the University of Cambridge. It covers full tuition fees and provides a stipend for living expenses, making it one of the most prestigious awards for international researchers. Candidates from Pakistan and India are encouraged to apply if they demonstrate leadership potential and academic excellence. This award is designed to foster a diverse community of future leaders who will make a positive impact on their home countries and the world.

Eligibility

  • Must be a citizen of a country outside the UK.
  • Applying for a full-time postgraduate course (Masters or PhD) at Cambridge.
  • Demonstrated academic excellence and potential for leadership.
  • Commitment to making a positive impact on your home country or the world.

Benefits

  • Covers full tuition fees for the duration of the course.
  • Provides a stipend for living expenses and travel costs.
  • Access to exclusive Gates Cambridge community and networking events.

How to apply

  1. First, apply for your chosen postgraduate course at the University of Cambridge.
  2. Complete the separate Gates Cambridge Scholarship application form.
  3. Submit required documents including transcripts, references, and a personal statement.

FAQs

Can students from Pakistan and India apply for this scholarship?

Yes, the Gates Cambridge Scholarship is open to all international students from outside the UK, including those from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the academic and leadership criteria.

Does the scholarship cover PhD research costs?

The scholarship covers tuition fees and provides a stipend for living expenses. Specific research costs may need to be addressed in your course application, but the core financial support is included.
