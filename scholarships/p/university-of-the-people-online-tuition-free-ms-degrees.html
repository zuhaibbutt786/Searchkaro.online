University of the People Online Tuition Free MS Degrees

University of the People offers fully online, tuition-free master's and doctoral programs designed for working professionals. Students in Pakistan and India can access high-quality education without paying tuition fees, only covering small assessment costs. The university is accredited, making its degrees recognized globally for career advancement.

Eligibility

  • Hold a bachelor's degree for MS programs or a master's degree for PhD programs
  • Demonstrate English proficiency through TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo English Test
  • Submit official transcripts and a statement of purpose

Benefits

  • No tuition fees, only minimal assessment costs per course
  • 100% online format allows you to study while working in Pakistan or India

How to apply

  1. Create an account on the University of the People website and select your desired program
  2. Submit your application form, academic transcripts, and English proficiency scores
  3. Wait for the admissions decision and complete enrollment by paying the assessment fee

FAQs

Is University of the People accredited?

Yes, the university is accredited by the Distance Education Accrediting Commission (DEAC) and recognized by the US Department of Education.

Can students from Pakistan and India apply?

Yes, University of the People accepts applications from students worldwide, including those residing in Pakistan and India.
