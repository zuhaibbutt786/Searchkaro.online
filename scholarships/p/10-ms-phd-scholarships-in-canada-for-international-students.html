10+ MS & PhD Scholarships in Canada for International Students

Canada offers robust funding opportunities for international graduate researchers. This guide highlights over ten prestigious scholarships for MS and PhD candidates. These awards cover tuition, living expenses, and research costs. Ideal for students from Pakistan and India seeking global education.

Eligibility

  • Non-Canadian citizens with a valid passport
  • Holding a relevant undergraduate or master's degree
  • Admitted or applying to a Canadian university program

Benefits

  • Covers tuition fees and mandatory health insurance
  • Provides monthly living stipend and research allowances

How to apply

  1. Shortlist universities and specific scholarship programs
  2. Prepare transcripts, LORs, and a strong research proposal
  3. Submit applications via university portals or official sites

FAQs

Are these scholarships open to students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, most listed scholarships are open to all international students, including those from South Asia.

Do I need to apply for the scholarship separately?

It depends on the award. Some are automatic upon admission, while others require a separate application.
