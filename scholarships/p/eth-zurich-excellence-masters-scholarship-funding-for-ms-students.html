ETH Zurich Excellence Masters Scholarship – Funding for MS Students

The ETH Zurich Excellence Masters Scholarship offers full financial support for outstanding students pursuing a Master's degree at ETH Zurich. It is open to applicants from Pakistan, India and all other countries, provided they meet the academic standards. Recipients receive tuition coverage, a living stipend and travel assistance. The scholarship aims to attract global talent to Switzerland's leading technical university.

Eligibility

  • Hold a recognized bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.0 (or equivalent) in a relevant field.
  • Be a citizen of Pakistan, India or any other country and demonstrate strong academic and research potential.
  • Provide proof of English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS) or German proficiency if the program requires it.

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver, mandatory fees and a monthly living stipend for the entire Master's program.
  • Travel allowance, health insurance coverage, and access to ETH Zurich's research facilities and networks.

How to apply

  1. Create an ETH Zurich online applicant account and submit your Master's program application.
  2. Upload the scholarship application form, academic transcripts, CV, recommendation letters, and language test scores.
  3. Submit the complete package before the deadline and monitor your email for interview or additional document requests.

FAQs

Can Indian or Pakistani students apply for this scholarship?

Yes, the ETH Zurich Excellence Masters Scholarship is open to candidates from Pakistan, India and all other nations who meet the eligibility criteria.

What costs does the scholarship cover?

The award covers tuition, mandatory university fees, a monthly living stipend, travel expenses to Switzerland, and health insurance for the duration of the Master's program.

Is German language proficiency required?

Most English‑taught Master's programs at ETH Zurich do not require German, but if your chosen program is taught in German you must provide proof of German proficiency.

When will I be notified about the decision?

Successful applicants are typically informed within 8‑12 weeks after the scholarship deadline via the ETH Zurich admissions portal.
