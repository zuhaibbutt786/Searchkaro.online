ARES MS/PhD Scholarships in Belgium for Pakistan & India

The ARES program offers fully funded MS and PhD scholarships for students from Pakistan, India and other developing countries to study in Belgium. Selected scholars receive tuition waivers, a monthly living stipend, travel support and health insurance. The scholarships aim to foster research collaboration and build expertise in priority fields. Applications are managed online through the ARES portal.

Eligibility

  • Citizen of a developing country (e.g., Pakistan, India, or other eligible nations).
  • Bachelor’s degree with at least 2:1 (or equivalent) for MS; Master’s degree for PhD applicants.
  • English language proficiency (minimum IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 80).

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver plus a monthly living allowance for the entire study period.
  • Travel grant, health insurance, and access to Belgium’s research facilities.

How to apply

  1. Register on the ARES portal and create an applicant profile.
  2. Upload academic transcripts, CV, research proposal (PhD) or statement of purpose (MS), and language test scores.
  3. Submit the completed application before the deadline and monitor email for interview invitations.

FAQs

Who is eligible to apply for the ARES scholarships?

Students from Pakistan, India and other developing countries who meet the academic and language requirements can apply.

What expenses does the ARES scholarship cover?

The scholarship covers tuition, a monthly stipend, travel costs, health insurance, and research-related expenses.
