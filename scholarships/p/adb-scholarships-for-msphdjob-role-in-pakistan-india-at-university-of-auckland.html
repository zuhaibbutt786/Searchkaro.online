ADB Scholarships for MS/PhD/Job Role in Pakistan & India at University of Auckland

Explore ADB Scholarships for Pakistani and Indian citizens pursuing MS, PhD, or job role studies at the University of Auckland in New Zealand. The scholarships cover tuition, living costs, insurance, and travel. Apply at least six months prior to course commencement.

Eligibility

  • Nationals of ADB borrowing member countries
  • Currently residing in Pakistan or India
  • Planning to pursue MS, PhD, or job role studies at the University of Auckland

Benefits

  • Full tuition coverage
  • Living costs assistance
  • Insurance and travel benefits
  • Opportunity to study at a world-renowned institution

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Research and confirm program eligibility
  2. Step 2: Check ADB Scholarship eligibility criteria
  3. Step 3: Prepare application documents and submit through ADB's online portal
