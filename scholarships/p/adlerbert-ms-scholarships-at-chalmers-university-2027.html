Adlerbert MS Scholarships at Chalmers University 2027

The Adlerbert Study Scholarships support international Master's students at Chalmers University of Technology in Sweden. These awards are available to applicants from 143 eligible countries, including Pakistan and India. The program offers funding for any Master's degree field offered at the university. Applications for the 2027/2028 intake are open with a deadline of 15 January 2027.

Eligibility

  • Applicants must be from one of the 143 eligible countries, including Pakistan and India.
  • Must be applying for a full-time Master's degree program at Chalmers University of Technology.
  • Must meet the specific admission requirements for the chosen MS course.

Benefits

  • Financial support towards tuition fees for the Master's program.
  • Opportunity to study at a top-ranked Swedish technical university.

How to apply

  1. Select a Master's program at Chalmers University of Technology.
  2. Submit your application for admission through the university's official portal.
  3. Ensure your application is submitted before the 15 January 2027 deadline.

FAQs

Is the Adlerbert Scholarship available for students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, the scholarship is open to international students from 143 countries, which includes both Pakistan and India.

What is the deadline for the 2027 intake?

The application deadline is 15 January 2027, with courses starting in August 2026.

Does the scholarship cover all fields of study?

Yes, it is available for any Master's degree field offered at Chalmers University of Technology.
