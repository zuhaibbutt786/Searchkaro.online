UBC International Scholars Program: MS/PhD Scholarships

The University of British Columbia (UBC) International Scholars Program offers competitive funding to high-achieving international students pursuing Master's or Doctoral degrees. This initiative aims to attract top talent from around the world to Canada's top-ranked research university. It provides significant financial support to help cover tuition and living expenses.

Eligibility

  • Hold a strong academic record with a high GPA from a recognized institution.
  • Be admitted to or applying for a full-time Master's or PhD program at UBC.
  • Demonstrate exceptional research potential and leadership qualities.

Benefits

  • Substantial tuition fee waivers and annual stipends for the duration of the program.
  • Access to world-class research facilities and a diverse global academic community.

How to apply

  1. Submit a complete application for admission to a UBC graduate program.
  2. Ensure your application highlights your academic excellence and research proposal.
  3. Wait for the admissions decision, as scholarship consideration is often automatic for qualified applicants.

FAQs

Do I need a separate application for the UBC International Scholars Program?

In many cases, admission to a competitive graduate program at UBC automatically qualifies you for consideration for this scholarship, but you must meet the high academic standards.

Is this scholarship available for students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, the program is open to international students worldwide, including those from Pakistan and India, provided they meet the strict academic and research criteria.
