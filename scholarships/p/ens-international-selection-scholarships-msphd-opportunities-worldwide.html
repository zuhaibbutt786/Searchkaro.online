ENS International Selection Scholarships – MS/PhD Opportunities Worldwide

ENS International Selection Scholarships offer international students the chance to pursue MS or PhD studies in top research institutions across the globe. The program covers tuition, provides a monthly stipend, and connects scholars with leading experts and cutting‑edge facilities. Applicants from Pakistan, India, and other countries can apply to advance their academic and research careers.

Eligibility

  • International students currently enrolled in an MS or PhD program in science, engineering, or technology.
  • Strong academic record with a minimum GPA requirement (typically 3.0/4.0 or equivalent).
  • Demonstrated research potential through a well‑structured proposal and relevant experience.

Benefits

  • Full tuition coverage and a competitive monthly stipend for living expenses.
  • Access to world‑class laboratories, equipment, and mentorship from leading scholars.

How to apply

  1. Prepare application materials: CV, transcripts, research proposal, language proficiency test scores, and recommendation letters.
  2. Submit the online application through the ENS scholarship portal before the specified deadline.
  3. Await notification for interview or selection; successful candidates receive scholarship award and enrollment support.

FAQs

Who is eligible for the ENS International Selection Scholarships?

International students pursuing an MS or PhD in science, engineering, or technology, with a strong academic record and research proposal.

What documents are required for the application?

Applicants must submit a CV, official transcripts, a detailed research proposal, proof of English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS or equivalent), and two recommendation letters.
