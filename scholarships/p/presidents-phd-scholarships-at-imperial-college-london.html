President’s PhD Scholarships at Imperial College London

2027-28 President's PhD Scholarships are open to high-achieving students of any nationality. The scholarships are towards a PhD in any faculty at Imperial College London in the UK and cover full tuition fees, a stipend and research costs. Application rounds close on 2 November 2026, 11 January 2027 and 1 March 2027. PhDs start between August and November 2027.
