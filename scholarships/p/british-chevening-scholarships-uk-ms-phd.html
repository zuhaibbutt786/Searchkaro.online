British Chevening Scholarships UK MS PhD

The British Chevening Scholarship provides fully funded opportunities for outstanding students from Pakistan, India and around the world to study a Master's or PhD in the United Kingdom. Recipients receive tuition, a living‑cost allowance and access to a global network of leaders. The program values academic excellence, leadership potential and a commitment to return and contribute to your home country. Apply now to boost your academic and professional future.

Eligibility

  • Citizen of Pakistan, India, or another Chevening‑eligible country
  • Hold a recognized bachelor's degree (or be in the final year) with a strong academic record
  • At least two years of full‑time work experience or equivalent leadership experience

Benefits

  • Full tuition fees for your chosen Master's or PhD programme
  • Living‑cost allowance and travel support to the UK

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Verify eligibility and select a Chevening‑approved university programme
  2. Step 2: Prepare your application, including personal statements, references and required documents
  3. Step 3: Submit the online application and, if shortlisted, attend the interview

FAQs

Where can I find the application deadline for the Chevening Scholarship?

The exact deadline varies each year; please consult the official Chevening website for the current application timeline.

Can I apply for a Chevening Scholarship if I already hold a Master's degree?

Yes, you may apply for a Chevening Scholarship to pursue a PhD, provided you meet all other eligibility criteria.
