Top 10+ International MS Scholarships for Pakistan & India Students

This guide highlights over ten prestigious international scholarships for Master's degrees offered by global organizations like the World Bank, ADB, and UNESCO. It covers major programs including Erasmus Mundus, Commonwealth, and Rotary fellowships. These opportunities provide full funding for students from Pakistan, India, and other developing nations. Each entry includes eligibility criteria and application strategies.

Eligibility

  • Holders of a relevant undergraduate degree with strong academic records
  • Citizens of developing countries, with specific focus on Pakistan and India
  • Proficiency in English (IELTS/TOEFL) as required by specific programs

Benefits

  • Full coverage of tuition fees, living stipends, and travel expenses
  • Access to global networks, mentorship, and career development opportunities

How to apply

  1. Research specific deadlines and requirements for each global organization
  2. Prepare a strong Statement of Purpose tailored to the scholarship's mission
  3. Submit applications directly through the official portals of the institutions

FAQs

Are these scholarships open to students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, most of these global organizations and foundations specifically target students from developing nations, including Pakistan and India.

Do I need to apply to the university separately?

It depends on the program. Some, like Erasmus Mundus, require a joint application, while others like Commonwealth may require a university offer first.

Is the funding full or partial?

Most listed programs offer full funding, covering tuition, living costs, and airfare, but you should verify specific stipend details on the official site.
