EPFL Master Excellence Fellowships 2027: Full Guide for Students

The EPFL Master Excellence Fellowship supports outstanding students from any nationality pursuing a Master's degree at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne. This prestigious award provides significant financial aid to help cover living costs during your studies in Switzerland. It is an ideal opportunity for high-achieving candidates from Pakistan, India, and other regions to study at a top global university.

Eligibility

  • Must be an outstanding student of any nationality applying for a Master's degree at EPFL.
  • Your Master's application must be validated by 15 December 2026 for the September 2027 intake.
  • Applicants should demonstrate academic excellence and potential for research or professional impact.

Benefits

  • Financial support of CHF 10,000 per semester to assist with living expenses.
  • Access to world-class education and research facilities at a leading Swiss university.

How to apply

  1. Submit your Master's degree application to EPFL before the validation deadline of 15 December 2026.
  2. Ensure your academic records and supporting documents clearly highlight your excellence and achievements.
  3. Monitor your EPFL application portal for any specific fellowship-related forms or notifications.

FAQs

Are students from Pakistan and India eligible for this fellowship?

Yes, the EPFL Master Excellence Fellowships are open to outstanding students of any nationality, including those from Pakistan and India.

What is the financial value of the EPFL Master Excellence Fellowship?

The fellowship provides CHF 10,000 per semester to help cover living costs during your Master's studies.

When is the deadline to apply for the 2027 intake?

Your Master's application must be validated by 15 December 2026 for courses starting in September 2027.
