University of British Columbia's International Scholars Program for MS/PhD or Future Professionals

Experience world-class education at the University of British Columbia through the International Scholars Program for MS/PhD students or future professionals. This program offers unique opportunities and competitive benefits for exceptional candidates from Pakistan and India.

Eligibility

  • Applicants must hold a bachelor's degree or its equivalent
  • Pakistani and Indian citizens are highly encouraged to apply
  • Preference may be given to candidates with strong research potential and academic achievements

Benefits

  • Competitive stipend and tuition waivers
  • Access to top-tier research facilities and resources
  • Opportunities for international exposure and networking

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Check program requirements and eligibility criteria on the University of British Columbia website
  2. Step 2: Prepare your application materials including transcripts, letters of recommendation, research proposal, and CV
  3. Step 3: Submit your application through the UBC Online Application System

FAQs

What are the program requirements for the International Scholars Program?

Visit the University of British Columbia website for detailed program requirements and eligibility criteria.

Can I apply if I am not a Pakistani or Indian citizen?

While preference is given to Pakistani and Indian citizens, qualified applicants from other countries may also be considered.

Are there any language requirements for the program?

Applicants are expected to have a strong command of English as the university language.
