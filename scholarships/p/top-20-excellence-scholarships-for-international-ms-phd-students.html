Top 20 Excellence Scholarships for International MS & PhD Students

This guide lists the 20 most prestigious excellence scholarships available for exceptional MS and PhD students worldwide. It highlights eligibility, benefits, and application steps, helping Pakistani and Indian scholars secure funding for their graduate studies abroad. Each scholarship offers substantial financial support, covering tuition and living expenses, and is open to a diverse range of disciplines.

Eligibility

  • Citizen of Pakistan, India, or any other country with a valid passport
  • Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.5/4.0 (or equivalent) in the most recent degree
  • Admission to a full‑time MS or PhD program at a recognized university
  • Proof of English proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL, or equivalent) meeting scholarship standards

Benefits

  • Full tuition coverage for the entire duration of the MS or PhD program
  • Annual living allowance, health insurance, and travel grant (where applicable)

How to apply

  1. Create an account on the scholarship’s official portal and fill in personal details
  2. Upload required documents: academic transcripts, CV, statement of purpose, and recommendation letters
  3. Submit the completed application before the announced deadline and monitor email for confirmation

FAQs

Can I apply for more than one of these scholarships at the same time?

Yes, you may submit separate applications for each scholarship as long as you meet the individual eligibility criteria.

Will the scholarship cover my research expenses and conference travel?

Most excellence scholarships include a research allowance or travel grant, but the exact amount varies; check each scholarship’s guidelines for details.
