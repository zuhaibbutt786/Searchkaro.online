Oxford Scholarships for Developing Country MS/PhD Students in Pakistan and India

Explore Oxford Scholarships, offering MS/PhD opportunities for Pakistani and Indian students. These scholarships support students from developing countries around the world. Discover benefits, eligibility criteria, and application steps.

Eligibility

  • You are a Pakistani or Indian national.
  • You are pursuing or have recently completed your undergraduate studies in Pakistan or India.
  • You are a citizen of a developing country

Benefits

  • Funding for MS/PhD studies at Oxford University.
  • Expenses covered, including tuition fees, living costs, and travel.
  • Opportunities for research collaboration and global networking

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Research available scholarships and apply through the relevant Oxford University portal.
  2. Step 2: Prepare your application materials, including academic transcripts, research proposal, and letters of recommendation.
  3. Step 3: Submit your application before the specified deadline.
  4. Note: Contact the scholarship provider or Oxford University for further assistance

FAQs

Are Pakistani and Indian students eligible for these scholarships?

Yes, Pakistani and Indian students can apply for these scholarships as long as they are pursuing or have recently completed undergraduate studies in Pakistan or India and are citizens of a developing country.

What is the scholarship application process like?

The scholarship application process involves researching available scholarships, applying through the relevant Oxford University portal, preparing application materials, and submitting the application before the specified deadline. For further assistance, contact the scholarship provider or Oxford University.
