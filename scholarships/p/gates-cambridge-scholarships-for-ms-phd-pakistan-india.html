Gates Cambridge Scholarships for MS & PhD – Pakistan & India

The Gates Cambridge Scholarship is a prestigious, fully funded award for outstanding students pursuing a master's or PhD at the University of Cambridge. It welcomes applicants from Pakistan, India, and around the world, covering tuition, living expenses, and travel. Recipients join a vibrant international community and gain access to Cambridge's world‑class resources. The scholarship aims to develop future leaders who will make a positive impact globally.

Eligibility

  • Citizenship of Pakistan, India, or any other non‑UK country.
  • Admission to a full‑time MSc, MPhil, or PhD program at the University of Cambridge.
  • Outstanding academic record, leadership potential, and a commitment to improving the lives of others.

Benefits

  • Full tuition fees, a generous maintenance allowance, and travel costs to and from the UK.
  • Access to a global network of Gates Cambridge scholars, mentorship, and career development support.

How to apply

  1. Apply for your chosen Cambridge graduate program and receive an unconditional offer.
  2. Complete the Gates Cambridge online application, including essays, references, and a research proposal.
  3. Submit the application before the deadline and await the selection decision.

FAQs

Can I apply for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship if I am studying for a master's degree?

Yes. The scholarship is open to both MSc/MPhil (master's) and PhD candidates who have an unconditional offer from Cambridge.

What documents are required from Indian and Pakistani applicants?

You will need academic transcripts, proof of English proficiency, two academic references, a personal statement, and a research proposal relevant to your chosen course.

Is there a separate deadline for applicants from Pakistan or India?

No. All international applicants follow the same deadline set by the Gates Cambridge Trust each year.

Will the scholarship cover my family’s living expenses?

The maintenance allowance is intended for the scholar’s personal costs. Dependents are not covered, but you may request additional support during the application.
