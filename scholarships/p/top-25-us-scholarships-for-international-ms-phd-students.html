Top 25 US Scholarships for International MS & PhD Students

Studying in the United States can be financially challenging, but numerous scholarships are available for talented students from Pakistan and India. This guide lists the top 25 scholarships that support Master's and Doctoral studies across various fields. Each scholarship offers generous funding, mentorship, and networking opportunities to help you succeed academically and professionally.

Eligibility

  • Must be a citizen of Pakistan, India, or any other non‑US country
  • Accepted into an accredited US university for an MS or PhD program
  • Demonstrated academic excellence and research potential

Benefits

  • Full or partial tuition coverage, plus living stipend in most cases
  • Access to mentorship, research labs, and professional networks

How to apply

  1. Create a strong academic profile: transcripts, test scores, and research statements
  2. Gather required documents such as recommendation letters and a personal essay
  3. Submit the online application before the scholarship’s deadline and follow up with any additional requests

FAQs

Can I apply for multiple US scholarships at the same time?

Yes, you can submit applications to several scholarships, but ensure each application meets the specific eligibility and documentation requirements.

Do these scholarships cover visa and travel expenses?

Most scholarships focus on tuition and living costs; some may provide a modest travel allowance, but you should budget separately for visa fees and airfare.
