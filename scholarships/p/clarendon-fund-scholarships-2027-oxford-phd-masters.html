Clarendon Fund Scholarships 2027: Oxford PhD & Masters

The Clarendon Fund offers prestigious scholarships for Masters and PhD students at the University of Oxford. Open to candidates from Pakistan, India, and globally, it supports study in any field. Applications for the 2027-2028 intake are currently open, with course commencement in October 2027.

Eligibility

  • Applicants must be from any country, including Pakistan and India.
  • Must be applying for a full-time Masters or PhD degree at Oxford.
  • Must meet the specific academic entry requirements for the chosen course.

Benefits

  • Covers full tuition fees for the duration of the course.
  • Provides a stipend to cover living expenses in the UK.

How to apply

  1. Select your desired Masters or PhD course on the Oxford website.
  2. Submit your application before the course-specific deadline (Dec 2026 or Jan 2027).
  3. Indicate your interest in the Clarendon Fund in your application form.

FAQs

Are Pakistani and Indian students eligible?

Yes, the Clarendon Fund is open to eligible students from all over the world, including Pakistan and India.

What is the application deadline for 2027 entry?

Deadlines vary by course but are typically December 2026 or January 2027 for October 2027 start.
