Padua International Excellence Scholarships at University of Padua (MS/PhD/job role eligible)

Explore the Padua International Excellence Scholarships for Pakistani and Indian students seeking MS, PhD, or job role opportunities at the esteemed University of Padua in Europe. Apply for €8,000 annual scholarships and fee exemptions for Bachelor's or Master's degrees, with application deadlines of 15 Nov 2026 (Call One) and 1 Mar 2027 (Call Two), courses starting in 2027/28.

Eligibility

  • Applicants from Pakistan and India
  • MS, PhD, or job role candidates
  • English-taught degree programmes at the University of Padua

Benefits

  • €8,000 annual scholarship
  • Fee exemption for Bachelor's or Master's degrees
  • Opportunities for MS, PhD, or job role aspirants

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Research and apply for eligible English-taught programmes at the University of Padua
  2. Step 2: Submit completed application forms by respective deadlines (15 Nov 2026 for Call One and 1 Mar 2027 for Call Two)
  3. Step 3: Ensure your application meets all eligibility criteria (MS, PhD, or job role candidates, Pakistani and Indian students) for the Padua International Excellence Scholarships.

FAQs

Are Pakistani and Indian students eligible for the Padua International Excellence Scholarships?

Yes, Pakistani and Indian students are eligible for the Padua International Excellence Scholarships if they are pursuing MS, PhD, or job role opportunities and meet the English-taught degree programmes at the University of Padua eligibility criteria.

What is the scholarship amount for the Padua International Excellence Scholarships?

The Padua International Excellence Scholarships offer €8,000 annually for eligible candidates. Additionally, Pakistani and Indian students can avail fee exemptions for their Bachelor's or Master's degrees.

When are the application deadlines for Call One and Call Two?

The application deadlines for Call One (15th November 2026) and Call Two (1st March 2027) provide valuable opportunities for Pakistani and Indian students to pursue their MS, PhD, or job role aspirations at the University of Padua. Make sure to meet the English-taught degree programmes criteria.
