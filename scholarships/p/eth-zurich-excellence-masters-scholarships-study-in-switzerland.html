ETH Zurich Excellence Masters Scholarships – Study in Switzerland

The ETH Zurich Excellence Masters Scholarships offer outstanding international students a chance to pursue a Master's degree at one of Europe's top universities. The award covers tuition fees and provides a living allowance, making Swiss education more accessible. Applicants from Pakistan, India and other countries are encouraged to apply. This scholarship helps you join a vibrant research community and build a global network.

Eligibility

  • Hold a recognized bachelor's degree with a strong academic record (typically GPA 3.0/4.0 or higher).
  • Be a citizen of Pakistan, India, or any other country (global eligibility).
  • Demonstrate proficiency in English (IELTS/TOEFL) or German, depending on the program language.
  • Apply for a Master's program offered by ETH Zurich and meet its admission criteria.

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver for the entire Master's program.
  • Monthly living allowance to cover accommodation and daily expenses.

How to apply

  1. Choose an eligible Master's program at ETH Zurich and note the application deadline.
  2. Prepare required documents: academic transcripts, language test scores, CV, motivation letter, and recommendation letters.
  3. Submit your program application through the ETH online portal, then apply for the Excellence Scholarship within the same system.

FAQs

Can students from Pakistan and India apply for the ETH Zurich Excellence Masters Scholarship?

Yes, the scholarship is open to applicants of any nationality, including students from Pakistan and India.

What does the scholarship cover?

It covers full tuition fees for the Master's program and provides a monthly living allowance for the duration of the study.
