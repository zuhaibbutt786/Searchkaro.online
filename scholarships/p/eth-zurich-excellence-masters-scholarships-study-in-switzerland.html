ETH Zurich Excellence Masters Scholarships – Study in Switzerland

The ETH Zurich Excellence Masters Scholarships offer top-performing students from Pakistan, India and around the world a chance to pursue a master's degree at one of Europe's leading universities. Recipients receive full tuition coverage, a monthly living stipend, and health insurance for the duration of their studies. The program aims to attract diverse talent to ETH Zurich's cutting‑edge research and education environment. Applications are competitive and based on academic excellence, motivation, and leadership potential.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor’s degree with at least a 3.0 GPA (or equivalent) from a recognized university.
  • Citizenship of Pakistan, India, or any other country with a strong academic record.
  • Proficiency in English (IELTS/TOEFL) – German or French language skills are optional but advantageous.

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver for the entire master’s program.
  • Monthly living allowance, health insurance, and support for study-related expenses.

How to apply

  1. Create an ETH Zurich online applicant account and select your desired master’s program.
  2. Submit the scholarship application together with transcripts, CV, motivation letter, and two recommendation letters.
  3. Wait for the selection committee’s decision; successful candidates receive an official award letter.

FAQs

Who can apply for the ETH Zurich Excellence Masters Scholarship?

The scholarship is open to outstanding students from any country, including Pakistan and India, who have completed a bachelor’s degree and meet the academic criteria.

What does the scholarship cover?

It covers full tuition fees for the master’s program, a monthly stipend for living expenses, and health insurance for the duration of the study.
