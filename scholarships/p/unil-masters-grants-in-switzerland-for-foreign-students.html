UNIL Master's Grants in Switzerland for Foreign Students

The University of Lausanne (UNIL) offers competitive grants to support international students pursuing Master's degrees in Switzerland. These awards are designed to attract top talent from around the world, including Pakistan and India. Successful candidates receive financial assistance that helps cover living costs and tuition, allowing them to focus on their academic research. This is a prestigious opportunity for MS aspirants seeking high-quality European education.

Eligibility

  • Holders of a recognized Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification.
  • Applicants must be enrolled in a full-time Master's program at the University of Lausanne.
  • Demonstrated academic excellence and strong motivation for the chosen field of study.

Benefits

  • Financial support to offset living expenses in Switzerland.
  • Access to world-class research facilities and faculty at UNIL.

How to apply

  1. Apply for admission to a Master's program at the University of Lausanne.
  2. Submit a separate application for the UNIL grant with your academic transcripts and CV.
  3. Provide a detailed motivation letter explaining your research goals and financial need.

FAQs

Is this scholarship open to students from Pakistan and India?

Yes, the UNIL Master's Grants are open to all foreign students, including those from Pakistan, India, and other countries worldwide.

Does the grant cover tuition fees?

The grant primarily supports living costs. Tuition fees at public Swiss universities are generally low, but you should verify the specific program costs directly with UNIL.

When is the application deadline?

Deadlines vary by program and academic year. Please check the official University of Lausanne website for the most current dates.
