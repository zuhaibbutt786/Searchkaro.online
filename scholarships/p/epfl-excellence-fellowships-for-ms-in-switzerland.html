EPFL Excellence Fellowships for MS in Switzerland

EPFL Excellence Fellowships offer MS scholarships for international students seeking to pursue studies at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne in Switzerland. Apply before December 15, 2025 (or April 30, 2026 for courses starting in September 2026) and benefit from competitive funding and world-class education. Read more.

Eligibility

  • Be a Pakistani or Indian national.
  • Hold a bachelor's degree or equivalent.
  • Meet EPFL's English proficiency requirements.
  • Have a strong academic record and research potential

Benefits

  • Competitive funding for tuition fees and living expenses
  • Access to top-tier research facilities and expertise
  • Opportunity to network with international scholars

How to apply

  1. Apply through EPFL's official website
  2. Submit your application before December 15, 2025 (or April 30, 2026) for courses starting in September 2026
  3. Follow application instructions and ensure all required documents are included

FAQs

What are the application deadlines?

Applications for EPFL Excellence Fellowships must be submitted before December 15, 2025 (or April 30, 2026) for courses starting in September 2026.

What fields of study are eligible?

The fellowships are available for eligible MS programs at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Switzerland. Please check the official EPFL website for the specific list of eligible fields of study.

What is the funding provided?

EPFL Excellence Fellowships offer competitive funding for tuition fees and living expenses. Scholars will also benefit from top-tier research facilities and expertise.
