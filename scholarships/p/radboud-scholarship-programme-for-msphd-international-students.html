Radboud Scholarship Programme for MS/PhD International Students

The Radboud Scholarship Programme offers exceptional MS/PhD international students the chance to pursue their academic dreams at Radboud University. With a generous stipend and comprehensive support, this programme aims to attract the brightest minds from Pakistan and India for a global career.

Eligibility

  • Be enrolled as a full-time student in a Pakistani or Indian university.
  • Hold a valid passport from Pakistan or India.
  • Have a research-oriented master's or PhD programme in mind at Radboud University.

Benefits

  • Competitive monthly stipend
  • Tuition fee waiver
  • Comprehensive support for academic and personal success
  • Opportunities for international research experience
  • Networking with renowned scholars and institutions

How to apply

  1. Step 1: Research and select a suitable MS/PhD programme at Radboud University.
  2. Step 2: Contact your Pakistani or Indian university's international office for guidance on application process.
  3. Step 3: Apply through the Radboud Scholarship Programme application portal and submit your application alongside required documents.

FAQs

What are the application deadlines for the Radboud Scholarship Programme?

Please check the official Radboud Scholarship Programme website for the latest application deadlines. Applications typically open in January and close in February, with an expected decision in March.

Are Pakistani or Indian citizens eligible for the Radboud Scholarship Programme?

Yes, Pakistani and Indian citizens are eligible to apply for the Radboud Scholarship Programme. However, they must be enrolled in a Pakistani or Indian university and meet the programme's eligibility criteria.
