UNICAF Scholarships for Online MS/PhD Degrees at UK Universities

The UNICAF Scholarship programme offers a unique opportunity for students in Pakistan and India to pursue online Master's and PhD degrees from reputable UK universities. It supports learners facing financial constraints while allowing them to study flexibly alongside work or personal commitments. Graduates receive a globally recognised UK degree that can accelerate career growth.

Eligibility

  • Hold a recognised bachelor's degree or equivalent.
  • Demonstrate English proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL or equivalent).
  • Show academic merit or relevant professional experience.

Benefits

  • Partial or full tuition fee coverage for the chosen online programme.
  • Award of a UK university degree that is internationally respected.

How to apply

  1. Visit the UNICAF website, browse available UK online MS/PhD programmes and select your preferred course.
  2. Complete the online application form and upload required documents (transcripts, English test scores, CV).
  3. Submit the application and wait for the scholarship decision via email.

FAQs

What types of programmes are covered by UNICAF scholarships?

UNICAF funds online Master's (MS) and Doctoral (PhD) programmes offered by partner UK universities.

Can students from Pakistan and India apply?

Yes, the scholarship is open to eligible applicants from Pakistan, India and other countries worldwide.
