Top 10 Switzerland Scholarships for MS & PhD Students

Switzerland offers world‑class research facilities and a vibrant academic environment. This guide lists the top 10 fully funded scholarships for master's and doctoral programs, open to international students from Pakistan, India and beyond. Learn about eligibility, benefits, and the simple steps to apply for these prestigious awards.

Eligibility

  • Citizenship of Pakistan, India or any other country with a valid passport
  • Accepted admission offer for an MS or PhD program at a Swiss university
  • Minimum academic standing (typically a 3.0 GPA or equivalent) and relevant research experience

Benefits

  • Full tuition waiver, living allowance and health insurance coverage
  • Access to cutting‑edge labs, research networks and Swiss cultural immersion

How to apply

  1. Gather required documents: academic transcripts, CV, research proposal, recommendation letters, and language certificates.
  2. Create an online profile on the scholarship portal, fill in personal details, and upload all documents before the deadline.
  3. Submit the application and monitor your email for any interview invitations or additional information requests.

FAQs

Can I apply for more than one Swiss scholarship at the same time?

Yes, you may apply to multiple scholarships as long as each application meets the specific eligibility criteria and you can manage the required documentation.

Do I need to know German or French to be eligible?

Language proficiency is not a mandatory eligibility factor for most scholarships, but many programs require proof of English proficiency (e.g., IELTS or TOEFL). Knowing the local language can help with daily life.
